# External Resources for Metadata Enrichment

This guide lists external catalogues, repositories and authority databases that can be used to enrich the metadata.

## Facsimile Repositories and Catalogues

- [Google Books](https://books.google.com/) — Digitized books from libraries and publishers worldwide. Full-text availability varies by edition, date and location.
- [BnF Catalogue](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/rechercher.do) and [Gallica](https://gallica.bnf.fr/) (France) — Search the Bibliothèque nationale de France catalogue for bibliographic records and Gallica for digitized materials.
- [MDZ](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en) (Germany) — The Munich Digitization Center provides digitized holdings from the Bavarian State Library and partner institutions.
- [KB Catalogue](https://webggc.oclc.org/cbs/DB=2.37/SET=2/TTL=1/) (Netherlands) — Catalogue of the National Library of the Netherlands. Records may link to digitized copies.
- [UCM Library](https://biblioteca.ucm.es/) (Madrid) — The Complutense University of Madrid library catalogue and digital collections.
- [HathiTrust Digital Library](https://www.hathitrust.org/) — Digitized books and journals contributed by research libraries. Access to full text depends on copyright status and location.
- [ProQuest](https://www.proquest.com/) — Subscription databases containing digitized historical books, newspapers and other primary sources. Institutional access may be required.
- [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/) — Freely accessible digitized books and other media from libraries and individual contributors.

## Authority Databases and Catalogues

Authority databases assign stable identifiers to people and bring together variant spellings, pseudonyms, dates and links to catalogue records. Useful resources include:

- [VIAF — Virtual International Authority File](https://viaf.org/en/viaf/59312738) — Aggregates authority records from national libraries and other institutions. The linked record is an example of a VIAF person record.
- [ISNI — International Standard Name Identifier](https://isni.org/isni/0000000079761590) — Provides persistent identifiers for public identities, including authors, researchers and other contributors. The linked record is an example.
- [BnF Authority Records](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/recherche-autorite.do?pageRech=rat) — The Bibliothèque nationale de France database of authorized names, variant forms and related bibliographic records.
- [IdRef](https://www.idref.fr/084719125) — The authority database for French higher education and research. The linked page is an example of an IdRef record.
- Library authority catalogues, such as the [Bibliotheksservice-Zentrum Baden-Württemberg catalogue](https://swb.bsz-bw.de/DB=2.104/PPNSET?PPN=079657354&INDEXSET=21) — Regional and institutional catalogues can provide additional name forms, dates, affiliations and bibliographic links.
