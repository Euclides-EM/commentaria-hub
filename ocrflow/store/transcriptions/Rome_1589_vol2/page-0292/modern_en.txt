MOREOVER, because in this book it was necessary for us to depart
from the numbering which Theon follows in the propositions of Euclid,
as we shall say at proposition 13, just as in all things we have neglected
the series of Campanus, as we explained at the beginning of the first
book; we have taken care that a double number be placed in the margin
opposite the propositions, of which the upper shows the order of Theon,
the lower however shows the series of Campanus. But if only one
number is found in the margin, understand that there is then no
disagreement between Theon and Campanus, as far as the numbering
of the propositions is concerned.
