AXIOMS OR
Pronouncements.

I.

A MAGNITUDE measuring however many
magnitudes, measures also the magnitude
composed from them.

II.

A MAGNITUDE measuring any magnitude,
measures also every magnitude which that
magnitude measures.

III.

A MAGNITUDE measuring the whole
magnitude, and the part taken away, measures
also the remainder.
