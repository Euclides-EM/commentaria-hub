ELEMENT
ELEVENTH.

DEFINITIONS.

1. A solid is that which has length,
breadth & depth: But
the boundaries of a solid are
surfaces.
2. A straight line is raised perpen-
dicularly upon a plane, when all
the lines drawn on that plane toward it
meet it at right angles.
3. A plane is raised perpendicularly
upon a plane, when the lines drawn on the one
of them perpendicularly to the line of
common section, are also perpendicular to
the other plane.
4. The inclination of a straight line to a plane
is the acute angle contained by that line, & by
another straight line drawn in the plane of the inclina-
tion, through the point at which a perpendicular
drawn from the end of that inclined line falls upon
the said plane.
