7. Parallel planes are those which, being
continued, do not meet.
8. Similar solids are those which are
contained by similar surfaces, equal in
number.
9. Equal and similar solids are those
which are contained by similar surfaces, e-
qual in number and magnitude.
10. A solid angle is the meeting of more than
two plane angles in one same point, those
being constituted on different planes.
11. A pyramid is a solid contained by sev-
eral planes, meeting in one same point,
and having another plane for base.
12. A prism is a solid contained by planes,
of which two that are opposite are equal,
similar, and parallel, and the others are paral-
