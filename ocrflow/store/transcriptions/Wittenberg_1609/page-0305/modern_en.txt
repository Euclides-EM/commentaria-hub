the rest for the most part being unknown.

6.  And those commensurable with it either
in length and in power, or in power
only, [are] Rational. All lines which
in whatever way are commensurable with the given
are called Rational, not indeed by
position, as that [one], but because in that
comparison with the given they are found commensurable.
And therefore also the lines whose squares
20 and 1000 are commensurable to any square, as 16
or 100, rightly we also
