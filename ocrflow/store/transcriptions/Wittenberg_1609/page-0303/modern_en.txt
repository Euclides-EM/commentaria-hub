Commensurable magnitudes are

measure measures. Thus also the
lines of 21 and 13 palms are commen-
surable, because one palm and
a half and 1/3 and 1/4 measure them. The same
is understood also of surfaces, which the same
surface measures, and of bodies, which the same
body measures.
