AXIOMS.

1. A magnitude which measures any
magnitude, also measures the magnitude
composed of them.

2. A magnitude which measures any
magnitude, also measures every magnitude
which that magnitude measures.

3. A magnitude which measures a whole
magnitude and a part taken away, also
measures the remainder.
