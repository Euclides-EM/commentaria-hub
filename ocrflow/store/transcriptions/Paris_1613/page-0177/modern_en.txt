Commensurable magnitudes are those
measured by one and the same measure.
Incommensurable magnitudes are those
which have no common measure.

Straight lines commensurable in
power, are those of which the
squares can be measured by one and the same sur-
face.

4. Incommensurable lines are those of which the
squares have no common surface that can
measure them.

This being so, it is evident that for every line proposed, one will find infinitely many
commensurable lines, and infinitely many incommensurable: some in length
and power, the others in power only.
