The definitions.

Magnitudes, measured by one and the same measure,
are called Commensurable.
But when one cannot find for them a common
measure, they are called Incommensurable.
When the squares of certain lines are measured
by one and the same measure, the lines are called
Commensurable in power.
But when, for their squares, one cannot find
a common measure, they are called Incommensurable.

and incommensurable, some in length
