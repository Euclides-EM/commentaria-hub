First Theorem.

With two unequal magnitudes proposed,
if from the greater there be taken away more than the half, and again
from the remainder again there be taken away more than the half, and this
be always done: there will be left some magnitude
less than the lesser of the two proposed.

Let there be two unequal magnitudes α β, of which
the greater is α β: I say if from α β there be taken away
more than the half, and from the remainder again more than the
half, and this be always done, there will be left some
magnitude less than ζ, magnitude γ. For γ
multiplied will at length sometime be greater than the
magnitude α β. let it be multiplied and let it be λ. a
multiple indeed of γ, but greater than α β.
