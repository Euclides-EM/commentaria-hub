In this place I say nothing else, than that you should add understanding
of this place by the addition of the word, power, which is added to the foregoing
definition, so that you may understand it of those lines which are
incommensurable in power; which since they are incommensurable in power,
have also this, that they are moreover incommensurable in
length. Let this have been said thus far. But
if you should desire to know more in this place: you would overturn the order
of the discipline, which is the surest teacher for learning.

Since these things are so, it can be shown, that of whatever
straight line is proposed to us: there also exist other
innumerable lines commensurable with it: and others
likewise incommensurable; these indeed in length
and in power; but those in power only.

I would have you know that this is the chief point of this book, which cannot, as
of the other preceding ones, be grasped in the first reading.
