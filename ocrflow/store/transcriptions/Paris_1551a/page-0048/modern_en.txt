In this place I say nothing else, than that you may add understanding:

definition, so that you may understand concerning those lines which are in-

Since these things are so, it can be shown, that whatever
straight line is proposed to us: there exist also other
innumerable lines commensurable with it: and other
incommensurable. these indeed in length:
and in power: but those in power only.
