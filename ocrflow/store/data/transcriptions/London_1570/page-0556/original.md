Here is to be noted, which thing alſo we before noted in the definitions, that Campane and others which followed him, brought in theſe phraſes of ſpeaches, to call ſome lynes rationall in power onely, and other ſome rationall in length and in power, which we cannot finde that Euclide euer vſed. For theſe wordes in length and in power are neuer referred to rationalitie or irrationalitie, but alwayes to the commenſurabilitie or incommenſurabilitie of lines. Which peruerting of wordes (as was there declared) hath much increaſed the difficulty and obſcurenes of this booke. And now I thinke it good agayne to put you in minde, that in theſe propoſitions which follow, we muſt euer haue before our eyes the rationall line firſt ſet, vnto which other lines compared are either rationall or irrationall, according to their commenſurability or incommenſurabilitie.

## ¶ The 16. Theoreme. The 19. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
A rectangle figure comprehended vnder right lines commenſurable in lengthe, being rationall according to one of the foreſaide wayes : is rationall.
[/Subhead]

[Margin]
Conſtruction.
[/Margin]

Then I ſay that the ſuperficies A C is rationall, deſcribe (by the 46. of the firſt) vpon the line A B a ſquare A D.

Demonſtration.

Wherefore that ſquare A D is rationall by the definition. And foraſmuch as the line A B is commenſurable in length vnto the line B C, and the line A B is equall vnto the lyne B D, therefore the lyne B D is commenſurable in length vnto the line B C. And as the line B D is to the line B C, ſo is the ſquare D A to the ſuperficies A C (by the firſt of the ſixt): but it is proued that the line B D is commenſurable vnto the line B C, wherefore (by the 10. of the tenth) the ſquare D A is commenſurable vnto the rectangle ſuperficies A C. But the ſquare D A is rationall, wherefore the rectangle ſuperficies A C alſo is rationall by the definition. A rectangle figure therefore comprehended vnder right lines commenſurable in length, beyng rationall accordyng to one of the foreſayd wayes is rationall: which was required to be proued.
