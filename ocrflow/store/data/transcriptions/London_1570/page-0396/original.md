## ☞ The 11. Theoreme. The 16. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
If there be foure right lines in proportion, the rectangle figure comprehended vnder the extremes, is equall to the rectangle figure contayned vnder the meanes. And if the rectangle figure which is contained vnder the extremes, be equall vnto the rectangle figure which is contayned vnder the meanes: then are thoſe foure lines in proportion.
[/Subhead]

[Margin]
Demonſtration of the firſt part.
[/Margin]

{dropcap:S|lines=3|style=decorated}Vppoſe that there be foure right lines in proportiō, namely, A B, C D, E, and F: ſo that as the line A B is to the line C D, ſo let the line E be to the line F. Then I ſay, that the rectangle figure comprehended vnder the extremes A B and F, is equall vnto the rectangle figure contayned vnder the meanes C D and E. From the poynt A (by the 11. of the firſt) raiſe vp vnto the right line A B a perpendicular line A G. And (by the ſame) from the point C vnto the right line C D raiſe vp a perpendicular line C H. And (by the 2. of the firſt) put the line A G equall vnto the line F, and put alſo ȳ line C H equall vnto the line E, and make complete the parallelogrammes G B and H D. Now for that (by ſuppoſition) as the line A B is to the line C D, ſo is the line E to the line F. But the line E is equall vnto the line C H, & the line F vnto the line A G, therefore as the line A B is to the line C D, ſo is the line C H to the line A G. Wherefore in the parallelogrãmes B G and D H the ſides which include ȳ equall angles, are reciprokally proportionall. But equiangle parallelogrammes whoſe ſides which include the equall angles, are reciprokall, are alſo equall (by the 14. of the ſixt). Wherefore the parallelogramme B G is equall vnto the parallelogramme D H. But the parallelogramme B G is that which is contayned vnder the lines A B and F, for the line A G is put equall vnto the line F. And the parallelogramme D H is that which is contained vnder the lines C D and E, for the line C H is put equall vnto the line E. Wherefore the rectangle figure contained vnder the lines A B and F, is equall vnto the rectangle figure contained vnder the lines C D and E.

[Diagram: two rectangles and four vertical lines labelled A, B, C, D, E, F, G and H]

[Margin]
The ſecond part which is the conuerſe of the firſt.
[/Margin]

But now ſuppoſe that the rectangle figure comprehended vnder the lines A B and F, be equall vnto the rectangle figure comprehended vnder ȳ lines C D & E.
