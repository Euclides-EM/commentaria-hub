Inſtances in this Probleme.
[/Margin]

In this propoſition the aduerſary paraduenture will cauill, that the circles ſhall not cut the one the other (which thing Euclide putteth them to do) But now if they cutte not the one the other, either they touch the one the other, or they are diſtaunte the one from the other. Firſt if it be poſſible let them tooche the one the other, as in the figure here put (the conſtruction whereof anſwereth to the conſtruction of Euclide).
