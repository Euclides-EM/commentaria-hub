Wherefore euery one of theſe triangles E K F, F L G, G M H, and H N E, is greater then the halfe of the ſegment of the circle which is deſcribed about it. Now then deuiding the circumferences remaining into two equall partes, and drawing right lines from the pointes where thoſe diuiſions are made, & ſo continually doing this, we ſhall at the length (by the 1. of the tenth) leaue certaine ſegmentes of the circle, which ſhall be leſſe then the exceſſe, whereby the circle E F G H excedeth the ſuperficies S. For it hath bene proued in the firſt Propoſition of the tenth booke, that two vnequall magnitudes being geuen, if from the greater be taken away more then the halfe, and likewiſe againe from the reſidue more then the halfe, and ſo continually, there ſhall at the length be left a certaine magnitude which ſhall be leſſe then the leſſe magnitude geuen. Let there be ſuch ſegmentes left, & let the ſegmentes of the circle E F G H, namely, which are made by the lines E K, K F, F L, L G, G M, M H, H N, and N E, be leſſe then the exceſſe, whereby the circle E F G H excedeth the ſuperficies S. Wherefore the reſidue, namely, the Poligonon figure E K F L G M H N, is greater then the ſuperficies S. Inſcribe in the circle A B C D a Poligonon figure like to the Poligonon figure E K F L G M H N, and let the ſame be A X B O C P D R. Wherefore (by the Propoſition next going before) as the ſquare of the line B D is to the ſquare of the line F H, ſo is the Poligonon figure A X B O C P D R to the Poligonon figure E K F L G M H N. But as the ſquare of the line B D is to the ſquare of the line F G, ſo is the circle A B C D ſuppoſed to be to the ſuperficies S. Wherefore (by the 11. of the fifth) as the circle A B C D is to the ſuperficies S, ſo is the Poligonon figure A X B O C P D R to the Poligonon figure E K F L G M H N. Wherefore alternately (by the 16. of the fifth) as the circle A B C D is to the Poligonon figure deſcribed in it, ſo is the ſuperficies S to the Poligonon figure E K F L G M H N. But the circle A B C D is greater then the Poligonon figure deſcribed in it. Wherefore alſo the ſuperficies S is greater then the Poligonon figure E K F L G H M N: but it is alſo leſſe: which is impoſſible.
