## The 3. Theoreme. The 3. Proposition.

*If a right line be deuided by chaunce: the rectangle figure cõprehended vnder the whole and one of the partes, is equall to the rectangle figure comprehended vnder the partes, & vnto the ſquare which is made of the foreſaid part.*

*{dropcap:S|lines=4|style=decorated|decoration="floral"}Vppoſe that the right line geuen AB be deuided by chaunce in the point C. Then I ſay that the rectangle figure comprehended vnder the lines AB and BC is equall vnto the rectangle figure comprehended vnder the lines AC and CB, and alſo vnto the ſquare which is made of the line BC.*
