### ¶ A Lemma or Aſſumpt.

And foraſmuch as by ſuppoſition as AB is to CD, ſo is AE to CF: and alternately, as AB is to AE, ſo is CD to CF. And now it is proued, that as AB is to CD, ſo is EB to FD. Wherefore againe alternately, as AB is to EB, ſo is CD to FD: Wherefore it followeth, that as AB is to AE, ſo is CD to CF: and againe, as the ſame AB is to EB, ſo is the ſame CD to DF.

[Margin]
A Lemma.
[/Margin]

### ☞ Corollary.

And hereby it is manifeſt, that if magnitudes compoſed be proportionall, then alſo by conuerſion of Proportion (which of ſome is called Proportion by Euerſion, and which is, as before it was defined, whẽ the antecedent is compared to the exceſſe, wherein the antecedent exceedeth the conſequent) they ſhall be proportionall.

A Corollary.
