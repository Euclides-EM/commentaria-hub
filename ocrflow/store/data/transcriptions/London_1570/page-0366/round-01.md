Suppoſe that the proportion of A to B be greater then the proportion of C to D. Then I ſay contrariwiſe by conuerſion, the proportion of B to A is leſſe then the proportion of D to C. For if the proportion of B to A be one and the ſame with the proportion of D to C, then conuerſedly the proportion of A to B is one and the ſame with the proportion of C to D, which is contrary to ſuppoſition. But if the proportion of B to A be greater then the proportion of D to C: Then let E be vnto A, as D is to C.

[Diagram: five horizontal line segments labelled A, B, C, D, and E]

And by the 13. of this booke, the proportion of E to A ſhall be leſſe then the proportion of B to A. Wherfore (by the firſt part of the tenth of the ſame) E is leſſe then B. And therefore by the ſecõd part of the 8. of the ſame, A hath vnto E a greater proportion then A hath to B. And foraſmuch as by conuerſe proportionalitie A is vnto E as C is to D, therfore by the 13. of the ſame, C hath to D a greater proportion then hath A to B, which is contrary to the ſuppoſition. For A was ſuppoſed to haue vnto B a greater proportion then hath C to D. Wherefore the proportion of B to A is neither one and the ſame with the proportion of C to D, nor greater then it. Wherfore it is leſſe: which was required to be proued.

It may alſo be demonſtrated directly. For let E be vnto B as C is to D. Then conuerſedly B is to E as D is to C. And foraſmuch as A is greater then E by the firſt part of the tenth of this booke, therfore by the ſecond part of the 8. of the ſame B hath vnto A a leſſe proportion then hath B to E. Wherfore by the 13. of the ſame B hath vnto A a leſſe proportion then hath D to C: which was required to be proued.

## ¶ The ſecond Propoſition.

If there be foure quantities, and if the proportion of the firſt to the ſecond be greater then the proportion of the third to the fourth, then alternately the proportion of the firſt to the third, ſhall be greater then the proportion of the ſecond to the fourth.

[Margin]
Demonſtratiõ leading to an impoſſibilitie.
[/Margin]

Let A haue vnto B a greater proportion then hath C to D. Then I ſay alternately A hath to C a greater proportion then hath B to D. For one and the ſame proportion it can not haue: for then alternately A ſhould be to B as C is to D, which is contrary to the ſuppoſitiõ. But if it haue a leſſe proportiõ, let E be vnto C as B is to D. Now thẽ by the 13. of this booke E hath vnto C a greater proportion then hath A to C. Wherefore (by the firſt part of the tenth of the ſame) E is greater thẽ A. Wherfore by the firſt part of the 8. of the ſame, E hath to B a greater proportion then hath A to B.
