But as the ſquares of the lines C B and C D are to the ſquares of the lines C B & B D, † ſo (any right line what ſo euer it be, being diuided by an extreme and meane proportion) is the line containing in power the ſquares of the whole line, and of the greater ſegmēt, to the line containing in power the ſquares of the whole line, and of the leſſe ſegment: wherfore (by the 11.of the fifth) as the ſquare of the line G (the ſide of the cube) is to the ſquare of the line E, ſo (any right line being deuided by an extreme and meane proportion) is the line containing in power the ſquares made of the whole line, and of the greater ſegmēt, to the line containing in power the ſquares made of the whole line, and of the leſſe ſegment: but the line G is the ſide of the Cube, and the line E of the Icoſahedron (by ſuppoſition.) If therfore a right line be deuided by an extreeme and meane proportion, as the line cōtaining in power the ſquares of the whole line, and of the greater ſegment, is to the line containing in power the ſquares of the whole line and of the leſſe ſegment: ſo is the ſide of the cube to the ſide of the Icoſahedron, being both deſcribed in one and the ſelfe ſame ſphere.
