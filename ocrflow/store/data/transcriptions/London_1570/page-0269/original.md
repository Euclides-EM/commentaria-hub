## The 4. Probleme. The 30. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
To deuide a circumference geuen into two equall partes.
[/Subhead]

[Margin]
Conſtruction.
[/Margin]

{dropcap:S|lines=3|style=decorated}uppoſe that the circumference geuen be ADB. It is required to deuide the circumference ADB into two equall partes. Draw a right line from A to B. And (by the 10. of the firſt) deuide the line AB into two equall partes in the point C. And (by the 11. of the firſt) from the point C rayſe vp vnto AB a perpendicular line CD. And draw theſe right lines AD and DB.

[Diagram: semicircle ADB with diameter AB, midpoint C, perpendicular CD, and lines AD and DB]

[Margin]
Demonſtration.
[/Margin]

And for aſmuch as the line AC is equall to the line CB, & the line CD is common to them both, therefore theſe two lines AC and CD are equall to theſe two lines BC and CD. And (by the 4. peticion) the angle ACD is equall to the angle BCD, for either of them is a right angle. Wherefore (by the 4. of the firſt) the baſe AD is equall to the baſe DB. But equall right lines do cut away equall circumferences, the greater equall to the greater, & the leſſe equall to the leſſe (by the 28. of the third.) And either of theſe circumferences AD and DB is leſſe then a ſemicircle. Wherefore the circumference AD is equall to the circumference DB. Wherefore the circumference geuen is deuided into two equall partes: Which was required to be done.

[Ornament]

## The 27. Theoreme. The 31. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
In a circle an angle made in the ſemicircle is a right angle.
[/Subhead]
