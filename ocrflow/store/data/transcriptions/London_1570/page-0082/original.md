[Margin]
Caſe.
[/Margin]

Further in diuers propoſitions, there happen diuers caſes: which are nothing els, but varietie of delineation and conſtruction, or chaunge of poſition, as when pointes, lines, ſuperficies, or bodies are chaunged. VVhich thinges happen in diuers propoſitions.

The thing geuen in this Probleme. The thing required. The propoſition. The expoſition. The determination. The conſtruction.
[/Margin]

{dropcap:N|lines=2|style=plain}Ow then in this Probleme, the thing geuen, is the line geuẽ: the thing required, to be ſerched out is, how vpõ that line to deſcribe an equilater triangle. The Propoſition of this Probleme is, Vpon a right line geuen not beyng infinite, to deſcribe an equilater triangle.

The expoſition is, Suppoſe that the right line geuen be A B, and this declareth onely the thing geuen. The determination is, It is required vpon the line A B, to deſcribe an equilater triangle: for thereby as you ſee, is declared onely the thing required. The conſtruction beginneth at theſe wordes, Now therfore making the centre the point A, & the ſpace A B, deſcribe (by the third peticion) a circle &c, and continueth vntil you come to theſe wordes, And foraſmuch as the point A &c. For thetherto are deſcribed circles and lines, neceſſarye both for the doyng of the propoſition, and alſo for the demonſtration therof.

[Margin]
The demonſtration.
[/Margin]

VVhich demonſtration beginneth at theſe wordes: And foraſmuche as the point A is the centre of the circle C B D &c: And ſo procedeth till you come to theſe wordes, VVherfore vpon the line A B is deſcribed an equilater triangle A B C. For vntill you come thether, is, by groundes before ſet and conſtructions had, proued, and made euident, that the triangle made, is equilater.

[Margin]
The perticuler concluſion.
[/Margin]

And then in theſe wordes, wherfore vpon the line A B, is deſcribed an equilater triangle A B C, is put the firſt concluſion. For there are commonly in euery propoſition two concluſions: the one perticuler, the other vniuersal: and from the firſt you go to the laſt. and this is the firſt and perticuler concluſion, for that it concludeth, that vpon the lyne A B is deſcribed an equilater triangle, which is according to the expoſition.

[Margin]
The Vniuersall concluſion.
[/Margin]

After it, followeth the laſt and vniuersal concluſion, wherfore vpon a right line geuen not being infinite is deſcribed an equilater triangle. For whether the line geuen be greater or leſſe then thys lyne, the ſame conſtructiõs and demonſtrations proue the ſame concluſion.

[Margin]
The note where by it is knowne to be a Probleme.
[/Margin]

Laſt of all is added this clauſe, Which is the thing which was required to be done: wherby as we haue before noted, is declared, that this propoſition is a Probleme and not a Theoreme.

[Margin]
No caſes in thys propoſition.
[/Margin]

As for varietie of caſes in this propoſition there is none, for that the line geuen, can haue no diuerſitie of poſition.

As you haue in this Probleme ſene plainelye ſet foorthe the thing geuen, and the thing required, moreouer the propoſition, expoſition, determination, conſtruction, demonſtration, and concluſion (which are generall alſo to many other both Problemes and Theoremes) ſo may you by the example therof diſtinct them, and ſearche them out in other Problemes, and alſo Theoremes.
