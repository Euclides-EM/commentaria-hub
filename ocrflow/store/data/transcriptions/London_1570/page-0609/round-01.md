## ❧ An Aſſumpt.

If a right line be deuided into two vnequall partes, the ſquares which are made of the vnequall partes, are greater then the rectangle parallelogramme cõtayned vnder the vnequall partes, twiſe.

Suppoſe that A B be a right line, and let it be deuided into two vnequall partes in the point C. And let the line A C be the greater part. Then I ſay, that the ſquares of the lines A C and C B, are greater thẽ that which is contained vnder the lines A C and C B, twiſe. Deuide (by the 10. of the firſt) the line A B into two equall partes, in the point D.
