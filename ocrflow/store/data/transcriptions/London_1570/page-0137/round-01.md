## The 17. Theoreme. The 26. Propoſition.

If two triangles haue two angles of the one equall to two angles of the other, ech to his correſpondent angle, and haue alſo one ſide of the one equall to one ſide of the other, either that ſide which lieth betwene the equall angles, or that which is ſubtended vnder one of the equall angles: the other ſides alſo of the one, ſhalbe equall to the other ſides of the other, eche to his correſpondent ſide, and the other angle of the one ſhalbe equall to the other angle of the other.

For if the ſide A B be not equall to the ſide D E, the one of them is greater.
