Now it is required to comprehende the ſame Pyramis in the ſphere geuẽ, and to proue that the diameter of the ſphere is in power ſeſquialtera to the ſide of the pyramis. Adde vnto the right line H K a right line directly, namely H L, and let the line H K be equall to the the line B C.* Now for that as the line A C is to the line C D, ſo is the line C D to the line C B (by the corollary of the 8. of the ſixth) but the line A C is equal to the line K H, & the line C D to the line H E, & the line C B to the line H L. Therfore as the line K H is to the line H E, ſo is the line H E to the line H L. Wherefore that which is contayned vnder the lines H K and H L is equall to the ſquare of the line E H. And ether of the angles K H E, & E H L is a right angle, wherefore a ſemicircle deſcribed vpon the line K L ſhall paſſe by the poynt E.* For if we draw a right line from the point E to the poynt L, the angle L E K ſhalbe a right angle, for that the triãgle E L K is equiangle to either of the triangles E L H and E H K (by the 8. of the ſixth.) Now then if the the diameter K L abiding fixed, the ſemicircle be turned round about, vntil it returne vnto the ſelfe ſame place from whenſe it began to be moued, it ſhall alſo paſſe by the pointes F and G. For drawing a right line from the ponit F to the point L, and an other from the poynt L to the point G, which alſo maketh at the points F and G right angles, the pyramis ſhall be contayned in the ſphere geuen. For the line K L being the diameter of the ſphere is equall to the line A B, which is the diameter of the ſphere geuen, for the line K H is put equall to the line A C, and the line H L to the line C B.
