### Corrolary.

Hereby it is manifeſt, that if a number meaſure two numbers it ſhall alſo meaſure their greateſt common meaſure. For if it meaſure the whole & the part taken away, it ſhall alwayes meaſure the reſidue alſo, which reſidue is at the length, the greateſt common meaſure of the two numbers geuen.
