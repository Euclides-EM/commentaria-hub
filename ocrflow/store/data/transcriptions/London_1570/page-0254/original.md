## ¶ The 2. Probleme. The 17. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
From a poynt geuen, to draw a right line which ſhall touch a circle geuen.
[/Subhead]

[Margin]
Conſtruction.
[/Margin]

{dropcap:S|lines=3|style=decorated|decoration="foliate"}Vppose that the poynt geuen be A, and let the circle geuen be BCD. It is required from the poynt A to draw a right line which ſhall touch the circle BCD. Take (by the firſt of the third) the centre of the circle, and let the ſame be E. And (by the firſt petition) draw the right line ADE. And making the centre E, and the ſpace AE, deſcribe (by yͤ third petition) a circle AFG. And from the poynt D raiſe vp (by the 11. of the firſt) vnto the line EA a perpendicular line DF. And (by the firſt petition) drawe theſe lines EBF and AB. Then I ſay, that from the point A is drawen to the circle BCD a touch line AB.

[Margin]
Demonſtration.
[/Margin]

For for aſmuch as the point E is the centre of the circle BCD, and alſo of the circle AFG, therfore the line EA is equall to the line EF, and the line ED to yͤ line EB, for they are drawen from the centre to the circumference. Wherefore to theſe two lines AE and EB, are equall theſe two lines EF & ED, and the angle at the poynt E is common to them both: Wherefore (by the 4. of the firſt) the baſe DF is equall to the baſe AB, and yͤ triangle DEF is equall to the triangle EBA, and the reſt of the angles remayning to the reſt of the angles remayning. Wherefore the angle EDF is equall to yͤ angle EBA. But the angle EDF is a right angle: Wherefore alſo the angle EBA is a right angle, and the line EB is drawen from the centre. But a perpendicular line drawen from the end of the diameter of a circle, toucheth the circle (by yͤ Corellary of the 16. of the third). Wherefore the line AB toucheth the circle BCD. Wherefore from the point geuen, namely, A, is drawen vnto yͤ circle geuẽ BCD, a touch line AB: which was required to be done.

[Margin]
An addition of Pelitarius.

Vnto a right lyne which cutteth a circle, to drawe a parallel line which ſhall touch the circle.
