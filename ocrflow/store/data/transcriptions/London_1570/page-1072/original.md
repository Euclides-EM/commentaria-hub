## ¶ The 32. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
*The proportion of the ſolide of an Icoſahedron to the ſolide of a Dodecahedron inſcribed in it, conſiſteth of the proportion of the ſide of the Icoſahedron to the ſide of the Cube contayned in the ſame ſphere, and of the proportion tripled of the diameter to the line which coupleth the centers of the oppoſite baſes of the Icoſahedron.*
[/Subhead]

[Margin]
Conſtruction.
[/Margin]

{dropcap:S|lines=5|style=decorated|decoration="interlaced pattern"}Vppoſe that there be a Dodecahedron whoſe diameter let be HI, and let the Icoſahedron contained in the ſame ſphere be ABGC, whoſe dimentient let be AC. And let the right line which coupleth the centres of the oppoſite baſes be BG. And let the dodecahedron inſcribed in the Icoſahedron be that which is ſet vpon the diameter BG, by the 5. of the ſixtenth. And let the ſide of the cube be DE, and let the ſide of the Icoſahedron be DF, both the ſayd ſolides being deſcribed in one and the ſelfe ſame ſphere.

[Margin]
Demonſtration.
[/Margin]

Then I ſay that the proportion of the ſolide of the Icoſahedron ABGC to the ſolide of the dodecahedron ſet vpon the diameter BG, inſcribed in it, conſiſteth of the proportion of the line DF to the line DE, and of the proportion tripled of the line AC to the line BG. For foraſmuch as the ſolide of the Icoſahedron ABGC is to the ſolide of the dodecahedron HI, being contayned in one and the ſelfe ſame ſphere, as DF is to DE, by the 8. of the fourtenth.

But the dodecahedron whoſe diameter is HI, is to the dodecahedron whoſe diameter is BG, in treble proportiõ of that in which the diameter HI is to the diameter BG, by the corollary of the 17. of the twelfth: & the lines HI and AC are equall by ſuppoſition (namely, the diameters of one and the ſelfe ſame ſphere). Wherefore as HI is to BG, ſo is AC to BG. Wherefore the proportion of the extremes, namely, of the Icoſahedron ABGC to the Dodecahedron ſet vppon the diameter BG which coupleth the centres, cõſiſteth (by the 5. diffinition of the ſixt) of the proportions of the meanes, namely, of the proportiõ of the Icoſahedron ABGC to the dodecahedron HI (which is one and the ſame with the proportiõ of DF to DE) and of the proportiõ of the ſame HI to the other dodecahedron ſet vpon the diameter BG, inſcribed in the ſame Icoſahedron ABGC, by the ſame 5. of the ſixtenth: which proportiõ is triple to the proportiõ of the line HI (or the line AC) to GB which coupleth the centres of the oppoſite baſes of the Icoſahedron. The proportion therefore of the ſolide of the Icoſahedron to the ſolide of a Dodecahedron inſcribed in it, conſiſteth of the proportion of the ſide of the Icoſahedron to the ſide of the Cube contayned in the ſame ſphere, and of the proportion tripled of the diameter to the line which coupleth the centres of the oppoſite baſes of the Icoſahedron.
