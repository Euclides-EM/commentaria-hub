## The fourth Propoſition.

[Subhead]
If a number geuen be deuided into two numbers: the ſquare number of the whole, is equall to the ſquare numbers of the partes, and to the ſuperficiall number which is produced of the multiplication of the partes the one into the other twiſe.
[/Subhead]

Suppoſe that the number geuen be AB: which let be deuided into two numbers AC and CB. Then I ſay that the ſquare number of the whole number AB, is equall to the ſquares of the partes, that is, to the ſquares of the numbers AC and CB, and to the ſuperficiall number produced of the multiplication of the numbers AC and CB the one into the other twiſe.
