{dropcap:S|lines=5|style=decorated|decoration="floral design"}uppoſe that the pyramis geuen, be ABGD: euery one of whoſe ſides, let be diuided into two equall partes in the poyntes E, M, K, L, P, N. And in euery one of the baſes of that pyramis, deſcribe the triangles EFP, PMN, NKL, and EMK: which triangles ſhall be equilater by the 4. of the firſt, for the ſides ſubtend equall angles of the pyramis, contayned vnder the halues of the ſides of the ſame pyramis: wherfore the ſides of the ſaid triangles are equall. Let thoſe ſides be diuided by an extreme and meane proportion (by the 30. of the ſixth) in the poyntes C, E, Q, R, S, T, H, I, O, V, Y, X. Now then thoſe ſides are cutte into the ſelfe ſame proportions, by the 2. of the fourtenth: and therefore they make the like ſections equall, by the 2. part of the ninth of the fiueth. Now I ſay, that the foreſayd poyntes doo receaue the angles of the Icoſahedron inſcribed in the pyramis ABGD. In the foreſayd triangles let there agayne be made other triangles by coupling the ſections, and let thoſe triangles be TRS, IOH, CEQ, and VXY, which ſhall be equilater: for euery one of their ſides doo ſubtend equall angles of equilater triangles, and thoſe ſayd equall angles are contayned vnder equall ſides (namely, vnder the greater ſegment and the leſſe): and therefore the ſides which ſubtend thoſe angles are equall by the 4. of the firſt.

Now let vs proue that at eche of the foreſayd poynts, as for example at T, is ſet the ſolide angle of an Icoſahedron. Forasmuch as the triangles TRS and TOQ are equilater and equall, the 4. right lines TR, TS, TQ, and TO ſhall be equall. And forasmuch as FPNK is a ſquare cutting the pyramis ABGD into two equall partes, by the corollary of the ſecond of this booke: the line TH ſhall be in power duple to the line TN or NH by the 47. of the firſt.

[Diagram: geometric conſtruction of the pyramid ABGD containing an inſcribed Icoſahedron, with labelled points]

For the lines TN or NH are equall, for that by conſtruction they are eche leſſe ſegmentes: and the line RT or TS is in power duple to the ſame line TN or NH (by the corollary of the 15. of this booke) for it ſubtendeth the angle of the triangle contayned vnder the two ſegmentes. Wherfore the lines TH, TS, TR, TQ, and TO are equall: and ſo alſo are the lines HS, SR, RQ, QO, and OH, which ſubtend the angles at the poynt T, equall. For the line QR contayneth in power the two lines PQ, and PR the leſſe ſegmentes, which two lines the line TH alſo contayned in power. And the reſt of the lines doo ſubtend angles (of equilater triangles) contayned vnder the greater ſegment and the leſſe. Wherefore the fiue triangles TRS, TSH, THO, TOQ, TQR are equilater and equall making the ſolide angle of an Icoſahedron at the poynt T, by the 16. of the thirtenth, in the ſide PN of the triangle PNM. And by the ſame reaſon in the other ſides of the 4. triangles PNM, NKL, EMK, & EFP, (which are inſcribed in the baſes of the pyramis) which ſides are 12. in number ſhall be ſet 12. angles of the Icoſahedrõ contayned vnder 20. equal & equilater triangles of which fower are ſet in the 4. baſes of the pyramis, namely, theſe fower triangles, TRS, HOI, CEQ, VXY: 4. triangles are vnder 4. angles of the pyramis: that is, the fower triangles CTX, YSH, ERV, TQO: and vnder euery one of the fixe ſides of the pyramis are ſet two triangles, namely: vnder the ſide GD the triangles THS and THO: vnder the ſide DB the triangles RQE and RQT: vnder the ſide DA the triangles COQ and COI: vnder the ſide AB, the triangles EXC and EXV: vnder the ſide BG the triangles SVR and SVY: and vnder the ſide AG the triangles IYH and IYX. Wherefore the ſolide being contayned vnder 20. equilater and equall triangles ſhall be an Icoſahedron by the 23. diffinitiõ of the eleuenth: and ſhall be inſcribed in the pyramis ABGD by the firſt diffinitiõ of this booke, for all his angles doo at one time touch the baſes of the pyramis. Wherefore in a trilater equilater pyramis geuen, we haue inſcribed an Icoſahedron.
