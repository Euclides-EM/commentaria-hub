## The ninth Propoſition.

[Subhead]
If a number be deuided into two equall numbers, and againe be deuided into two inequall partes: the ſquare numbers of the vnequall numbers, are double to the ſquare which is made of the multiplication of the halfe number into it ſelfe, together with the ſquare whiche is made of the number ſet betwene them.
[/Subhead]

For let the number AB being an euen number be deuided into two equall numbers AC & CB: & into two vnequall nũbers AD and DB. Then I ſay that the ſquare numbers of AD and DB, are double to the ſquares which are made of the multiplication of the numbers AC and CD into themſelues. For foraſmuch as the number AB is an euen number, and is deuided alſo into two equall numbers AC and CB, and afterward into two vnequall nũbers AD and DB: therefore the ſuperficiall nũber produced of the multiplication of the nũbers AD & DB, th’one into the other, together with the ſquare of the number DC, is equall to the ſquare of the number AC (by the fift propoſition). VVherfore the ſuperficiall number produced of the multiplication of the numbers AD and DB th’one into the other twiſe, together with two ſquares of the number CD, is double to the ſquare of the number AC.
