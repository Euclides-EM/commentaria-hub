### 25. Iſoſceles, is a triangle, which hath onely two ſides equall.

[Margin]
Definition of an Iſoſceles.
[/Margin]

The ſecond kinde of triangles hath two ſides of one length, but the third ſide is either longer or ſhorter thẽ the other two, as are the triangles here figured, B, C, D.

[Diagram: three triangles; B labelled A, E, and F; C labelled G, H, and K; D labelled L, M, and N]

In the triangle B, the two ſides AE and EF are equall the one to the other, and the ſide AF, is longer then any of them both: Likewiſe in the triangle C the two ſides GH and HK are equall, and the ſide GK is greater. Alſo in the triangle D, the ſides LM and MN, are equall, and the ſide LN is ſhorter.

### 26. Scalenum is a triangle, whoſe three ſides are all vnequall.

[Margin]
Definition of a Scalenum.
[/Margin]

As are the triangles E, F, in which there is no one ſide equall to any of the other. For in the triangle E, the ſide AC is greater then the ſide BC, and the ſide BC is greater thẽ the ſide AB. Likewiſe in the triangle F, the ſide DH, is greater thẽ the ſide DG, and the ſide DG, is greater then the ſide GH.

[Diagram: two triangles; E labelled A, B, and C; F labelled D, G, and H]

### 27. Againe of triangles, an Orthigonium or a right angled triangle, is a triangle which hath a right angle.

[Margin]
An Orthigonium triangle.
[/Margin]

As there are three kindes of triangles, by reaſon of the diuersitie of the ſides, ſo are there alſo three kindes of triangles, by reaſon of the varietie of the angles. For euery triangle either containeth one right angle, & two acute angles: or one obtuſe angle, & two acute: or three acute angles: for it is impoſſible that one triangle ſhould containe two obtuſe angles, or two right angles, or one obtuſe angle, and the other a right angle. All which kindes are here defined. Firſt a right angled triangle whiche hath in it a right angle. As the triangle BCD, of which the angle BCD, is a right angle.

### 28. An ambligonium or an obtuſe angled triangle, is a triangle which hath an obtuſe angle.

An Ambligonium triangle.
[/Margin]

As is the triangle B, whoſe angle ACD, is an obtuſe angle, and is alſo a Scalenon, hauing his three ſides vnequall: the triangle E, is likewiſe an Ambligonion, whoſe angle FGH, is an obtuſe angle, & is an Iſoſceles, hauing two of his ſides equall, namely FG and GH.
