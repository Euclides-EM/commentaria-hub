But now ſuppoſe that that which is comprehended vnder the lines A & C be equall vnto the ſquare made of the line B. Then alſo I ſay, that as the line A is to the line B, ſo is the line B to the lyne C. The ſame order of conſtruction that was before, beyng kept, foraſmuch as yͭ which is contained vnder the lynes A and C is equall vnto the ſquare which is made of the line B. But the ſquare which is made of the line B is that which is contained vnder yͤ lines B & D, for the line B is put equall vnto the line D. Wherefore that which is contayned vnder the lines A and C is equall vnto that which is contayned vnder the lines B and D. But if the rectangle figure comprehended vnder the extremes, be equall vnto the rectangle figure comprehended vnder the meane lynes; the foure right lines ſhall be proportionall (by the 16. of the ſixth) Wherfore as the line A is to the line B, ſo is the line D to the line C. But the line B is equall vnto the lyne D. Wherfore as the line A is to the lyne B, ſo is B to the line C. If therefore there be three right lynes in proportion, the rectangle figure comprehended vnder the extremes, is equall vnto the ſquare that is made of the meane. And if the rectangle figure which is contayned vnder the extremes, be equall vnto the ſquare made of the meane, then are thoſe three right lines proportionall: which was required to be demonſtrated.
