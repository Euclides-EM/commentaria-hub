## The 2. Probleme. The 10. Proposition.

[Subhead]
To deuide a right line geuẽ not deuided, like vnto a right line geuen beyng deuided.
[/Subhead]

[Margin]
Conſtruction.
[/Margin]

{dropcap:S|lines=4|style=decorated|decoration="ornamental floral frame"}Vppoſe that the right line geuen not deuided be AB, and the right lyne geuen being deuided, let be AC. It is required to deuide yͤ line AB which is not deuided like vnto the line AC which is deuided. Suppoſe the lyne AC be deuided in the pointes D and E, & let yͤ lines AB & AC ſo be put, that they make an angle at all aduentures, and draw a line from B to C, and by the pointes D and E draw vnto the line BC (by the 31. of the firſt) two parallel lines DF and EG: and by the point D vnto the line AB (by the ſame) draw a parallel line DHK. Wherefore either of theſe figures FH and HB are parallelogrammes.

Wherefore the line DH is equall vnto the line FG, and the line HK is equall vnto the line GB. And becauſe to one of the ſides of the triangle DKC, namely, to the ſide KC is drawen a parallel line HE, therefore the line CE (by the 2. of the ſixt) is in proportion vnto the line ED as the line KH is to the line HD: but the line KH is equall vnto the line BG, and the line HD is equall vnto the line GF. Wherefore (by the 11. of the fift) as CE is vnto ED, ſo is BG to GF. Agayne becauſe to one of the ſides of the triangle AGE, namely, to GE is drawen a parallel lyne FD, therefore the line ED (by the 2. of the ſixth) is in proportion vnto the lyne DA, as the line GF is to the line FA. And it is already proued that as CE is to ED, ſo is BG to GF. VVherfore as CE is to ED, ſo is BG to GF, and as ED is to DA, ſo is GF to FA. VVherfore the right line geuen not deuided, namely, AB, is deuided like vnto the right line geuen being deuided, which is AC: which was required to be done.
