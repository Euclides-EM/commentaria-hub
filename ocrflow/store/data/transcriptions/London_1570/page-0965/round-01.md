## ¶ A Corollary added by Fluſſas.

A perpendicular line from any angle drawen to the baſe of a pentagon, paſſeth by the centre.

For if we drawe a right line from the poynt A to the poynt C, and an other from the poynt A to the point D: thoſe right lines ſhall be equall, by the 4. of the firſt: and therefore in the triangle A C D the angles at the points C and D, are by the 5. of the firſt, equall. But the angles made at the point where the line A G cutteth the line C D, are by ſuppoſition right angles: wherefore by the 26. of the firſt, the line C D is by the line A G diuided into two equall partes, and it is alſo diuided perpendicularly: wherefore by the corollary of the firſt of the third in the line A G is the centre of the circle: and therefore the line A G paſſeth by the centre.

If in a circle hauing a rationall line to his diameter be inſcribed an equilater pentagon: the ſide of the pentagon is an irrationall line, and is of that kinde which is called a leſſe line.
