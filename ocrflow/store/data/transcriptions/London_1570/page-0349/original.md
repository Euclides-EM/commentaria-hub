## The 12. Theoreme. The 12. Proposition.

[Subhead]
If there be a number of magnitudes how many ſoeuer proportionall: as one of the antecedentes is to one of the conſequentes, ſo are all the antecedentes to all the conſequentes.
[/Subhead]

[Margin]
Conſtruction.
[/Margin]

Then I ſay, that as A is to B, ſo is A, C, E, to B, D, F. Take equemultiplices to A, C, and E. And let the ſame be G, H, K.
