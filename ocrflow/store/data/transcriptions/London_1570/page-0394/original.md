But now ſuppoſe that the ſides about the equall angles be reciprocally proportionall, ſo that as the ſide DB is to the ſide BE, ſo let the ſide GB be to the ſide BF. Then I ſay, ȳ the parallelogramme AB is equall vnto ȳ parallelogramme BC. For, for that as the ſide DB is to the ſide BE, ſo is the ſide GB to the ſide BF: but as the ſide DB is to the ſide BE, ſo (by the 1. of the ſixt) is the parallelogramme AB to the parallelogramme FE: and as the ſide GB is to the ſide BF, ſo is the parallelogramme BC to the parallelogramme FE. Wherefore alſo (by the 11. of the fift) as the parallelogramme AB is to the parallelogramme FE, ſo is the parallelogramme BC to the ſame parallelogramme FE. Wherefore the parallelogramme AB is equall vnto the parallelogramme BC (by the 9. of the fift). Wherefore in equall and equiangle parallelogrammes the ſides which containe the equall angles are reciprocally: and if in equiangle parallelogrammes the ſides which containe the equall angles be reciprocally, the parallelogrammes alſo ſhall be equall: which was required to be proued.
