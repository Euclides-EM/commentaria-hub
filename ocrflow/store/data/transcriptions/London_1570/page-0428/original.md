### ¶ The firſt Corollary.

[Subhead]
To reſolue a rectiline figure geuen into two like rectiline figures which ſhall haue alſo a proportion geuẽ.
[/Subhead]

[Margin]
The firſt Corollary.
[/Margin]

For if there be put three right lines in the proportiõ geuẽ, and if the line AB be cut in the ſame proportion that the firſt line is to the third, the rectiline figures deſcribed of the lines AF and FB (which figures haue the ſame proportion that the lines AE and EB haue) ſhall be in double proportion to that which the lines AF and FB are (by the firſt Corollary of the 20. of this booke). Wherfore the right lines AF and FB are the one to the other in the ſame proportion that the firſt of the three lines put is to the ſecond. For the firſt line to the third, namely, the line AE to the line EB is in double proportion that it is to the ſecond, by the 10. definition of the fift.

### ¶ The ſecond Corollary.

[Subhead]
Hereby may we learne, how from a rectiline figure geuen to take away a part appoynted, leauing the reſt of the rectiline figure like vnto the whole.
[/Subhead]

[Margin]
The ſecond Corollary.
[/Margin]

For if frõ the right line AB be cut of a part appoynted, namely, EB (by the 9. of this booke) as the line AE is to the line EB, ſo is the rectiline figure deſcribed of the line AF to the rectiline figure deſcribed of the line FB (the ſayd figures being ſuppoſed to be like both the one to the other and alſo to the rectiline figure deſcribed of the line AB, and being alſo in like ſort ſituate). Wherfore taking away from the rectiline figure deſcribed of the line AB, the rectiline figure deſcribed of the line FB, the reſidue, namely, the rectiline figure deſcribed of the line AF ſhall be both like vnto the whole rectiline figure geuen deſcribed of the line AB, and in like ſort ſituate.

### ¶ The third Corollary.

[Subhead]
To compoſe two like rectiline figures into one rectiline figure like and equall to the ſame figures.
[/Subhead]

[Margin]
The third Corollary.
[/Margin]

Let their ſides of like proportiõ be ſet ſo that they make a right angle, as the lines AF and FB are. And vpõ the line ſubtending the ſaid angle, namely, the line AB, deſcribe a rectiline figure like vnto the rectiline figures geuen and in like ſort ſituate (by the 18. of this booke) and the ſame ſhall be equall to the two rectiline figures geuen (by the 31. of this booke).

## ¶ The ſecond Propoſition.

[Subhead]
If two right lines cut the one the other obtuſe angled wiſe, and from the endes of the lines which cut the one the other be drawen perpendicular lines to either line: the lines which are betwene the endes and the perpendicular lines are cut reciprocally.
[/Subhead]
