And by the ſame reaſon may be proued, that the reſt of the eleuen ſolide angles of the Icoſahedron, are ſet in the ſections of euery one of the ſides of the Octohedron, namely, in the pointes E, N, V, H, K, M, X, D, S, Q, T. Wherefore there are 12. angles of the Icoſahedron. Moreouer, foraſmuch as euery one of the baſes of the Octohedron, do eche containe triangles of the Icoſahedron, as in the pyramis ABCFP (which is the halfe of the Octohedron) the triangle FCP receaueth in the ſections of his ſides the triangle GMS: and the triangle CPB containeth the triangle NXS: and the triangle BAP contayneth the triangle HND: and moreouer the triangle APF containeth the triangle KDG; and the ſame may be proued in the oppoſite pyramis ABCFL: Wherefore there ſhall be eight triangles. And foraſmuch as beſides theſe triangles, to euery one of the ſolide angles of the Octohedron are ſubtended two triangles, as the triangles KEG and MEG, to the angle F: and the triangles HNV and XNV, to the angle B: alſo the triangles NDS and GDS, to the angle P: likewiſe the triangles DHK and QHK, to the angle A: Moreouer the triangles EQT and VQT, to the angle L: and finally the triangles SXM and TXM, to the angle C: theſe 12. triangles being added to the 8. former triangles, ſhall produce 20. triangles equall and equilater coupled together: which ſhall make an Icoſahedron, by the 25. definition of the eleuenth: and it ſhall be inſcribed in the Octohedron geuen ABCFPL, by the firſt definition of this booke: for the 12. angles thereof are ſet in 12. like ſections of the ſides of the Octohedron. Wherefore in an Octohedron geuen, is inſcribed an Icoſahedron.

### ¶ Firſt Corollary.

[Subhead]
The ſide of an equilater triangle being diuided by an extreme and meane proportion: a right line ſubtending within the triangle, the angle which is contained vnder the greater ſegment and the leſſe: is in power duple to the leſſe ſegment of the ſame ſide.
[/Subhead]

For the line KE, which ſubtendeth the angle KFE of the triangle AFL, which angle KFE is contained vnder the two ſegmentes KF & FE, was proued equall to the line HK, which containeth in power the two leſſe ſegmentes HA and AK, by the 47. of the firſt, for the angle HAK is a right angle. Wherefore the line KE or HK, is in power duple to the line AK.

### ✣ Second Corollary.

[Subhead]
The baſes of the Icoſahedron are concentricall (that is, haue one and the ſelfe ſame centre) with the baſes of the Octohedron which contayneth it.
[/Subhead]

For ſuppoſe that ABG be the baſe of an Octohedron contayning ECD the baſe of an Icoſahedron: and let the centre of the baſe ABG be the point F. And drawe theſe right lines FA, FB, FC, and FE. Now then the two lines FA and AE ſhall be equall to the two lines FB and BC: for they are lines drawen from the centre, and are alſo leſſe ſegmentes: and they contayne the halfes of equall angles. Wherfore (by the 4. of the firſt) the baſes FC and FE are equall: and by the ſame reaſon vnto them ſhall be equall the other line FD. Wherefore making the centre the point F: and the ſpace FE deſcribe a circle and it ſhall be circumſcribed about the triangle CED: and ſo ſhall the point F the centre of the baſe of the Octohedron be the centre of CED the baſe of the Icoſahedron.
