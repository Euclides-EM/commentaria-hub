And now if in theſe ſolides thus compoſed, ye take away the ſolide angles, there ſhalbe reſtored againe the firſt compoſed ſolides: namely, the ſolide angles taken away from a Dodecahedron and an Icoſahedron compoſed into one, there ſhalbe left an Icoſidodecahedron: the ſolide angles takẽ away from a cube, and an octohedrõ cõpoſed into one ſolide, there ſhalbe left an exocthedrõ.
