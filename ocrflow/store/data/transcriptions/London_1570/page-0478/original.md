## ¶ The 11. Theoreme.  The 13. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
If there be numbers in continuall proportion how many ſo euer, and ech multiplying himſelfe produce certayne numbers, the numbers of them produced ſhall be proportionall. And if thoſe numbers geuen at the beginning multiplying the numbers produced, produce other numbers, they alſo ſhalbe proportionall: and ſo ſhall it be continuing infinitely.
