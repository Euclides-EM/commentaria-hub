This propoſition Pelitarius calleth Gnomicall, and miſticall, for that of it (ſayth he) ſpring infinite demonſtrations, and vſes in geometry. And he putteth the conuerſe thereof after this manner.

[Margin]
This propoſition called Gnomical and miſtical.
[/Margin]

If a parallelogramme be deuided into two equall ſupplementes, and into two complements whatſoeuer: the diameter of the two complementes ſhall be ſet directly, and make one diameter of the whole parallelogramme.

[Margin]
The conuerſe of this propoſition.
[/Margin]
