### An example of this Propoſition in numbers.

Take a number, as 11. and deuide it into two partes, namely, 7. and 4: and multiply 11. into 7, and then into 4. and there ſhalbe produced 77. and 44: both which numbers added together make 121. which is equall to the ſquare number produced of the multiplication of the number 11. into himſelfe, as you ſee in the example.
