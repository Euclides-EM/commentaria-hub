This Propoſition ſheweth the generation of the fourth irrationall line, called a ſecond bimediall line. The definition wherof is euident by this Propoſition, which is thus. A ſecond bimediall line is an irrationall line, which is made of two mediall lines commenſurable in power onely ioyned together, which comprehend a mediall ſuperficies. And it is called a ſecond bimediall, becauſe the two mediall lines of which it is compoſed, cõtaine a mediall ſuperficies, and not a rationall. Now a mediall is by nature & in knowledge after a rationall.
