## The 26. Theoreme. The 31. Propoſit.

Parallelipipedons conſiſting vpon equall baſes, and being vnder one and the ſelfe ſame altitude, are equall the one to the other.

{dropcap:S|lines=4|style=decorated|decoration="floriated initial with flower"}Vppoſe that vppon theſe equall baſes A B and C D do conſiſte theſe parallelipipedons A E and C F, being vnder one & the ſelf ſame altitude. Thẽ I ſay, that the ſolide A E is equall to the ſolide C F. Firſt let the ſtanding lines, namely, H K, B E, A G, L M, O P, D F, C X, and R S, be erected perpendicularly to the baſes A B and C D, and let the angle A L B not be equall to the angle C R D.
