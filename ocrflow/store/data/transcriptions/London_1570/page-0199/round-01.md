## The 5. Theoreme. The 5. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
If a right line be deuided into two equall partes, & into two vnequall partes: the rectangle figures comprehended vnder the vnequall partes of the whole, together with the ſquare of that which is betwene the ſectiõs, is equall to the ſquare which is made of the halfe.
