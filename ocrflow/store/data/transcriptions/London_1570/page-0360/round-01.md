## The 22. Theoreme. The 22. Proposition.

[Subhead]
If there be a number of magnitudes, how many ſoeuer in one order, and as many other magnitudes in an other order, which being taken two and two in ech order are in one and the ſame proportion, they ſhall alſo of equalitie be in one and the ſame proportion.
