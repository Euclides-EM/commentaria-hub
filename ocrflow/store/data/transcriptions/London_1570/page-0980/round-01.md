Wherefore the ſubtending lines P O, P L, V O, V X, T X, T N, S N, S M, R M, R L, containe in power the ſelfe ſame ſquare that the ſides of the pentagon O L M N X containe, or that the ſides of the pentagon P R S T V containe: and therefore thoſe ſubtending lines are equall to the ſides of the foreſaid pentagons. Wherefore the triangles contained of thoſe ſubtending lines and of the ſides of the pentagons, and which are ten in number, namely, P L O, O V P, V O X, V X T, T X N, T N S, S N M, S M R, R M L, and R L P, are equilater. Againe produce the right line Z W on either ſide to the points Q & Y: and vnto the ſide of the decagon, namely, to the line O E, put the lines Z Y and W Q equal.
