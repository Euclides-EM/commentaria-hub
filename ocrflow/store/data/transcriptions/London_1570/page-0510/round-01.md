## ¶ The 22. Theoreme. The 22. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
*If odde numbers how many ſoeuer be added together, & if their multitude be euen, the whole alſo ſhall be euen.*
[/Subhead]

[Margin]
Demonſtration.
[/Margin]

{dropcap:S|lines=3|style=decorated|decoration="foliate pattern"}uppoſe that theſe odde numbers A B, B C, C D, and D E, being euen in multitude, be added together. Then I ſay, that the whole A E is an euen number. For foraſmuch as euery one of theſe numbers A B, B C, C D, and D E, is an odde number, if ye take away vnitie from euery one of them, that which remayneth of euery one of them is an euen number.

[Diagram: a dotted horizontal line divided by points labelled A, B, C, D, and E]

Wherefore they all added together, are (by the 21. of the ninth) an euen number: and the multitude of the vnities taken away is euen. Wherefore the whole A E is an euen number: which was required to be proued.

## ¶ The 23. Theoreme. The 23. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
*If odde numbers how many ſoeuer be added together, and if the multitude of them be odde, the whole alſo ſhall be odde.*
[/Subhead]

[Margin]
Demonſtration.
[/Margin]

{dropcap:S|lines=3|style=decorated|decoration="foliate pattern"}uppoſe that theſe odde numbers A B, B C, and C D being odde in multitude be added together. Then I ſay, that the whole A D is an odde number. Take away from C D, vnitie D E, wherefore that which remayneth C E is an euen number. But A C alſo (by the 22. of the ninth) is an euen number. Wherefore the whole A E is an euen number.

[Diagram: a dotted horizontal line divided by points labelled A, B, C, E, and D]

But D E which is vnitie being added to the euen number A E, maketh the whole A D an odde number: which was required to be proued.
