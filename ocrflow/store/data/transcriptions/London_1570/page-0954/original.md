### A Corollary. 2.

[Margin]
Note 4. wayes of progreſſion, in the proportion of a line diuided by extreme and middle proportion.
[/Margin]

It is alſo manifeſt, that you may by any of the fower wayes, here ſpecified, proceede infinitely, in the proportion of a line deuided by extreame & middle proportion: And in the firſt, and fourth wayes, encreaſing continually the quantities of the lines made: but in the ſecond and third wayes, diminiſhing continually the quantities of the ſayd whole lines, made (and thereby their ſegmentes). And yet, neuertheleſſe reteyning in euery line made (by any of the wayes) and in his ſegments, all, and the ſame properties, which the firſt line, and his ſegmentes haue. After which rate of Progreſſion, as the termes in continuall proportion do encreaſe, and are moe in number: So, likewiſe, do the middle proportionalls, (accordingly) become moe: But euer fewer in number, by two, then the termes of the Progreſſion are.

### ¶ What Reſolution is.

[Margin]
What reſolution and compoſition is, hath before bene taught in the beginning of the firſt booke.
[/Margin]

Reſolution, is the aſſumption or taking of the thing which is to be proued, as graunted, and by thinges which neceſſarily follow it, to paſſe vnto ſome truth graunted.

### ¶ What Compoſition is.

Compoſition, is an aſſumption or taking of a thing graunted, and by thinges which of neceſſity follow it, to paſſe vnto the finding out of the thing ſought or to be proued.
