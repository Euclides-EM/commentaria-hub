### An example of this Propoſition in numbers.

Suppoſe a number, namely, 14. to be deuided into two partes 8. and 6. The whole number 14. multiplied into 8. one of his partes, produceth 112: the partes 8. & 6. multiplied the one into the other produce 48, which added to 64 (which is the ſquare of 8. the former part of the number) amounteth alſo to 112: whiche is equall to the former ſumme.
