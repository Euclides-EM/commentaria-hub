[Margin]
A Dodecahedron.
[/Margin]

Deſcribe this figure which conſiſteth of twelue equilater and equiangle Pentagons, vpon the foreſaid matter, and finely cut as before was taught the eleuen lines contained within the figure, and bow and folde the Pentagons accordingly. And they will ſo cloſe together, that they will make the very forme of a Dodecahedron.
