Certaine additions of Campane.

Here Campane addeth, that the two circles ACD and BDE, do cut the one the other: and that the circle ACD cutteth of from the circle BDE an arke equall to the arke BD, and that the circle BDE cutteth of from the circle ACD an arke equall to the arke DC.

[Margin]
The firſt part demonſtrated.
[/Margin]

The firſt part is manifeſt. For if the leſſe do not cut the greater but touch it as in the poynt D. Then (by the 11. of the third) the centre of either of them ſhall be in one line, namely, in the line AD: for that in it is the centre of the greater circle, and in the ſelfe ſame is the poynt of the touch. Wherefore (by the 31. of the third) the angle ACD is a right angle. And therfore (by the 13. of the firſt) the angle DCB is a right angle: and ſo ſhall the angle ABD be a right angle (for it is equall to the angle DCB, as it hath bene proued) which (by the 32. of the firſt) is impoſſible. Wherefore they ſhall cut the one the other as in the poyntes D & E. Now I ſay, that the arke ED of the greater circle, is equall to the arke DB: and that the arke ED of the leſſe circle, is equall to the arke DC.

The ſecond part demonſtrated.
[/Margin]

Wherfore the whole angle AED is double to the angle BAD, & therfore is equall to either of the angles ABD and ADB. And foraſmuch as the angle AED is equall to the angle ADE (by the 5. of the firſt) for that the lines AD and AE are drawen from the centre, therefore the two angles at the pointes E and D of the triangle AED, ſhall be equall to the two angles at the poyntes D and B of the triangle ADB: and therefore the angle remayning of the one at the poynt A, ſhall be equall to the angle remayning of the other at the ſame point A (by the 32. of the firſt).
