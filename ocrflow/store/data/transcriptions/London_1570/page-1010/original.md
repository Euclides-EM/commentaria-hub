## A Corollary.

[Margin]
This Corollary Campane alſo addeth after the 7. propoſition in his order.
[/Margin]

*The ſuperficieces of a Dodecahedron and of an Icoſahedron deſcribed in one and the ſelfe ſame ſphere, are the one to the other, as that which is contained vnder the ſide of the one and the perpendicular line drawne vnto it from the centre of his baſe, to that which is contained vnder the ſide of the other, and the perpendicular line drawne to it from the centre of his baſe.* For as thirtye times is to thirty times, ſo is once to once by the 15. of the fifth.

## The 6. Propoſition.

[Margin]
The 5. propoſition after Campane.
[/Margin]

*The ſuperficies of a Dodecahedron, is to the ſuperficies of an Icoſahedron deſcribed in one and the ſelfe ſame ſphere, in that proportion, that the ſide of the Cube is to the ſide of the Icoſahedron contained in the ſelf ſame ſphere.*
