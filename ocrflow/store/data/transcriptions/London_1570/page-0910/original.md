Now foraſmuch as the axes L N, N E, and E K are equall the one to the other: Therefore the cylinders P R, R B, and B G are (by the 11. of the twelueth) in proportion the one to the other as their baſes are. And foraſmuch as the axes L N, N E, and E K are equall the one to the other, and the cylinders P R, R B, and B G are alſo equall the one to the other, and the multitude of the axes L N, N E, and E K is equall to the multitude of the cylinders P R, R B, and B G: therefore how multiplex the whole axe K L is to the axe E K, ſo multiplex is the whole cylinder P G to the cylinder B G. And (by the ſame reaſon) alſo how multiplex the whole axe M K is the axe K F, ſo multiplex is the whole cylinder W G to the cylinder G D. Wherefore if the axe K L be equall the axe K M, the cylinder P G is equall to cylinder G W. And if the axe K L be greater then the axe K M, the cylinder P G is greater then the cylinder G W. And if it be leſſe it is leſſe. Now therefore there are foure magnitudes, namely, the two axes E K, and K F, and the two cylinders B G, and G D, and vnto the axe E K, and to the cylinder B G, namely, to the firſt and the third are taken equemultiplices, namely, the axe K L, and the cylinder P G. And likewiſe vnto the axe K F, and vnto the cylinder G D, namely, the ſecond and the fourth, are taken other equemultiplices, namely, the axe K M, and the cylinder G W. And it is proued that if the axe K L, exceede the axe K M, the cylinder P G exceedeth the cylinder G W: and that if it be equall it is equall, and if it be leſſe it is leſſe. Wherefore (by the 6. definition of the fift) as the axe E K is to the axe K F, ſo is the cylinder B G to the cylinder G D. If therefore a cylinder be diuided by a plaine ſuperficies being a parallel to the two oppoſite plaine ſuperficieces: then as the one cylinder is to the other cilinder, ſo is the axe of the one to the axe of the other: which was required to be proued.

## ¶ The 14. Theoreme.  The 14. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
Cones and Cylinders conſiſting vpon equall baſes, are in proportion the one to the other as their altitudes.
[/Subhead]
