For ( by the 7. of the eleuenth ) if there be any two parallell right lines, and if in either of them be taken a point at allauentures, a right line drawn by theſe points is in one, & the ſelfe ſame playne ſuperficies with the parallels. And by the ſame reaſon alſo euery one of the fower ſided figures S O P T, and T P R V is in one and the ſelfe ſame playne ſuperficies. And the triangle V R X is alſo in one and the ſelfe ſame plaine ſuperficies ( by the 2. of the eleuenth ).
