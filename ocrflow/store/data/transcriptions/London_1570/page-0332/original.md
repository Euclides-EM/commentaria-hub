## Conuerſe proportion, or proportion by conuerſion is, when the conſequent is taken as the antecedent, and ſo is compared to the antecedent as to the conſequent.

[Margin]
The thirtenth definition.
[/Margin]

Suppoſe as before foure magnitudes in proportion, A, B, C, D, as A to B, ſo C to D: if ye referre B the conſequent of the firſt proportion, as antecedent, to A the antecedent of the firſt, as to his conſequent: and likewiſe if ye referre D the conſequẽt of the ſecond proportion as antecedẽt to C the antecedẽt of the ſecond proportiõ, as to his cõſequent: thẽ ſhall ye haue the magnitudes in thys order. As B to A cõſequent to antecedẽt: ſo D to C conſequent to antecedẽt. And thys is called cõuerſe proportion. So alſo in numbers, 9. to 3, as 6. to 2, eyther is tripla, wherfore comparing 3. to 9, the conſequent of the firſt to hys antecedent 9, and alſo 2. the conſequent of the ſecond to hys antecedent 6, by conuerſe proportion it commeth to paſſe as 3. to 9, ſo 2. to 6: For either is ſubtripla.
