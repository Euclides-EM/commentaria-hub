## ¶ An Aſſumpt.

A rationall ſuperficies exceedeth a rationall ſuperficies, by a rationall ſuperficies.

Let A D be a rationall ſuperficies, and let it exceede A F being alſo a rationall ſuperficies by the ſuperficies E D. Then I ſay that the ſuperficies E D is alſo rationall. For the parallelogramme A D is commenſurable to the parallelogramme A F, for that either of them is rationall. Wherefore (by the ſecond part of the 15. of the tenth) the parallelogramme A F is commenſurable to the parallelogramme E D. But the the parallelogramme A F is rationall. Wherfore alſo the parallelogramme E D is rationall.
