## ¶ The 31. Theoreme. The 37. Propoſition.

If without a circle be taken a certaine point, and from that point be drawen to the circle two right lines, of which, the one doth cut the circle and the other falleth vpon the circle, and that in ſuch ſort, that the rectangle parallelogramme which is cõtayned vnder the whole right line which cutteth the circle, and that portion of the ſame line that lieth betwene the point and the vtter circumferẽce of the circle, is equall to the ſquare made of the line that falleth vpon the circle: then that line that ſo falleth vpon the circle ſhall touch the circle.
