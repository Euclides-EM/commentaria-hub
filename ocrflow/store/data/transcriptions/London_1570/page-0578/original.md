## ¶ The 12. Probleme. The 35. Propoſition.

To finde out two right lines incommenſurable in power, whoſe ſquares added together, make a mediall ſuperficies, and the parallelogramme contained vnder them, make alſo a mediall ſuperficies, which parallelogramme moreouer, ſhall be incommenſurable to the ſuperficies made of the ſquares of thoſe lines added together.
