Wherefore it ſhall touch them. And it is deſcribed in the ſquare ABCD: wherefore in a ſquare geuen is deſcribed a circle: which was required to be done.

## ❧ The 9. Probleme. The 9. Propoſition.

About a ſquare geuen, to deſcribe a circle.
