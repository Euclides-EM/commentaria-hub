The like varietie in ech of the other two caſes. Euclides conſtruction and demonſtration ſerueth in all theſe caſes, and in their varities alſo.
[/Margin]

And the two other caſes alſo may in like maner haue ſeuen varieties: as in the figures here vnderneth and on the other ſide of this leafe ſet it is manifeſt. And here is to be noted, that in theſe three caſes and in all their varieties alſo, the conſtruction & demonſtration put by Euclide (namely, the drawing of lines frõ the point B to the point E & from the pointe C to the point H, and ſo prouing it by the former propoſition) will ſerue onely in the fourth varietie of ech caſe, there nedeth no farther conſtruction: for that the concluſion ſtraight way followeth by the former propoſition.
