### An other more briefe demonſtration of the ſame after Campane.

[Margin]
An other demonſtration after Campane.
[/Margin]

Suppoſe that A be a greater line, vnto which let the line B be commẽſurable, either in length and power, or in power onely. And take a rational line C D. And vpon it apply the ſuperficies C E equall to the ſquare of the line A: and alſo vpõ the line F E (which is equall to the rationall line C D) apply the parallelogramme F G equall to the ſquare of the line B. And foraſmuch as the ſquares of the two lines A and B are commenſurable by ſuppoſition, the ſuperficies C E, ſhalbe commenſurable vnto the ſuperficies F G: and therefore by the firſt of the ſixt and tenth of this booke, the line D E is commenſurable in length to the line G E. And foraſmuch as (by the 63. of this booke) the line D E is a fourth binomiall line, therefore by the 66. of this booke the line G E is alſo a fourth binomiall line: wherefore by the 57. of this booke the line B which contayneth in power the ſuperficies F G is a greater line.

## ¶ The 51. Theoreme. The 69. Propoſition.

A line commenſurable to a line contayning in power a rationall and a mediall: is alſo a line contayning in power a rationall and a mediall.

[Margin]
Conſtruction.
[/Margin]

{dropcap:S|lines=4|style=decorated|decoration="white letter on crosshatched square ground"}Vppoſe that A B be a line contayning in power a rationall and a mediall. And vnto the line A B let the line C D be commenſurable, whether in length and power, or in power onely. Thẽ I ſay that the line C D is a line cõtayning in power a rationall & a mediall. Duide the line A B into his parts in the poynt E. Wherfore (by the 40. of the tenth) the lines A E and E B are incommenſurable in power, hauing that which is made of the ſquares of them added together medial, and that which is contayned vnder thẽ rationall. Let the ſame conſtruction be in this that was in the former. And in like ſort we may proue that the lines C F and F D are incommenſurable in power, and that that which is made of the ſquares of the lines A E and E B is commenſurable to that which is made of the ſquares of the lines C F and F D, and that that alſo which is contayned vnder the lines A E and E B is commẽſurable to that which is contayned vnder the lines C F and F D. Wherefore that which is made of the ſquares of the lines C F and F D is mediall, and that which is contayned vnder the lines C F and F D is rationall. Wherefore the whole line C D is a line contayning in power a rationall and a mediall: which was required to be demonſtrated.
