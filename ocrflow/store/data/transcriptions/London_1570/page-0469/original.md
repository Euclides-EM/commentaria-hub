And foraſmuch as (by the 20. of the ſeuenth) that alwaies happeneth touching the extremes, namely, that A and B multiplying the numbers produced F and K, ſhall produce other prime numbers, namely, the extremes of fiue numbers in continuall proportion, therefore (by the firſt of this booke) all fiue are the leſt of that proportion. ### ¶ Corollary.

Hereby it is manifest, that if three numbers being in continuall proportion, be the leſt of all numbers that haue the ſame proportion with them, their extremes are ſquares: and if there be foure their extremes are cubes.

For the extremes of three are produced of the multiplying of the nũbers A and B into them ſelues. And the extremes of foure are produced of the multiplying of the rootes A and B into the ſquares C and E, whereby are made the cubes F and K.
