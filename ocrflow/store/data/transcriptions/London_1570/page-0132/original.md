## The 15. Theoreme. The 24. Propoſition.

If two triangles haue two ſides of the one equall to two ſides of the other, ech to his correſpondent ſide, and if the angle cōtained vnder the equall ſides of the one, be greater then the angle contayned vnder the equall ſides of the other: the baſe alſo of the ſame, ſhalbe greater then the baſe of the other.
