## A fourth reſiduall line is, when the ſquare of the whole lyne excedeth the ſquare of the lyne adioyned, by the ſquare of a lyne incommenſurable vnto it in length, and the whole lyne is alſo commenſurable in length to the rationall lyne.

[Margin]
Fourth diffinition.
[/Margin]

[Figure: three horizontal lines, the first labelled A and B, the second labelled D, C and E, the third labelled F]

As the reſiduall lyne beyng as before C D, & the lyne adioyned E C, and the whole E D, let the ſquare of the whole lyne E D exceede the ſquare of the line adioyned E C by the ſquare of the lyne F, and let the lyne F be incommenſurable in length to the whole line E D, and let E D the whole line be commenſurable in length to the rationall lyne A B, then is the reſiduall line C D by this declaration a fourth reſiduall lyne.

## A fiueth reſiduall line is, when the ſquare of the whole lyne exceedeth the ſquare of the lyne adioyned, by the ſquare of a lyne incommenſurable vnto it in length, and the lyne adioyned is commenſurable in length to the rationall lyne.

[Margin]
Fifth diffinition.
[/Margin]

[Figure: two horizontal lines, the first labelled D, C and E, the second labelled F]

As the reſiduall line beyng C D, the lyne adioyned E C, and the whole lyne E D, let the ſquare of the whole lyne E D exceede the ſquare of the line adioyned E C by the ſquare of the lyne F, and let the line F be incommenſurable in length to the whole lyne E D, and let alſo E C the lyne adioyned be commenſurable in length to the rationall line A B, then ſhall the reſiduall C D be by this definition a fifth reſiduall lyne.

## A ſixth reſiduall line is when the ſquare of the whole line, exceedeth the ſquare of the line adioyned, by the ſquare of a line incommenſurable vnto it in length, and neither the whole line nor the line adioyned is commenſurable in length to the rationall line.

[Margin]
Sixth diffinition.
[/Margin]

[Figure: two horizontal lines, the first labelled D, C and E, the second labelled F]

As ſuppoſe the reſiduall line to be C D, and the lyne adioyned to be E C, and the whole lyne compoſed of them let be E D, and let the ſquare of the whole lyne E D exceede the ſquare of the line adioyned by the ſquare of the lyne F, which line F let be incommenſurable in length to the whole lyne E D : moreouer let neither the whole lyne C D nor the line adioyned E C, be commenſurable in length to the rationall line A B, then ſhall the reſiduall lyne C D be by this explication a ſixt reſiduall lyne, and the laſt.
