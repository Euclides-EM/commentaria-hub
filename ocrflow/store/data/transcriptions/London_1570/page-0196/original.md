But C B is equall vnto G K, and C G is equall vnto K B: wherfore G K is equall vnto K B. VVherfore the figure C G K B conſiſteth of foure equall ſides. I ſay alſo that it is a rectangle figure. For foraſmuch as C G is a parallel vnto B K & vpon thẽ falleth a right line C B, therfore (by yͤ 29. of the 1.) the angles K B C, and G C B are equall vnto two right angles. But the angle K B C is a right angle, wherfore yͤ angle B C G is alſo a right angle. VVherfore (by the 34. of the firſt) the angles oppoſite vnto them, namely, C G K, and G K B are right angles. VVherfore C G K B is a rectangle figure. And it was before proued that the ſides are equall. VVherfore it is a ſquare, and it is deſcribed vpon the line B C. And by the ſame reaſon alſo H F is a ſquare, and is deſcribed vpon the line H G, that is, vpon the line A C.*

*VVherfore the ſquares H F and C K are made of the lines A C and C B. And foraſmuch as the parallelograme A G is (by the 43. of the firſt) equall vnto the parallelogramme G E. And A G is that which is contayned vnder A C and C B, for C G is equall vnto C B, wherfore G E is equall to that which is contained vnder A C and C B. VVherfore A G and G E are equall vnto that which is comprehended vnder A C and C B twiſe. And the ſquares H F and C K are made of the lines A C and C B. VVherfore theſe foure rectangle figures H F, C K, A G, and G E are equall vnto the ſquares which are made of the lines A C and C B, and to the rectangle figure which is comprehended vnder the lines A C and C B twiſe. But the rectangle figures H F, C K, A G, and G E are the whole rectangle figure A D E B which is the ſquare made of the line A B. VVherfore the ſquare which is made of the line A B is equall to the ſquares which are made of the lines A C and C B, and vnto the rectangle figure, which is comprehended vnder the lines A C and C B twiſe. If therfore a right line be deuided by chaunce, the ſquare which is made of the whole line, is equall to the ſquares which are made of the partes, & vnto the rectangle figure which is comprehended vnder the partes twiſe: which was required to be proued.*
