For foraſmuch as what partes A B is of C, the ſelfe ſame partes is D E of F: therefore how many partes of C there are in A B, ſo many partes of F are there in D E. Deuide A B into the partes of C, that is, into A G and G B, and likewiſe D E into the partes of F, that is, into D H and H E.

Now then the multitude of theſe A G and G B is equall to the multitude of theſe D H and H E. And foraſmuch as what part A G is of C, the ſelfe ſame part is D H of F: therefore what part A G is of C, the ſelfe ſame part is A G and D H added together of C and F added together. And by the ſame reaſon alſo what part G B is of C, the ſelfe ſame part is G B and H E added together of C and F added together. Wherefore what partes A B is of C, the ſelfe ſame partes are A B and D E added together of C and F added together: which was required to be demonſtrated.

## ¶ The 5. Theoreme. The 7. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
If a number be the ſelfe ſame part of a number, that a part taken away is of a part taken away: then ſhall the reſidue be the ſelfe ſame part of the reſidue, that the whole was of the whole.
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:S|lines=4|style=decorated|decoration="ornamental foliage"}uppoſe that the number A B be of the number C D the ſelfe ſame part, that the part taken away A E is of the part taken away C F. Then I ſay, that the reſidue E B is of the reſidue F D the ſelfe ſame part that the whole A B is of the whole C D.

[Margin]
This propoſition and the next following in diſcrete quãtitie anſwereth to the fifth propoſitiõ of the fifth boke in continuall quãtity. Conſtruction. Demonſtration.
[/Margin]

What part A E is of C F, the ſelfe ſame part let E B be of G C. And for that what part A E is of C F, the ſame part is E B of G C, therefore what part A E is of C F, the ſame part (by the 5. of the ſeuenth) is A B of F G.

[Diagram: two horizontal dotted lines labelled G, C, F, D; and A, E, B]

But what part A E is of C F, the ſame part (by ſuppoſition) is A B of C D. Wherefore what part A B is of F G, the ſelfe ſame part is A B of C D. Wherefore A B is one & the ſelfe ſame part of both theſe numbers G F and C D. Wherefore G F is equall vnto C D (by the ſecond common ſentence of the ſeuenth). Take away C E which is common to them both. Wherefore the reſidue G C is equall vnto the reſidue E D. And foraſmuch as what part A E is of C F, the ſame part is E B of G C: but G C is equall vnto E D: therefore what part A E is of F C, the ſelfe ſame part is E B of F D. But what part A E is of C F, the ſame part is A B of C D. Wherefore what part E B is of F D, the ſame part is A B of C D. Wherefore the reſidue E B is of the reſidue F D the ſelfe ſame part that the whole A B is of the whole C D: which was required to be demonſtrated.

## ¶ The 6. Theoreme. The 8. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
If a number be of a number the ſelfe ſame partes, that a part taken away is of a part taken away, the reſidue alſo ſhall be of the reſidue the ſelfe ſame partes that the whole is of the whole.
[/Subhead]
