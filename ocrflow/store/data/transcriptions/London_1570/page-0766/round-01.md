## M. Dee his annotation.

Euclides wordes in this 13. propoſition admit two caſes: one, if the poynt aſsigned be in the playne ſuperficies, (as cõmonly the demonſtrations ſuppoſe) the other, if the poynt aſsigned be any where with out the ſayd playne ſuperficies, to which, the perpendiculars fall, is conſidered. Contrary to either of which, if the aduerſarie affirme, admitting from one poynt two right lines, perpendiculars to one and the ſelfe ſame playne ſuperficies, and on one and the ſame ſide thereof, by the 6. of the eleuenth he may be bridled: which will fore him to confeſſe his two perpendiculars to be alſo parallels. But by ſuppoſition agreed one, they concurre at one and the ſame poynt. which (by the definition of parallels) is impoſsible. Therefore our aduerſary muſt recant aud{printer-error-correction:and} yelde to our propoſition.
