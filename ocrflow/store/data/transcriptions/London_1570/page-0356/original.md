## ☞ The 18. Theoreme. The 18. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
If magnitudes deuided be proportionall: then alſo compoſed they ſhall be proportionall.
[/Subhead]

[Margin]
Demonſtration of proportion by compoſition.

This propoſition is the conuerſe of the former.

Demonſtratiõ leading to an impoſſibility.
[/Margin]

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=decorated|decoration="foliate pattern"}Vppoſe that the magnitudes deuided being proportionall, be AE, EB, CF, & FD, ſo that as AE is to EB, ſo let CF be to FD. Then I ſay, that compoſed alſo they ſhall be proportionall, that is, as AB is to BE, ſo is CD to DF. For if AB be not vnto BE, as CD is to FD, then ſhall AB be vnto BE, as CD is either vnto a magnitude leſſe then FD, or vnto a magnitude greater. Let it firſt be vnto a leſſe, namely, to DG.

[Diagram: two vertical lines labelled A, E, B and C, F, G, D]

And foraſmuch as, as AB is to BE, ſo is CD to DG: the compoſed magnitudes therefore are proportionall, wherefore deuided alſo they ſhall be proportionall (by yᵉ 17. of the fyrſt). Wherfore as AE is to EB, ſo is CG to GD. But by ſuppoſition as AE is to EB, ſo is CF to FD. Wherefore (by the 11. of the fift) as CG is to GD, ſo is CF to FD. Now then there are foure magnitudes, CG, GD, CF, and FD: of which the firſt CG is greater then the third CF: Wherefore (by the 14. of the fift) the ſecond GD is greater then the fourth FD. But it is alſo put to be leſſe then it: which is impoſſible. Wherefore it can not be that as AB is to BE, ſo is CD to a magnitude leſſe then FD. In like ſort may we proue, that it can not be ſo to a magnitude greater then FD. For by the ſame order of demonſtration, it would follow that FD is greater then the ſayd greater magnitude: which is impoſſible. Wherefore it muſt be to the ſelfe ſame. If therefore magnitudes deuided be proportionall, then alſo compoſed they ſhall be proportionall: which was required to be proued.

## ☞ The 19. Theoreme. The 19. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
If the whole be to the whole, as the part taken away is to the part taken away: then ſhall the reſidue be vnto the reſidue, as the whole is to the whole.
[/Subhead]
