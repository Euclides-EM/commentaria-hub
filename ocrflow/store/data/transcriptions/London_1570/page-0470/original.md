## The 3. Probleme. The 4. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
Proportions in the leaſt numbers how many ſoeuer beyng geuen, to finde out the leaſt numbers in continuall proportion in the ſaid proportions geuẽ.
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:S|lines=4|style=decorated|decoration="foliate letter in a square frame"}Vppoſe that the proportions in the leaſt numbers geuen, be A to B, C to D, and E to F. It is required to finde out the leaſt numbers in continuall proportion, in the ſame proportion that A is to B, and that C is to D, and that E is to F. Take the leaſt number whom B and C do meaſure, and let the ſame be G. And how often B meaſureth G, ſo many times let A meaſure H. And how oftẽ C meaſureth G, ſo many times let D meaſure K. Now E either meaſureth K or meaſureth it not. Firſt let it meaſure it.

And how often E meaſureth K, ſo many times let F meaſure L. And foraſmuch as how often A meaſureth H, ſo many times doth B meaſure G: therfore by the 17. of the ſeuenth, as A is to B, ſo is H to G. And by the ſame reaſon as C is to D, ſo is G to K, and moreouer as E is to F, ſo is K to L. Wherefore theſe numbers H, G, K, L, are in continuall proportion, and in the ſame proportion that A is to B, and that C is to D, and moreouer that E is to F.
