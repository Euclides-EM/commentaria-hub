ſegmentes of the ſphere be the one that wherein is A B C, whoſe toppe is B: and the other let be that wherein is A E C and his toppe, let be E : I ſay that a cone which hath his baſe the circle about A C, & heith a line which to B F (the heith of the ſegment, whoſe toppe is B,) hath that proportion that a line compoſed of D E, the ſemidiameter of the ſphere, and E F (the heith of the other remayning ſegment, whoſe toppe is E) hath to E F, (the heith of that other ſegment remayning), is equall to the ſegment of the ſphere K, whoſe toppe is B. To make this cone, take my eaſy order thus. Frame your worke for the finding of the fourth proportionall line: by making E F the firſt: and a line compoſed of D E and E F, the ſecond: and the third, let be B F: then by the 12. of the ſixth, let the fourth proportionall line be found: which let be F G: vpon F the center of the baſe of the ſegment, whoſe toppe is B, erect a line perpendicular equall to F G found: and drawe the lines G A and G C: and ſo make perfect the cone G A C. I ſay, that the cone G A C, is equall to the ſegment (of the ſphere K) whoſe toppe is B. In like maner, for the other ſegmēt whoſe toppe is E, to finde the heith due for a cone equall to it : by the order of the Theoreme you muſt thus frame your lines: let the firſt be B F: the ſecond D B and B F, compoſed in one right line, and the third muſt be E F: where by the 12. of the ſixth, finding the fourth, it ſhall be the heith, to rere vpon the baſe, (the circle about A C,) to make an vpright cone, equall to the ſegment, whoſe toppe is E.

## Logiſtically.

The Logiſticall finding hereof is moſt eaſy: the diameter of the ſphere being geuen, and the portions of the diameter in the ſegmentes conteyned (or axes of the ſegmentes) being knowne. Then order your numbers in the rule of proportion, as I here haue made moſt playne, in ordring of the lines: for the ſought heith will be the producte.

## A Corollary. 1.

Hereby, and other the premiſes it is euident that to any ſegment of a Sphere, whoſe whole diameter is knowne and the Axe of the ſegment geuen, An vpright cone may be made equall: or in any proportion, betwene two right lines aſſigned: and therefore alſo a cylinder may to the ſayd ſegment of the Sphere, be made equall, or in any proportion geuen, betwene two right lines.

## A Corollary. 2.

Manifeſtly alſo, of the former theoreme, it may be inferred that a Sphere, and his diameter being deuided, by one and the ſame playne ſuperficies, to which the ſayd diameter is perpendicular : the two ſegmentes of the Sphere, are one to the other in that proportion, in which a rectangle parallelipipedon hauing for his baſe the ſquare of the greater part of the diameter, and his heith a line compoſed of the leſſe portion of the diameter, and the ſemidiameter: to the rectangle parallelipipedon, hauing for his baſe the ſquare of the leſſe portion of the diameter, & his heith a line compoſed of the ſemidiameter & the greater part of the diameter.

## A Theoreme. 8.

Euery Sphere, to the cube, made of his diameter, is (in maner) as 11. to 21.
