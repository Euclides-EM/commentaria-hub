I will geue you an other example. Suppoſe, you haue two thinges, as *C*, and *D*: and of *C*, the Heate to be in the 4. degree: and of *D*, the Colde, to be remiſſe, euen vnto the *Temperament*. Now, for *C*, you take 4: and for *D*, you take a Ciphre: which, added vnto 4, yeldeth onely 4. The middle, or halfe, whereof, is 2. Wherefore the *Forme reſulting* of *C*, and *D*, is Hote in the ſecond degree: for, 2. degrees, accounted from *C*, toward *D*, ende iuſte in the 2. degree of heate.
