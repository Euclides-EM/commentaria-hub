## ¶ The 9. Probleme. The 29. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
Vpon a right line geuen to apply a parallelogramme equall vnto a rectiline figure geuen, and exceeding in figure by a parallelogramme like vnto a parallelogramme geuen.
[/Subhead]
