Seuenth definition.
[/Margin]

A right lyne is ſayd to be coapted or applied in a circle, when the extremes or endes therof, fall vppon the circumference of the circle.

As the line BC is ſayd to be coapted or to be applied to the circle ABC, for that both his extremes fall vpon the circumference of the circle in the pointes B and C. Likewiſe the line DE. This definition is very neceſſary, and is properly to be taken of any lyne geuen to be coapted and applied into a circle, ſo that it exceede not the diameter of the circle geuen.
