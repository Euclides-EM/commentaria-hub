## The 22. Theoreme. The 25. Propoſition.

If a Parallelipipedõ be cutte of a playne ſuperficies beyng a parallel to the two oppoſite playne ſuperficieces of the ſame body: then, as the baſe is to the baſe, ſo is the one ſolide to the other ſolide.

Thẽ I ſay that as the baſe A E F W is to the baſe E H C F, ſo is the ſolide A B F V to the ſolide E G C D. And foraſmuch as theſe right lines, L K, K A, and A E are equall the one to the other, therfore theſe parallelogrammes L O, K W, and A F, are alſo (by the firſt of the ſixt) equall the one to the other: and ſo alſo (by the ſame) are theſe parallelogrammes K X, K B, and A G equall the one to the other. And by the ſame reaſon alſo the parallelogrammes E C, H Z, and M S are equall the one to the other, and the parallelogrammes H G, H I, and I N are equal the one to the other.
