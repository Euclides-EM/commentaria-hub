## ¶ A Corollary added by Fluſſas.

[Subhead]
Vnto a plaine ſuperficies being geuen, to drawe by a point geuen without it, a parallel plaine ſuperficies.
[/Subhead]

Suppoſe as in the former deſcription that the ſuperficies geué be A B C, & let the point geuẽ without it be G. Now then by the point G drawe (by the 31. of the firſt) vnto the lines A B and B C parallel lines G H and H K. And the ſuperficies extended by the lines G H and G K ſhall be parallel vnto the ſuperficies A B C, by this 15. Propoſition.
