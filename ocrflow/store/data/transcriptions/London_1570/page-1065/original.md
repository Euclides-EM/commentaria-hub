The ſide therfore of a Dodecahedron is to the ſide of an Icoſahedron inſcribed in it, as the leſſe ſegment of the perpendicular of the pentagon, is to that line which is drawne from the centre to the ſide of the ſame pentagon.

## ¶ The 24. Propoſition.

[Subhead]
If halfe of the ſide of an Icoſahedron be deuided by an extreme & meane proportion: and if the leſſe ſegment thereof be taken away from the whole ſide, and againe from the reſidue be taken away the third part: that which remaineth ſhall be equall to the ſide of the Dodecahedron inſcribed in the ſame Icoſahedron.
