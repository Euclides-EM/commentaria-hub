Latin interpreters have named “rationale”. By what reasoning
led, I do not know: to me indeed it is not much approved. Let there-
fore ἄρτι be any straight line, about which the discourse ought to be set forth.
Etymology, the reasoning in the setting forth of things to be signified
by simple words, as the ancients seem to have pursued with great diligence:
so I judge it not to be despised by us, that we may aid
the knowledge of the things themselves. The derivation of this word ἄρτι
seems to me to be drawn ἀπὸ
τῆς ἐξόδου whence τὰ ἄρτια καὶ ἔξερτα. ἔστι, I interpret that
which is speakable, certain, conceded, and determinate:
and as if, for example, you should say that to be, which is sayable, and
can be signified by a word. Therefore ἄρτι will be any line,
of whatever magnitude is proposed. Therefore it is named ἄρτι,
because we can divide a given line into as
many parts as we shall wish. For it is known from the ninth
proposition of the sixth book. From a given line to take away a like part.
