So far Tartalea. Nor indeed is this nothing,
and yet everything is not in it. But I would not hesitate
to affirm this, that from here began those beginnings of ours, which
have poured such dense darkness upon the truth of the things handed down
in these books, that teachers and learners for the most part all
have gone different ways: so that when they proceeded further, they
understood nothing more than that they understood nothing: our
judgment indeed about that matter, when there shall be need, we will set forth.

And the square which is described from the proposed line,
which we wished to be called ἐντὴν, let it be called ἐντον.
