31 IF an odd number is prime to some number:
it will also be prime to its double.
32 OF numbers from a binary of doubles, each one
is only evenly even.
33 IF a number has an odd half: it is only evenly odd.
34 IF an even number is neither a double of a binary, nor
has an odd half: it is evenly even, and evenly odd.
35 IF there are any numbers whatsoever continually proportional,
and there are subtracted from the second and the last equals to the
first: then as the excess of the second to the first, so the excess of the
last to all those preceding it.
36 IF from unity any numbers whatsoever are set out continually
in double proportion, until the whole compounded becomes prime;
and this whole multiplied into the last makes some number: the result
will be Perfect.

1 WITH two unequal magnitudes proposed,
if from the greater there be taken more than the half; and from that
which remains, again there be taken more than the half; and this be
done continually: there will at length be left some magnitude which
will be less than the proposed lesser magnitude.
2 IF with two unequal magnitudes proposed,
the lesser be always subtracted from the greater, by some alternate
subtraction, and the remainder does not measure the preceding:
the magnitudes themselves will be incommensurable.
