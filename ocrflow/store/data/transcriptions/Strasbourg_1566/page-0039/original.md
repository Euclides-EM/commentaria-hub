[Figure: three æquediſtant horizontal lines αβ, εζ, γδ crossed by a transversal through points η, θ, κ]

Sit linea recta αβ, æquediſtans lineæ rectæ εζ. Item ſit linea recta γδ, æquediſtans lineæ rectæ εζ. ὁ διορισμός. Dico quod linea recta αβ, æquediſtat lineæ rectæ γδ. ἡ κατασκευή. Incidat in prædictas lineas, recta quædam linea ηκ.

[Subhead]

Primus. Linea recta incidens in lineas æquediſtantes: facit angulos alternos inter ſe æquales. Linea recta αβ, æquediſtat lineæ rectæ εζ. Et incidit in eas linea recta ηκ.
