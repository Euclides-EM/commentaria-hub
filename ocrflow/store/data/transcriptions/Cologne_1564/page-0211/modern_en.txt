TERMS.

Commensurable magnitudes are said (to be those) which, being measured by the same
measure, are measured.

DEFINITIONS.

I

Commensurable magnitudes are called those which the same measure measures.

β

Incommensurable, (are those) of which it is not possible that any common measure
should come to be.
