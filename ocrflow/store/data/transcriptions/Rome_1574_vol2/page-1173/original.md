Deinde ducta DC, perpendiculari ad AB, connectantur rectæ AC, BC. Ad interuallum rectæ EF, quæ ipſi BC, ſit æqualis, circulus deſcribatur ex centro E; in quo pentagonum æquilaterum inſcribatur FGHIK.
