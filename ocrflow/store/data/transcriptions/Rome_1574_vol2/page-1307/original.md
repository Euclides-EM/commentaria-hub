## THEOR. 14. PROPOS. 14.

LATVS Octaedri ſeſquialterum eſt lateris ſibi inſcriptæ Pyramidis.

SIT una ſex pyramidum Octaedri ABCDE, cuius baſis quadratum ABCD, triangula autem ad uerticem E, ſint ABE, EBC, CED, DEA, ſintque horum triangulorum centra F, G, H, I, quæ rectis iungantur FG, GH, HI, IF, quadratum cubi in Octaedro deſcripti conſtituentibus, ut docuimus in propoſ. 4. lib. 15. Quoniam uero Pyramis in cubo deſcripta, deſcribitur quoque in Octaedro, cui cubus imponitur, ut in ſcholio propoſ. ultimæ lib. 15. expoſuimus; Eſtque latus pyramidis in cubo deſcriptæ diameter baſis cubi, ut conſtat ex propoſ. 1. lib. 15. Erit ducta diameter G I, latus Pyramidis in Octaedro dicto deſcriptæ. Dico igitur latus octaedri BC, ſeſquialterum eſſe lateris Pyramidis G I. Ductis enim rectis E G K, EIL, diuidentur latera AB, CD, bifariam in K, L, ex ſcholio propoſ. 12. lib. 13. ipſæque EK, EL, ſeſquialterę erunt rectarũ EG, EI, per coroll. propoſ. 18.
