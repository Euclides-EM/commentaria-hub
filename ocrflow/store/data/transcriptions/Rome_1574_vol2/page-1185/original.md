## COROLLARIVM I.

CVM ergo demonſtratum ſit; recta LN, extrema ac media ratione ſecta, maius ſegmentum eſſe rectam RS: Sit autem LN, æqualis lateri cubi, & RS, lateri Dodecaedri; Perſpicuum eſt, ſi latus cubi ſecetur extrema ac media ratione, maius ſegmētum eſſe latus Dodecaedri in eadem ſphæra deſcripti.
