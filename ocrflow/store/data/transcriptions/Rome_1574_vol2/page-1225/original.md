SEQVITVR ex demonſtratione huius propoſitionis, omne triangulum æquilaterum, cuius latus fuerit Rationale, eſſe ſuperficiem Mediam. Nam ex eo, quod latera B C, E F, & ob id eorum dimidia C H, F I, Rationalia ſunt oſtenſa, demonſtratum eſt, tam rectas C H, B H, quã F I, E I, eſſe Rationales potentia tantum commenſurabiles; Ac propterea rectangula ſub C H, B H, & ſub F I, E I, hoc eſt, triangula B C D, E F G, ipſis æqualia, eſſe Media.
