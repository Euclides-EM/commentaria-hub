If four numbers are proportional; by permuting
they will also be proportional.

Let the numbers B, C, D, F be proportional, that is to say,
that there is the same proportion of B to
C as of D to F. I say that by per-
muting, there is the same proportion of
B to D as of C to F. Let the line
G, taken of whatever magnitude one
wishes, be divided into as many equal parts
as there are units in B, and let
the three other lines H, I, L be
taken each with as many equal parts
as those of G as there are units
in the three numbers C, D, F, so that
each magnitude represents each number. And the four
magnitudes G, H, I, L will be in the same proportion as the four
numbers B, C, D, F. For each of them is composed of as many
equal parts as the ones to the others, as the numbers are
composed of units, by the construction; and therefore G will con-
tain H as many times as B contains C; or else G will
be the same part, or the same parts of H, as B of C;
or else G will contain H as many times, and besides the same
part or the same parts of H, as B will contain C, and besides
the part or the parts of C, since there cannot be
other proportions than those between numbers by 15.
