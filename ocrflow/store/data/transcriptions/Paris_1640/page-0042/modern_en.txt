14. Square number, is a number produced by the multiplication
of a number by itself. Or else it is a plane number
whose sides are equal. And the number which produces it by its
multiplication is called its side or its square root. It is
also defined, equally equal number.

When the sides of the plane number are equal, the plane number
is called a square number, because the units being dis-
posed in length and in breadth seem to represent a superficial square,
