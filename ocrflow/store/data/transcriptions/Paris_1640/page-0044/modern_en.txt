18. One number measures another number, by that of the multi-
plication of which it produces the measured number.

We have said upon the 7th definition, that a number mea-
sures a number, when it is contained in it so many times.
Now this so many times will be expressed by the units of an
other number; and this number is that by which the number
measuring measures the number measured. Whence it follows that the
measuring [number] multiplying that by which it measures, must produ-
ce the measured number. In this way, because 3. is contained
9. times in 45. it measures 45. by 9. and therefore 3. being mul-
tiplied by 9. produces 45.
