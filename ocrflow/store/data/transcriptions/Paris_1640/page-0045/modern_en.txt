AXIOMS.

1. UNITY measures a number by the unities of which it is
composed.
   This is certain, since unity taken as many times as there
are unities in a number makes the number.

2. Every number measures itself by unity.
   That is to say that each number is once itself.

3. If two numbers by their multiplication produce a 3rd,
each of them measures this product by the other.
   Because 4 and 8 produce 32: 8 measures 32 by 4, since
it is as many times in 32 as there are unities in 4. And by
the same reason 4 measures 32 by 8.

4. If a number measuring another number multiplies that by
which it measures it, it will produce the measured number.
   Thus because 2 measures 8 by 4, that is to say, that it is con-
tained 4 times in 8; multiplying 4 it will produce 8.

5. If a number measures other numbers, it will measure the com-
posite of the same numbers.
   This principle is known by natural light: for if the number
A measures the two numbers BG, CD, it will mea-
sure also the total BD, which is composed of them,
since it will be contained in it as many times as in
the two separately.
