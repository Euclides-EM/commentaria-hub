Moreover one can well know, by these same definitions;

12.A number multiplies another number, when the multi-
plied is repeated as many times, as there are unities in the multi-
plier, & that from this repetition there is produced a 3rd.
In this way the number 4. being repeated as many times, as there
are unities in 6. that is to say, 6. times, & producing by this repe-
tition, the number 24. is multiplied by the number 6. And therefore,
The multiplication of one number by another, is the in-
vention of a number, which contains as many times one of the
proposed, as there are unities in the other.
As in this example, 24. contains as many times 4. as there
are unities in 6. One can also next define division,
which is the contrary of multiplication, in this way.
The division of one number by another, is the invention
of a 3rd number, which is contained as many times in the greater
of the proposed, that is to say, in the dividend, which must be
considered the greater, as there are unities in the other.
For if one divides 20. by 5. there will come 4. which is as many times
contained in 20. as there are unities in 5.
