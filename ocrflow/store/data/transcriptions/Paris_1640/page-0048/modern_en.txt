One can in the same way demonstrate in numbers, all
the propositions that have been demonstrated in magnitudes in the 2nd
& 5th books, where the numbers can be squared, by taking
always the magnitudes that express the proposed numbers:
which Euclid & some of his interpreters have well
recognized, since in the 15th 9th & in the Lemma of the 29th 10th which
are arithmetical propositions, they make use of the propositions
of the second book, although they have been demonstrated only in
magnitudes, & not in numbers.

And for this reason we will also cite all the propo-
sitions, which have been demonstrated in magnitudes in the six first
books, when we shall have need of them: in the same way as
if they were demonstrated in numbers.
