17. Similar plane numbers are those whose sides are pro-
portional.

    Having conceived what a plane number is, & what proportional
numbers are, it is easy to understand this defini-
tion, & what similar plane numbers are. For since
the plane number is produced by the multiplication of two numbers,
which are its sides: if the proportion of the same sides is
found again in two other numbers; & if these latter two
are multiplied one by the other, the two products will be similar
plane numbers, because of the proportion of the two sides of the one,
equal to the proportion of the two sides of the other. Thus 2. 3. by
multiplying the one by the other, produce 6. And because 4. 6.
are in the same proportion as 2. 3. if one multiplies 4. by 6.
their product 24. will be a plane number, similar to 6. the first
product.
