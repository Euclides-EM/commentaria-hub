8.  And the square, which is made from the
aforementioned straight line, let it be called
rational.
For just as that given line was called rational,
so also the square made from it must be called
rational.

9.  And those commensurable with this indeed,
[rational], are called rational. Thus also all plane
surfaces commensurable with the square, being
rational, are to be called rational, not by position
but by comparison.

10.  But those incommensurable with this,
let them be called irrational. Also whatever
surfaces are called irrational, whose lines are
incommensurable with a rational line.
