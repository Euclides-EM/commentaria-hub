TENTH BOOK.
ON PROPORTIONAL LINES.

I.

The proportion of lines depends on two things,
parallels and angles, and thus it could not be
treated well until after the explanation of the
one and the other. And even in order to
understand well the whole mystery of it, it is
necessary to take up again many things about
parallels which we shall propose in the form
of Lemmas.

FIRST LEMMA.  DEFINITION.
II.
A space bounded on one side between two parallels
and undefined on the other, is called a parallel space.

SECOND LEMMA.  DEFINITION.
III.
Since in these spaces one considers only the distance
between the parallels, their magnitude depends on this
distance which is measured by the perpendiculars
contained between these parallels, which we shall call
for this reason the perpendiculars of the spaces.
And from this it follows that these spaces are equal
when the perpendiculars of the one are equal to the
perpendiculars of the other.
