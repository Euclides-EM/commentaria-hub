                    Eighth Lemma.
IX.  IN the same parallel space, or in equal parallel
spaces, all the equally inclined are equal,
and all the equal are equally inclined. VIII.54.
     And conversely the parallel spaces are equal when
the equally inclined in them are equal. For from that it is
certain that the perpendiculars are so also. VIII. 56.

                    Ninth Lemma.
X.   WHEN one same line is cut by several
lines all parallel, all the portions of this
cut line are equally inclined between the parallels which
enclose them. VIII. 57.

                    Tenth Lemma.
XI.  WHEN there is proportion between four lines, one says
that two of these lines are proportional to the two other
lines when the two antecedents of the proportion are found
in the first two, and the two consequents
in the last two. From which it follows also that, Alter-
nando, one can also take the first two for the
two terms of one ratio, and the last two for the
two terms of the other.
