## PROP. XVII.

[Diagram: numbers arranged in rows: A, 3. / B, 2. C. 4. / AB, 6. AC, 12.]

*Si numerus* A *duos numeros* B, C *multiplicans fecerit aliquos* AB, AC; *geniti ex ipſis eandem rationem habebunt, quam multiplicati.* ( AB. AC : : B. C. )

Nam quia AB = A in B, ᵃ erit 1 toties, in

a 15. def. 7.
[/Margin]

A,quoties B in AB. ᵃ item quia AC = A in C. erit 1 toties in A, quoties C in AC. ergò quoties B in AB, toties C in AC. quare B. AB : :

[Margin]
b 20. def. 7.
[/Margin]

C. AC. ᶜ ergò viciſſim, B. C : : AB. AC.

c 13. 7.

## PROP. XVIII.

[Diagram: numbers arranged in two columns: C, 5. A, 3. AC, 15. / C, 5. B, 9. BC, 45.]

*Si duo numeri* A, B, *numerum quempiam* C *multiplicantes fecerint aliquos* AC, BC; *geniti ex ipſis eandem rationem habebunt, quam multiplicantes.* (A. B : : AC. BC.)

[Margin]
a 16. 7.
[/Margin]

Nam AC ᵃ = CA; & BC ᵃ = CB; ſic idem C multiplicans A, & B producit AC, & BC.

[Margin]
b 17. 7.
[/Margin]

ᵇ ergò A. B : : AC. BC. Q. E. D.

### *Schol.*

Ex his pendet modus vulgaris reducendi fractiones ( 3/5, 7/9 ) ad eandem denominationem. Nam duc 9 tam in 3, quàm in 5, proveniunt 27/45 = 3/5 quoniam ex his, 3. 5 : : 27. 45. item duc 5 in 7, & 9, prodeunt 35/45 = 7/9. quia 7. 9 : : 35. 45.

## PROP. XIX.

[Diagram: numbers arranged in rows: A, 4. B, 6. C, 8. D, 12. / AD, 48. BC, 48.]

*Si quatuor numeri proportionales fuerint,* ( A B : : C. D) ; *qui ex primo & quarto fit numerus* AD, *æqualis eſt ei, qui ex ſecundo & tertio fit, numero* BC.
