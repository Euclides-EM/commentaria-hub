# LIB. XIV.

## Prop. I.

[Diagram: circle ABC with chord BC, points E, F, G, D on the vertical diameter to A, lines drawn from C to G and D]

{dropcap:Q|lines=6|style=decorated|decoration="square frame with foliate scrollwork"}uæ ex D centro circuli cujuſpiã ABC in pentagoni eidem circulo inſcripti latus BC ducitur perpendicularis DF, dimidia eſt utriuſque lineæ ſimul, & lateris hexagoni DE, & lateris decagoni EC eidem circulo ABC inſcripti.

[Margin]
a 4. 1. b 5. 1. c 32. 1. d hyp. & 33. 6. e 20. 3. f 7. ax. g 6. 1.
[/Margin]

Sume FG = FE, & duc CG. ᵃ Eſtque CE = CG. ergò ang. CGE ᵇ = CEG ᵇ = ECD. ergò ang. ECG ᶜ = EDC ᵈ = ¼ ADC ᵉ = ½ CED (½ ECD). proinde ang. GCD = ECG = EDC. ᵍ quare DG = GC (CE). ergò DF = CE (DG) + EF = DE + CE. / 2 Q. E. D.

## Prop. II.

[Diagram: two parallel lines, A G B C above and D H E F below]

*Si binæ rectæ lineæ* AB, DE *extremâ ac mediâ ratione ſecentur* (AB. AG :: AG. GB. & DE. DH :: DH. HE) *ipſæ ſimiliter ſecabuntur, in eaſdem ſcilicet proportiones.* (AG. GB :: DH. HE.)

[Margin]
a 17. 6. b 8. 2. c 2. ax. 1. d 22. 5. & 22. 6.
[/Margin]

Accipe BC = BG; & EF = EH. Eſtque AB × BG ᵃ = AGq. quare ACq ᵇ = 4 ABG + AGq ᶜ = 5 AGq. Similiter erit DFq = 5 DHq. ᵈ ergò AC. AG :: DF. DH, componendo igitur AC + AG. AG :: DF + DH.
