## PROP. XLI.

[Diagram: straight line labelled A, C, B, with C dividing it]

*Si duæ rectæ lineæ* AC, CB *potentiâ incommenſurabiles componantur; quæ faciant compoſitum quidem ex ipſarum quadratis medium, quod autem ſub ipſis continetur, rationale; tota recta linea* AB *irrationalis erit: vocetur autem rationale ac medium potens.*

Nam 2 rectang. ACB, ᵃ ρ̊ν ᵇ ⊓⊔ ACq + CBq ᶜ μν. ᵈ ergò 2 ACB ᵈ ⊓⊔ ABq. quare ᵉ AB eſt ρ̊. Q. E. D.

## PROP. XLII.

[Diagram: straight line labelled G, H, K beside a rectangle E, D, F, A, C, B divided by a vertical line DC]

*Si duæ rectæ lineæ* GH, HK *potentiâ incommenſurabiles componantur, quæ faciant & compoſitum ex ipſarum quadratis medium, & quod ſub ipſis continetur medium, incommenſurabiléq; compoſito ex quadratis ipſarum; tota recta linea* GK *irrationalis erit: vocetur autem bina media potens.*

Ad expoſitam FB ρ̊, fiant rectang. AF = GKq, & CF = GHq + HKq. Quoniam GHq + HKq (CF) ᵃ eſt μν; latitudo CB ᵇ erit ρ̊. Item quia 2 rectang. GHK (ᶜ AD) ᵃ eſt μν, etiam AC ᵇ erit ρ̊. Porrò quia rectang. AD ᵃ ⊓⊔ CF, ᵈ atque AD. CF : : AC. CB, ᵉ erit AC ⊓⊔ CB. ᶠ Quare AB eſt ρ̊. ergò ᵍ rectang. AF, id eſt, GKq eſt ρ̊ν. ʰ proinde GK eſt ρ̊. Q. E. D.
