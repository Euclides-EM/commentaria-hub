## PROP. VII.

[Diagram: pentagon labelled A, B, C, D, E with diagonals BE, AC, BD, and CE, intersecting at F]

*Si pentagoni æquilateri* ABCDE *tres anguli, ſive qui deinceps* EAB, ABC, BCD, *ſive* EAB, BCD, CDE *qui non deinceps ſint, æquales fuerint, æquiangulum erit ipſum pentagonum* ABCDE.

Paribus deinceps angulis ſubtendantur rectæ BE, AC, BD.

Quoniam latera EA, AB, BC, CD, angulíque incluſi ᵃ

a *hyp.*
[/Margin]

[/Margin]
