### *Coroll.*

Hinc, ſi ſimilia proportionalia ſimilibus proportionalibus addantur, tota erunt proportionalia.

## PROP. XIII.

[Diagram: twelve horizontal lines in three columns labelled G, A, B, K; H, C, D, L; I, E, F, M]

*Si prima* A *ad ſecundam* B *eandem habuerit rationem, quàm tertia* C *ad quartam* D; *tertia verò* C *ad quartam* D *majorem habuerit rationem; quàm quinta* E *ad ſextam* F; *prima quoque* A *ad ſecundam* B *majorem rationem habebit, quàm quinta* E *ad ſextam* F.

Sume ipſarum A, C, E æquemultiplices G, H, I: ipſarúmque B, D, F æquemultiplices K, L, M. Quia A.B :: C.D; Si H ⊏ L, ᵃ erit

[Margin]
a 6. def 5.
[/Margin]

G ⊏ K. Sed quia C/D ⊏ E/F, ᵇ fieri poteſt ut ſit

[Margin]
b 8. def. 5.
[/Margin]

H ⊏ L, & I non ⊏ M. ergò fieri poteſt ut G ⊏ K, & I non ⊏ M. ᶜ ergò A/B ⊏ E/F. Q.E.D.

[Margin]
c 8. def. 5.

### *SCHOL.*

Quòd ſi C/D ⊐ E/F, erit quoq; A/B ⊐ E/F. Item ſi A/B ⊏ C/D ⊏ E/F. erit A/B ⊏ E/F. & ſi A/B ⊐ C/D ⊐ E/F erit A/B ⊐ E/F.
