## Prop. XVI.

[Diagram: two parallel vertical planes AB and CD cut by a plane HEIGF, with triangle apex I above and points E, C, G, H, B, F, D]

*Si duo plana parallela* AB, CD *plano quopiam* HEIGF *ſecentur, communes illorum ſectiones* EH, GF *ſunt parallelæ.*

Nam ſi dicantur non eſſe parallelæ, cùm ſint in eodem plano ſecanti, convenient alicubi, puta in I. quare cùm totæ HEI, FGI ᵃ

[Margin]
a 1. 11
[/Margin]

ſint in planis AB, CD productis, etiam hæc convenient, contra hypoth.

## Prop. XVII.

[Diagram: three parallel planes EF, GH, IK shown as stacked rectangles, cut by lines ALB and CMD, with points A, C, F, E, L, M, H, G, B, D, K, I]

*Si duæ rectæ lineæ* ALB, CMD *parallelis planis* EF, GH, IK *ſecentur, in eaſdem rationes ſecabuntur* (AL. LB :: CM. MD).

Ducantur in planis EF, IK rectæ AC, BD. item AD occurrens plano GH in N; junganturque NL, NM. Plana triangulorum ADC, ADB faciunt ſectiones BD, LN; & AC, NM ᵃ

[Margin]
a 16. 11.
[/Margin]

parallelas. ergò AL. LB ᵇ :: AN. ND ᵇ :: CM. MD. Q. E. D.

[Margin]
b 2. 6.
[/Margin]
