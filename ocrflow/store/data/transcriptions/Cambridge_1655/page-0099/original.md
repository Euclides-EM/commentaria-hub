## PROP. IV. *Probl.* 4.

[Diagram: triangle ABC with inscribed circle of centre D, touching the sides at E, F and G; lines BD, CD bisecting the angles at B and C, and perpendiculars DE, DF, DG]

*In dato triangulo* ABC *circulum* EFG *inſcribere.*

Duos angulos B, & Cᵃ

[Margin]

[/Margin]

biſeca rectis BD, CD coeuntibus in D. Ex Dᵇ

[Margin]
b 12. 1.

duc perpendiculares DE, DF, DG. circulus centro D per E deſcriptus tranſibit per G; & F, tangétque tria latera trianguli.

Nam ang. DBEᶜ

[Margin]
c *conſtr.*
[/Margin]

=DBF; & ang. DEBᵈ

[Margin]

[/Margin]

=DFB; & latus DB commune eſt, ᵉ
