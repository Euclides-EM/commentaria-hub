## PROP. IV.

[Diagram: dotted lines labelled A 6, B 7, B 18, B 9.]

*Omnis numerus* A, *omnis numeri* B, *minor majoris, aut pars eſt, aut partes.*

Si A, & B primi ſint inter ſe, ᵃ erit

[Margin]
a 4. def. 7.
[/Margin]

A tot partes numeri B, quot ſunt in A unitates. (ut 6=⁶⁄₇ 7.) Sin A metiatur B, ᵇ liquet

[Margin]
b 3. def. 7.
[/Margin]

A eſſe partem ipſius B. (ut 6=⅓ 18.) denique ſi A, & B aliter compoſiti inter ſe fuerint, ᶜ maxima

[Margin]
c 4. def. 7.
[/Margin]

communis menſura determinabit, quot partes A conficiat ipſius B; ut 6=⅔ 9.

## PROP. V.

[Diagram: dotted lines labelled A 6, D 4, and B G C 12 with segments 6 6, E H F 8 with segments 4 4]

*Si numerus* A *numeri* BC *pars fuerit, & alter* D *alterius* EF *eadem pars; & ſimul uterque* (A+D) *utriusque ſimul* (BC+EF) *eadem pars erit, quæ unus* A *unius* BC.

Nam ſi BC in ſuas partes BG, GC ipſi A æquales; atque EF in ſuas partes FH, HF ipſi D æquales reſolvantur; ᵃ erit

[Margin]
a hyp.
[/Margin]

numerus partium in BC æqualis numero partium in EF. Quum igitur A+D ᵇ=BG+EH=GC+HF, erit

[Margin]
b conſt. & 2. ax. 1.
[/Margin]

A+D toties in BC+EF, quoties A in BC. Q. E. D.

Vel ſic breviùs. Sit a=x/2 & b=y/2. ᶜ ergò

[Margin]
c 2. ax. 1.
[/Margin]
