## Prop. VII.

[Diagram: parallel lines AB and CD with points E and F, joined by straight line EF and curved line EGF]

*Si duæ ſint parallelæ rectæ lineæ* AB, CD, *in quarum utraque ſumpta ſint quælibet puncta* E, F; *illa linea* EF, *quæ ad hæc puncta adjungitur, in eodem eſt cum parallelis plano* ABCD.
