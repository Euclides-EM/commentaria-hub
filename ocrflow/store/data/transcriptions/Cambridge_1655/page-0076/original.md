## Prop. VIII.

[Diagram: circle with centre K and diameter BI; point A outside the circle; lines from A to L, B, C, D, E on the convex side and to F, G, H, I on the concave side; lines from K to the points on the circumference]

*Si extra circulum ſumatur punctum quodpiam* A, *ab eóque puncto ad circulum deducantur quædam lineæ* AI, AH, AG, AF, *quarum una quidem* AI *per centrum* K *protendatur, reliquæ verò ut libet; in cavam peripheriam cadentium rectarum linearum maxima quidem eſt illa* AI, *quæ per centrum ducetur, aliarum autem ei quæ per centrum tranſit propinquior* AH *remotiore* AG *ſemper major eſt. In convexam verò peripheriam cadentium rectarum linearum minima quidem eſt illa* AB, *quæ inter punctum* A, *&* *diametrum* BI *interponitur; aliarum autem ea, quæ eſt minimæ propinquior* AC *remotiore* AD *ſemper minor eſt. Duæ autem tantùm rectæ lineæ* AC, AL *æquales ab eō puncto in ipſum circulum cadunt, ad utraſque partes minimæ* AB, *vel maximæ* AI.
