## PROP. IV. *Probl.* 4.

[Diagram: triangle ABC with inscribed circle centre D touching the sides at E, F, G; lines BD, CD bisecting angles B and C, perpendiculars DE, DF, DG; labels A, G, E, D, B, F, C]

*In dato triangulo* ABC *circulum* EFG *inſcribere.*

Duos angulos B, & C ᵃ

[Margin]
a 9. 1.
[/Margin]

biſeca rectis BD, CD coeuntibus in D. Ex D ᵇ

[Margin]
b 12. 1.
[/Margin]

duc perpendiculares DE, DF, DG. circulus centro D per E deſcriptus tranſibit per G, & F, tangétque tria latera trianguli.

Nam ang. DBE ᶜ

[Margin]
c *conſtr.*
[/Margin]

=DBF; & ang. DEB ᵈ

[Margin]
d 12. *ax.*
[/Margin]

=DFB; & latus DB commune eſt, ᵉ

[Margin]
e 26. 1.
[/Margin]

ergò DE=DF. Simili argumento DG=DF. circulus igitur centro D deſcriptus tranſit per E, F, G; & cùm anguli ad E, F, G ſint recti, tangit omnia trianguli latera. Q.E.F.

### *Scholium.*

Hinc, *cognitis lateribus trianguli, inveniuntur*

[Margin]
*Petr. Herig.*
[/Margin]

*eorum ſegmenta, quæ fiunt à contactibus circuli inſcripti.* Sic.

Sit AB 12, AC 18, BC 16. Erit AB+BC=28. ex quo ſubduc 18=AC=AE+FC, remanet 10=BE+BF. ergò BE, vel BF=5. proinde FC, vel CG=11. quare GA, vel AE=7.
