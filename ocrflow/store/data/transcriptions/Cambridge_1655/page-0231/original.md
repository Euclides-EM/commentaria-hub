## PROP. XXIX.

[Diagram: numbers listed as A, 3. B, 5. AB, 15. with a line over AB]

*Si impar numerus A, imparem numerum B multiplicans, fecerit aliquem AB; factus AB impar erit.*

Nam AB ᵃ

[Margin]
a 15. def. 7.
[/Margin]

componitur ex B impari numero toties accepto, quoties unitas includitur in A etiam impari. ᵇ

[Margin]
b 23. 9.
[/Margin]

ergò AB eſt impar. Q. E. D.

### *Scholium.*

[Diagram: numbers B, 12 over A, 3, with (C, 4.]

*1. Numerus A impar numerum B parem metiens, per numerum parem C eum metitur.*

Nam ſi C impar dicatur, quoniam ᵃ

[Margin]
a 9. ax. 7.
[/Margin]

B=AC, ᵇ

[Margin]
b 29. 9.
[/Margin]

erit B impar; contra Hypoth.

[Diagram: numbers B, 15 over A, 3, with (C, 5.]

*2. Numerus A impar numerum B imparem metiens, per numerum C imparem eum metitur.*

Nam ſi C dicatur par; ᵃ

[Margin]
a 28. 9.
[/Margin]

erit AC, vel B par, contra Hypoth.

[Diagram: numbers B, 15 over A, 3, with (C, 5.]

*3. Omnis numerus (A & C) metiens imparem numerum B eſt impar.*

Nam ſi utervis A, vel C dicatur par, ᵃ

[Margin]
a 28. 9.
[/Margin]

erit B numerus par, contra Hypoth.
