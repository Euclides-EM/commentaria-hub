## PROP. XXXIX.

[Diagram: numbers: A, 12. / B, 4, C, 3.]

*Si numerum* A *quiſpiam numerus* B *metiatur, ille* A *quem* B *metitur, partem habebit* C, *à metiente* B *denominatam.*

Nam quia A/B ᵃ

[Margin]
a hyp.
[/Margin]

= C, ᵇ

[Margin]
b 9. ax. 7.
[/Margin]

erit A = BC. ᶜ

[Margin]
c 7. ax. 7.
[/Margin]

ergò A/C = B. Q. E. D.

## PROP. XL.

[Diagram: numbers: A, 15. / B, 3. C, 5.]

*Si numerus* A *partem habuerit quamlibet* B, *metietur illum numerus* C, *à quo ipſa pars* B *denominatur.*

Nam quia BC ᵃ

[Margin]
a hyp. & 9. ax. 7.
[/Margin]

= A, ᵇ

[Margin]
b 7. ax. 7.
[/Margin]

erit A=B/C. Q.E.D.

## PROP. XLI.

[Diagram: numbers: ½ G, 12. / ⅓ H--- / ¼]

*Numerum reperire* G, *qui minimus cùm ſit, habeat datas partes,* ½, ⅓, ¼.

ᵃ

[Margin]
a 38. 7.
[/Margin]

Inveniatur G minimus, quem denominatores 2, 3, 4 metiuntur. ᵇ

[Margin]
b 39. 7.
[/Margin]

Liquet G habere partes, ½, ⅓, ¼. Si fieri poteſt H ⊐ G habeat eaſdem partes; ᶜ

[Margin]
c 40. 7.
[/Margin]

ergò 2, 3, 4 metiuntur H, & proinde G non eſt minimus, quem 2, 3, 4 metiuntur, contra conſtr.
