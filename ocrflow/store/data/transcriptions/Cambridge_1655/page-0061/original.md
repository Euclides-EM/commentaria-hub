### *Schol.*

Propoſitiones decem primæ hujus libri valent etiam in numeris. Reliquas quilibet tyro examinet. pro hac, ſit AF 6, & AB 12, ſectus in A D 5, DE 3, & EB 4. Eſtque 6×12 (AG) =72. 6×5 (AH) =30. 6 in 3 (DI) =18. denique 6×4 (E G) =24. Liquet verò 30+18+24=72.

## PROP. II.

[Diagram: arc labelled Z over a horizontal line divided into segments A and E, with a separate line labelled B below]

*Si recta linea* Z *secta ſit utcunque; rectangula, quæ ſub tota* Z, *& quolibet ſegmentorum* A, E *comprehenduntur, æqualia ſunt ei, quod à tota* Z *fit, quadrato.*

Dico Z A+Z E=Zq. Nam ſume B=Z.
