## PROP. XXV.

[Diagram: numbers and line: A. 9. B. 4. / C 3. D. -]

*Si duo numeri* A, B *primi inter ſe fuerint, qui unum eorum* A *metitur numerus* C, *ad reliquum* B *primus erit.*

Nam ſi affirmes aliquem D numeros B, & C metiri, ᵃ

[Margin]
a 11. ax. 7.
[/Margin]

ergò D metiens C, metitur A. ergò A & B non ſunt primi inter ſe, contra Hypoth.

## PROP. XXVI.

[Diagram: numbers and lines: A, 5. C, 8. / B, 3. / AB, 15. E ---- / F ----]

## PROP. XXVII.

[Diagram: numbers: A, 4. B, 5. / Aq, 16. / D, 4.]
