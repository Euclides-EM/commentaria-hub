## PROP. XLIII.

[Diagram: semicircle on diameter AB with an inner semicircle on CD; points A, C, F, E, D, B on the base line; line from F to G on inner arc, perpendicular from E to G, perpendicular from D to L on outer arc]

Si fieri poteſt, binomium AB alibi in E ſecetur in alia nomina AE, EB. Liquet AB ſecari utrobique inæqualiter, quia AD ⊓⊔ DB, & AE ⊓⊔ EB.
