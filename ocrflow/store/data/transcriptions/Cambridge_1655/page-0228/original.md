## PROP. XIX.

[Diagram: numbers: A, 8. B, 12. C, 18. D, 27. BC, 216.]

*Tribus numeris datis A, B, C, conſiderare an poſſit ipſis quartus proportionalis D inveniri.*

Si A metiatur BC per aliquem D, ᵃ

[Margin]
a 9. ax. 7.
[/Margin]

ergò AD=BC; ᵇ

[Margin]
b ex 19.
[/Margin]

conſtat igitur eſſe A. B :: C. D. Q. E. F.

Sin A non metiatur BC, non datur quartus proportionalis, quod oſtendetur, prout in præcedenti.

## PROP. XX.

[Diagram: numbers: A, 2. B, 3. C, 5. D, 30. G ---]

*Primi numeri plures ſunt omni propoſitâ multitudine primorum numerorum A, B, C.*

ᵃ

[Margin]
a 38. 7.
[/Margin]

Sit D minimus, quem A, B, C metiuntur, ſi D+1 primus ſit, res patet; ſi compoſitus, ᵇ

[Margin]
b 33. 7.
[/Margin]

ergò aliquis primus, puta G, metitur D+1, qui non eſt aliquis trium A, B, C; nam ſi ità, quum is ᶜ

[Margin]
c ſuppoſ.
[/Margin]

totum D+1, & ᵈ

[Margin]
d conſtr.
[/Margin]

ablatum D metiatur, ᵉ

[Margin]
e 12. ax. 7.
[/Margin]

idem reliquam unitatem metietur. Q. E. A. Ergò propoſitorum primorum numerorum multitudo aucta eſt per D+1; vel ſaltem per G.

## PROP. XXI.

[Diagram: dotted line divided at A, E, B, F, C, G, D, with numbers 5, 5, 3, 3, 2, 2 above the segments and 20 after D]

*Si pares numeri quotcunque AB, BC, CD componantur, totus AD par erit.*

ᵃ

[Margin]
a 6. def. 7.
[/Margin]

Sume EB=½ AB, & FC=½ BC; & GD=½ CD. ᵇ

[Margin]
b 12. 7.
[/Margin]

liquet EB+FC+GD=½ AD. ᶜ

[Margin]
c 6. def. 7.
[/Margin]

ergò AD eſt par numerus. Q. E. D.
