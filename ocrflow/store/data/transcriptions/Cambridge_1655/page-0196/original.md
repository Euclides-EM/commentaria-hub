## PROP. XIV.

[Diagram: numbers arranged in two columns: A, 9. B, 6. C, 3. / D, 6. E, 4. F, 2.]

*Si ſint quotcunque numeri* A,B,C, *& alii totidem* D,E,F *illis æquales multitudine, qui bini ſumantur, & in eadem ratione* (A.B : : D. E. & B. C : : E. F) *etiam ex æqualitate in eadem ratione erunt.* (A. C : : D. F).

Nam quia A.B : : D. E, ᵃ erit viciſſim, A.D : :

[Margin]
a 13. 7.
[/Margin]

B. E : : ᵃ C. F. ᵃ ergò iterum permutando A.C : : D. F. Q.E.D.

## PROP. XV.

[Diagram: 1 . D.. / B ... 3. E ......6.]

*Si unitas numerum quempiam* B *metiatur; æquè autem alter numerus* D *alterum quendam numerum* E *metiatur; & viciſſim æquè unitas tertium numerum* D *metietur, & ſecundus* B *quartum* E.

Nam quia 1 eſt eadem pars ipſius B, quæ D ipſius E, ᵃ erit viciſſim 1 eadem pars ipſius D,

[Margin]
a 9. 7.
[/Margin]

quæ B ipſius E. Q. E. D.

## PROP. XVI.

[Diagram: numbers arranged in two columns: P, 4. A, 3. AB, 12. / A, 3. B, 4. BA, 12.]

*Si duo numeri* A, B *ſeſe mutuò multiplicantes fecerint aliquos* AB, BA, *geniti ex ipſis* AB, BA *æquales inter ſe erunt.*

Nam quia AB = A in B, ᵃ erit 1 in A toties

[Margin]
a 15. def. 7.
[/Margin]

quoties B in AB. ᵇ ergò viciſſim 1 in B toties

[Margin]
b 15. 7.
[/Margin]

erit, quoties A in AB. atqui quoniam BA = B

[Margin]
c 4. ax. 7.
[/Margin]

in A, ᵃ erit 1 in B toties, quoties A in BA. ergò quoties 1 in AB, toties 1 in BA; & ᶜ proinde AB = BA. Q. E. D.
