XXXI. Solida figura in ſolida figura dicitur inſcribi, quando omnes anguli figuræ inſcriptæ conſtituuntur vel in angulis, vel in lateribus, vel denique in planis figuræ, cui inſcribitur.

XXXII. Solida figura ſolidæ figuræ viciſſim circumſcribi dicitur, quando vel anguli, vel latera, vel denique plana figuræ circumſcriptæ tangunt omnes angulos figuræ, circum quam deſcribitur.

## Prop. I.

[Diagram: plane figure with lines labelled A, B, C, D, E, F; line AC in the plane and CB rising above it]

*Rectæ lineæ pars quædam* AC *non eſt in ſubjecto plano, quædam verò* CB *in ſublimi.*

Producatur AC in ſubjecto plano uſque ad F, vis CB eſſe in directum ipſi AC; ergò duæ rectæ AB, AF habent commune ſegmentum AC. ª Q. F. N.

[Diagram: two intersecting lines meeting at E, with a triangle labelled D, B, F, G, E]
