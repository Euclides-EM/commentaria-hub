## PROP. XXIV.

[Diagram: parallelogram ABCD with diagonal AC; lines through I parallel to the sides form parallelograms EG and HF about the diagonal; labels A, D, E, F, G, I, B, H, C]

*In omni parallelogrammo* ABCD, *quæ circa diametrum* AC *ſunt parallelogramma* EG, HF, *& toti & inter ſe ſunt ſimilia.*

Nam parallelogramma EG, HF habent ſingula unum angulum cum toto communem. ᵃ

[Margin]
a 29. 1.
[/Margin]

ergò toti & ſibi mutuò æquiangula ſunt. ᵃ Item tam triangula ABC, AEI, IHC, quàm triangula ADC, AGI, IFC ſunt inter ſe æquiangula. ᵇ

[Margin]
b 4. 6.
[/Margin]

ergò AE. EI :: AB. BC, ᵇ atque AE. AI :: AB. AC; ᵇ& AI. AG :: AC. AD. ᶜ

[Margin]
c 22. 5.
[/Margin]

ex æquali igitur, AE. AG :: AB. AD. ᵈ

[Margin]
d 1. def. 6.
[/Margin]

ergò Pgra. EG, BD ſimilia ſunt. eodem modo HF, BD ſimilia ſunt, ergò, &c.
