# LIB. VI.

[Subhead]
*Definitiones.*
[/Subhead]

[Diagram: two triangles ABC and DCE standing on a common straight line BCE, sharing vertex C]

I. {dropcap:S|lines=6|style=decorated|decoration="letter S in a square frame surrounded by scrollwork"}Imiles figuræ rectilineæ ſunt (ABC, DCE), quæ & angulos ſingulos ſingulis æquales habent; atque etiam latera, quæ circum angulos æquales, proportionalia.

*Ang.* B = DCE *&* AB. BC : : DC. CE. *item ang.* A = D; *atque* BA. AC : : CD. DE. *denique ang.* ACB = E. *atque* BC. CA : : CE. ED.

[Diagram: two parallelograms BD and BF meeting at B, with points D, C, H on the top line, A, B, G on the middle line, and E, F at the bottom]

II. Reciprocæ autem ſunt (BD, BF), cùm in utraque figura antecedentes, & conſequentes rationum termini fuerint. (hoc eſt AB. BG : : EB. BC.)

[Diagram: straight line AB divided at point C]

III. Secundùm extremam & mediam rationem recta linea AB ſecta, eſſe dicitur, cùm ut tota AB ad majus ſegmentum AC, ità majus ſegmentum AC ad minus CB ſe habuerit. (AB. AC : : AC. CB.)
