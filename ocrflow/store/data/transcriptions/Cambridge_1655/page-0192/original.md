## PROP. VI.

[Diagram: dotted lines labelled A G B 6 with segments 3 3, D H E 8 with segments 4 4, C 9, F 12]

*Si numerus* AB *numeri* C *partes fuerit; & alter* DE *alterius* F *eædem partes & ſimul uterq;* (AB+DE) *utriuſq; ſimul* (C+F) *eædem partes erit, quæ unus* AB *unius* C.

Divide AB in ſuas partes A G, G B; & DE in ſuas DH, HE. Partium in utroque AB, DE æqualis eſt multitudo, ex hypoth. Quum igitur AG ᵃ ſit eadem pars numeri C,

[Margin]
c 2. ax. 7.
[/Margin]

AB+DE eædem partes eſt ipſius C+F, quæ AB ipſius C. Q. E. D.
