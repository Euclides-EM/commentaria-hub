### Coroll.

Hinc, magnitudo metiens duas magnitudines, metitur & maximam earum menſuram communem.

## Prop. IV.

[Diagram: three horizontal lines; first labelled A; second labelled B, with D marked along it; third labelled C, with E and F marked along it]

*Tribus magnitudinibus commenſurabilibus datis* A, B, C; *maximam earum menſuram communem invenire.*

ᵃ Inveni D maximam communem menſuram

[Margin]
a 3. 10.
[/Margin]

duarum quarumcunque A, B, ᵃ item E ipſarum D, & C maximam communem menſuram; erit E quæſita.

Nam perſpicuum eſt E metiens D, & C ᵇ

[Margin]
b. *conſtr.* & 2. *ax.* 10.
[/Margin]

metiri tres A, B, C. Puta aliam F hâc majorem eaſdem metiri. ᶜ

[Margin]
c *cor.* 3. 10.
[/Margin]

ergò F metitur D; ᶜ proinde & E, ipſorum D, C maximam communem menſuram, major minorem. Q. E. A.

### Coroll.

Hinc quoque Magnitudo metiens tres magnitudines, metitur quoque maximam earum communem menſuram.
