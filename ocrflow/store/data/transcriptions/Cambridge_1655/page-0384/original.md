## Prop. V.

[Diagram: circle with points A, B, C, D on circumference, centre E, chords AD, BD, CD, BC, perpendiculars EF and EGC from E; separate vertical line H]

*Superficies dodecaedri ad ſuperficiem icoſaedri in eadem ſphæra deſcripti eandem proportionem habet, quam* H *latus cubi ad* AD *latus icoſaedri.*

Circulus ABCD ᵃ circumſcribat tam dodecaedri pentagonum, quàm icoſaedri triangulum; quorum latera BD, AD; ad quæ demittantur ex E centro perpendiculares EF, EGC. & connectatur CD.

[Margin]
a 3. 14.
[/Margin]

Quoniam EC + CD. EC ᵇ:: EC.CD.erit EG (ᶜ½ EC +CD). EF. (ᵈ½ EC) ᵉ:: EF. EG—EF (½ CD). atqui H. BD ᶠ:: BD.H—BD.ᵍ ergò H. BD :: EG. EF. proinde H×EF = BD×EG. quum igitur H. AD ʰ:: H×EF. AD×EF. erit H. AD :: BD×EG. AD×EF :: ˡ ſuperfic. dodecaedri ad ſuperfic. icoſaedri. Q. E. D.
