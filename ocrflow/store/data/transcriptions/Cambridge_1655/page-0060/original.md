# LIB. II.

## *Definitiones.*

[Diagram: rectangle with corners B (top left), C (top right), D (bottom right), A (bottom left)]

I. {dropcap:O|lines=5|style=decorated|decoration="O within a square foliate border with a central rosette"}Mne parallelogrammum rectangulum ABCD contineri dicitur ſub rectis duabus AB, AD, quæ rectum comprehendunt angulum.

*Quando igitur dicitur rectangulum ſub* BA, AD; *vel brevitatis causâ, rectangulum* BAD, *vel* BA×AD, (*vel* ZA *pro* Z×A) *deſignatur rectangulum, quod continetur ſub* BA, & AD *ad rectum angulum conſtitutis.*
