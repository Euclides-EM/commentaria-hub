## Prop. III.

[Diagram: pentagon ABCDE inscribed in a circle with diameter AG, points F and H on it, lines AC, CG; triangle LMN inscribed in a second circle with centre R; line I K below, and line O Q P beneath it]

*Idem circulus* ABD *comprehendit & Dodecaedri pentagonum* ABCDE, *& Icoſaedri triangulum* LMN, *eidem ſphæræ inſcriptorum.*

Duc diametrum AG, rectáſque AC, CG. Sítque IK diameter ſphæræ, ᵃ & IKq = 5 OPq. ᵇ fiátque OP. OQ :: OQ. QP. Quia ACq + CGq ᶜ = AGq ᵈ = 4 FGq; & ABq ᵉ = FGq + CGq. ᶠ erit ACq + ABq = 5 FGq. porrò, quia CA. AB ᵍ :: AB. CA − AB; ac OP. OQ :: OQ. QP. ʰ ideóque CA. OP :: AB. OQ. ᵏ erit 3 ACq (ˡ IKq). 5 OPq (ᵐ IKq) :: 3 ABq. 5 OQq. ergò 3 ABq = 5 OQq. Verùm ob ML ⁿ latus pentagoni circulo inſcripti, cujus radius OP, erunt 15 RMq ᵒ = 5 MLq ᵖ = 5 OPq + 5 OQq =* 3 ACq + 3 ABq 𐞥 = 15 FGq. ʳ ergò RM = FG. ˢ proinde circ. ABD = circ. LMN. Q. E. D.
