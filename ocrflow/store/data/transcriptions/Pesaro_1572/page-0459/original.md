## F. C. COMMENTARIVS.

Idem etiam demonſtrabitur, ſi baſes inæqualibus numero lateribus contineantur. Sint enim pyramides æquealtæ ABCDL EFGHKM, ſitq́; pyramidis ABCDL baſis quadrilaterum ABCD, & vertex L; pyramidis vero EFGHKM baſis ſit pentagonum EFGHK, & vertex M.
