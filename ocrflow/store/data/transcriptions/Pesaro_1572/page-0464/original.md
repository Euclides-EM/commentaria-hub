## THEOREMA. II.

Prismata ſimilia, quæ multiangulas habent baſes in ſimilia prismata triangulares baſes habentia diuiduntur, numeroq́; æqualia, & homologa totis: & prisma ad prisma triplam proportionem habet eius, quam latus homologum habet ad homologum latus.

Sint duo prismata ſimilia, & ſimiliter poſita AK MV, & prismatis quidem AK baſis ſit pentagonum ABCDE, & ipſi oppoſitum FGHKL; prismatis vero MV baſis ſit pentagonum MNOPQ, & oppoſitum ipſi RSTVX: ſitq́; latus AB lateri MN homologum. Dico prismata AK MV diuidi in prismata, quæ triangulares baſes habent, ſimilia & numero æqualia, & homologa totis; & prisma AK ad prisma MV triplam proportionem habere eius, quam habet AB ad MN. Iungantur EB EC LG LH QN QO XS XT. pentagonum igitur ABCDE diuiſum erit in tria triangula ABE EBC ECD; pentagonumq́; FGHKL diuiſum in tria triangula FGL LGH LHK: & ſimiliter pentagonum MNOPQ diuiſum erit in tria triangula MNQ QNO QOP: & pentagonum RSTVX in totidem triangula RSX XST XTV: parallelis autem planis ductis per LG GB, perq́; LH HC, & per XS SN, & per XT TO. Quoniam igitur ſimilia polygona in ſimilia triangula diuiduntur, numeroq́; æqualia, & homologa totis; erunt triangula ABE FGL ſimilia triangulis MNQ RSX, & triangula EBC LGH triangulis QNO XST, triangulaq́; ECD LHK ipſis QOP XTV ſimilia.
