10. But the figures incommensurable with the

Thus as the lines incommens[urable] with the
proposed rational line are called irrational: so also
the plane surfaces incommens[urable] with the square of
the rational line are called irrational.

11. And the straight lines which can [produce]
those irrational figures, are to be called
irrational & surd; namely if they are
squares their sides are to be irratio-
nal, but if they are other rectilinear
[figures] whatsoever, the lines which can [produce]

He calls the lines which can [produce] spaces
incommensurable with the square of the ra-
tional line, irrational: so that if these
irrational spaces are squares their sides will be
called irrational; if they are not squares, but are
other rectilinear [figures], the straight lines which de-
scribe squares equal to those incomm-
ens[urable] spaces will be called irrational: for a straight
line is said to be able [to produce] a figure, when the
square described on that line is equal to that figure.
