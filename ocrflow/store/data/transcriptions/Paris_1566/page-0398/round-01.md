## MONITVM.

Hactenus quæ ab Euclide tradidit Ypſicles diximus, quinque ſcilicet problemata eouſque ſuccincta vt obſcuriora euaſerint, quorum ideo demonſtrationes variare cogimur, maximè huius quintæ præcipuam partem à Campano relictam, ab Ypſicle verò nõ plenè oſtenſam, ſcilicet conſtructum pentagonum in eodem degere plano, præcipuum inquam ſolidis conſtruendis probandum, eorum facies planas eſſe. Præterea quæ demonſtrat Ypſicles ea ſæpius arte tradit qua effectum proponens cauſam recludit, ac ſi ab quodam auditum receperit effectum qui cauſam ignoraret, non tamen ideo Ypſiclem magni nominis virum id ignoraſſe dicemus, ſed forſan quadam incuria aut temporum iniuria veluti ſi ignoraſſet ad vos perueniſſe. ## Propoſitio ſexta. Problema 6.

In dato Octahedro, Pyramidem trilateram æquilateram figurare.

Eſto propoſitum octahedrum ABGDEI, cui inſcribenda ſit pyramis, ſumantur octahedri quatuor baſes, hoc eſt tres infimã EGD ambientes, hæ quidem AEG BED & IGD, quarta inſuper infimè oppoſita AIB, earum autem centra HCNL, ſuper triangulo HCN conſtruatur pyramis HCNL, quoniam baſes octahedri AGE ABI concidunt in rectas EG BI, quæ opponuntur in quadrato GEBI octahedri, mittantur à ſigno A per baſium centra HL perpendiculares AHF ALK eas ſecantes in F & K bifariam, per corollarium duodecimæ decimitertij. Recta igitur FK ſectiones iungens parallela erit & æqualis octahedri lateribus EB GI, per trigeſimamtertiam primi, rectáque HL ſecans latera AF AK æqualia proportione (namque AH & AL ex centris circulorum æqualium prodeunt) parallela erit rectæ FK, per ſecũdam ſexti, necnõ octahedri EB GI lateribus, per nonam vndecimi. Erit ideo AF ad AH vt FK ad HL, per quartam ſexti, ſunt enim ſimilia AFK & AHL triangula, per corollarium ſecundæ ſexti, ſed AF ſeſquialtera eſt rectæ AH, nam latus EG efficit HF dimidiam rectæ AH, per corollarium duodecimæ decimitertij, rectæ igitur HL ſeſquialterum erit FK vel GI latus octahedri. Hac arte reliquarum HN NC CL LN CH rectarum pyramidem HNCL componentium, latera octahedri ſeſquialtera eſſe docebimus, & proinde rectas æquales eſſe, ac ideo ex ipſis deſcripta triangula HCN HNL NCL & CHL pyramidem HNCL componentia, æqualia & æquilatera eſſe, quæ cùm angulos HCNL in centris baſium octahedri terminet, eidem octahedro inſcripta erit, per vigeſimamſextam diffinitionem vndecimi. ## Corollarium.

Pyramidis octahedro inſcriptæ baſes, baſibus octahedri ſunt parallelæ. Cùm enim latera baſis pyramidis ſeſe tangentia, lateribus octahedri ſeſe tangentibus ſint parallela, vt fuit HL lateri GI, parallela ſunt per ipſis plana, per decimamquintam vndecimi.

## Corollarium ſecundum.

Recta coniungens centra oppoſitarum octahedri baſium, ſeſquialtera eſt perpendicularis ab angulo inſcriptæ pyramidis in baſim. Cùm enim pyramis & eam continens cubus ad eadem ſigna terminent angulos, ex prima huius, vtraque eodem claudentur octahedro per quartam huius.
