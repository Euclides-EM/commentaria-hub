Dodecahedrum autem eiuſdem cubi duplum fuiſſe, dempto tertio minoris ſegmenti, ac inſuper minori ſegmento minoris dimidij reliqui, ſiue huius exceſſus, corollar. 33 huius patuit. Atqui pyramis eiuſdem cubi ſibi inſcripti nocuplum complexa eſt, per trigeſimam huius. Dodecahedrum itaque pyramidi inſcriptum bis continens eundem cubum, dempto eodem tertio minoris ſegmẽti, ac inſuper minori ſegmento minoris dimidij reliqui, ſibi ſumet, ſiue bipartietur nonas ſolidi pyramidis (quarum ſingulæ præſcripto cubo ſunt æquales) dempto hoc eodem exceſſu. Dodecahedri igitur ſolidum pyramidis ſibi circunſcriptæ ſolidi, &c.

## Propoſitio trigeſimaſexta.

Octahedrum excedit ſibi inſcriptum icoſahedrum ſolido parallelpipedo quod ſuper potentia lateris Icoſahedri vertice autem ea quæ ſemidimetientis Octahedri eſt maius ſegmentum.

Eſto octahedrũ A B C F P L, cui icoſahedrum inſcribamus H K E G M X N V D S Q T, per 16 decimiquinti, ductis dimetiẽtibus A Z R C B R O I F, & perpendiculari K O, parallelæ rectæ A Z R: Dico A B C F P L octahedrum inſcripto icoſahedro maius eſſe, parallelepipedo quod ſuper potentia lateris H K vel G E, vertice autem recta K O vel R Z maiori ſemidimetiẽtis A R ſegmento cõtinetur. Quoniam oſtenſum eſt eadem 16, triangula K D G & K E Q in baſibus octahedri A P F A L F deſcripta eſſe. Quare circa angulum ſolidum F remanent ſuper baſi F E G tria triãgula K E G K E F K F G, pyramidem K E F G concludentia, cui eadem cauſa æqualis & ſimilis erit, oppoſita ſuper eadem baſi F E G pyramis in E F G. Haud ſecus totidem ſupererunt ſingulis octahedri ſolidis angulis, ſcilicet binæ pyramides ſuper baſi A H K, binæ item ſuper B N V, binæq́. ſuper D P S, ac binæ ſuper Q L T, æquales & ſimiles, ex diffinit. 8 vndecimi. Duodecim itaque fient pyramides, ſuper baſi comprehenſa latere icoſahedri & binis minoribus ſegmentis lateris octahedri angulum rectum continentibus, vt G E F. Cùm autem latus G E angulum rectum ſubtendens, duplum poßit (ex 47 primi) ſingularum E F F G ſicq́ H K laterum A H A K, ſingulæq́. A H A K vel E F F G duplum poßint ſingularum A Z vel Z K angulum rectum continentium, hoc eſt, trianguli ſiue baſis A H K. Sequetur latus G E vel H K quadruplum poſſe trianguli E F G vel A H K. Pyramis autem K E F G, baſim E F G in plano octahedri F L B P habens, ſuſcipit verticem perpendicularem K O, per 4 diffinit. ſexti, quæ quidem eſt maius ſegmentum ſemidimetientis octahedri, per 16 decimiquinti, tres itaque pyramides ſub eodem vertice & æquis baſibus, priſmati quod ſuper eiſdem baſi & vertice conſtruitur erunt æquales, per 1 coroll. 7 duodecimi, quatuor igitur priſmata ſuper quadrupla baſi ipſi G E F (quæ æquatur potentiæ lateris G E) vertice verò K O, (vel R Z maiori ſegmento ſibi æquali) comprehendent ſolidum duodecim pyramidibus æquum. Quæ quidem duodecim pyramides componunt exceſſum octahedri in icoſahedrum ſibi inſcriptum. Octahedrum itaque excedit ſibi inſcriptum icoſahedrum ſolido, &c.
