## Corollarium primum.

Dimetiens Sphæræ dodecahedrum ambientis bina poteſt latera, ſcilicet dodecahedri & dodecahedrum ambientis cubi. Nam in priori figura ducta ab O centro recta O B ad angulum dodecahedri, binas poteſt O B dimidium cubi latus, & V B dimidium dodecahedri, per 47 primi. Dupla itaque rectæ O B dimetiens quidem ſphæræ dodecahedrum ambientis, duplas reliquarum O V V B poterit, per decimamquintam quinti, quæ ſunt cubi & dodecahedri latera propoſita.

## Corollarium ſecundum.

Cubi latus extrema & media ratione ſectum efficit minus ſegmentum latus dodecahedri ſibi inſcripti. Maius vero ſegmentum latus cubi eidem dodecahedro inſcripti. nam patuit latus dodecahedri eſſe maius ſegmentum lateris trianguli B E, latus verò B E (binis G B S F æquum) maius fuiſſe lateris G F cubi ſegmentũ, quæ quidem B E (ſubtendens angulum pentagoni) fuit (octaua huius) latus cubi dodecahedro inſcripti.

## Corollarium tertium.

Cubi latus, dodecahedri inſcripti & circunſcripti lateribus eſt æquale. Patuit enim hoc theoremate latus cubi efficere minus ſegmentum dodecahedri inſcripti latus, nempe vt prima figura G F ad S B, idem verò cubi latus patuit (decimaſeptima decimi tertij) angulum pentagoni dodecahedri circũſcripti ſubtendere, ac proinde efficere dodecahedri ſiue pentagoni latus maius ſegmentum, per primum corollarium eiuſdem, binis igitur eſt æquale.
