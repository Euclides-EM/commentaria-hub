## Propoſitio ſecunda.

Si duæ rectę lineę ſe ad inuicem ſecuerint, in vno ſunt plano, & omne triangulum in vno eſt plano.

Idem patebit, ſi obiiciatur D I B in ſublimi eſſe. Si igitur duæ rectæ lineæ ſe adinuicem ſecuerint, &c.*

## Propoſitio tertia.

Si bina plana ſe inuicem ſecuerint, communis eorum ſectio recta eſt linea.

[Figure: two intersecting quadrilateral planes with points labelled B at top, G at lower left, A and D at bottom]

*Bina plana A B G B ſe ſecent in linea B D: Dico B D eſſe rectam: quod ſi non credatur, cùm ſigna B & D in vtroque ſint A B & G B plano, ex hypotheſi, per ea ſigna duci binæ rectæ in vtroque plano poterunt, per ſeptimã diffinitionẽ primi: quæ (à tota diſiunctiua) aut vnicam aut binas efficiẽt rectas, ſiquidem vnicam rectam, ea communis ſectio planorum recta eſt, vt petimus. Si itaque bina plana ſe inuicem &c.*

## Propoſitio quarta.

Si recta linea duabus rectis ſe inuicem diſpeſcentibus, in communi ſectione ad angulos rectos ſteterit, & ad earundem planum ad angulos rectos erit.
