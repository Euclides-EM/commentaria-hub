## Propoſitio decimanona. Problema 19.

In data trilatera æquilatera Pyramede, Icoſahedrum deſcribere.

[Figure: triangle ABG containing an inscribed icosahedron with lettered points C E F H I K L M N O P Q R S T V X Y]

Eſto propoſita pyramis ABGD, cuius ſingula bifariam ſecẽtur latera ſignis FMKLPN, deſcriptis triangulis LFP PMN NKL et FMK in ſingulis eius pyramidis baſibus, quæ quidem triangula æqualitatera erũt, per quartã primi: nã latera ſubtendunt æquos baſium pyramidis angulos dimidiis eius lateribus comprehenſos, quæ ideo ſunt æqualia. Secentur ea latera extrema & media ratione (per trigeſimam ſexti) in ſignis C E Q R S T H I O N Y X. Ipſa latera in eaſdẽ rationes ſecantur, per ſecundam decimæ quarti, & ideo æquales efficiũt ſimiles ſectiones, per ſecũdam partẽ nonæ quinti: Dico præfata ſigna angulos ſuſcipere icoſahedri pyramidi A B G D inſcripti, fiant in præfatis triangulis alia rurſus triangula iunctis ſectionibus, Sint autem hæc T R S IOH CEQ & VXY, ipſa æquilatera erunt. Singula nanque eorum latera æquales angulos triangulorum æquilaterorum ſubtendunt, & æquis lateribus (maiori ſcilicet & minori ſegmẽto) comprehenſos, quæ ideo (per quartam primi) erunt æqualia. Oſtendamus igitur ad aliquod præfatorum ſignorum (ſcilicet T) conſtitui angulum ſolidum icoſahedri. Cùm triangula T R S & T Q O ſint æquilatera & æqualia æquales erunt quatuor rectæ T R T S T Q T O: & quia quadratum eſt F P N K, bifariam ſecans pyramidem A B G D per corollarium ſecundæ huius. Recta T H duplum poteſt rectæ T N vel N H, per quadrageſimamſeptimam primi, minoris quidem ſegmenti ex conſtructione. Quod idem duplum poteſt recta R T vel T S, angulum trianguli ſubtendens binis ſegmentis comprehenſum, per corollarium decimæ ſextæ huius. Rectæ igitur T H T S T R T Q T O æquales erunt, necnõ H S S R R Q Q O O H, angulos qui ad T ſubtendentes: nam Q R bina poteſt P Q P R minora ſegmenta, quæ potuit T H. Reliquæ verò angulos triãgulorũ æquilaterorũ maiori & minori ſectione comprehenſos ſubtendunt. Quinque itaque triãgula T R S T S H T H O T O Q T Q R æquilatera & æqualia, angulum ſolidũ icoſahedri ad ſignum T conſtituunt, per 16 decimitertij, in latere P N trianguli P N M. Eadem methodo in reliquis quatuor triãgulorũ P N M N K L F M K & L F P baſibus pyramidis inſcriptorũ lateribus, quæ latera duodecim ſunt numero, duodecim icoſahedri conſtituentur anguli, viginti triangulis æqualibus & æquilateris compoſiti, quorum quatuor in quatuor pyramidis baſibus, T R S H O I C E Q V X Y, quatuor ſub quatuor pyramidis angulis C I X T S H E R V & T Q O, & bini ſub ſingulis ſex pyramidis lateribus, ſcilicet ſub latere D G, T H S T H O, ſub D B, R Q E R Q T, ſub D A, C O Q C O I, ſub A B, E X C E X V, ſub B G, S V R S V Y, ſub A G, I Y H I Y X. Viginti itaque æquilateris & æqualibus triangulis comprehenſum, ſolidum icoſahedrum erit, per vigeſimamquartam diffinitionem vndecimi, pyramidi A B G D inſcriptum cùm eius ſimul anguli pyramidis ſimul baſes tangunt, per vigeſimamſextam diffinitionem vndecimi. In data itaque trilatera pyramide icoſahedrum deſcripſimus.
