## Corollarium.

Inſcriptæ ac eis circunſcriptæ regulares figuræ ſiue eas comprehendentes ſphæræ, idem centrum poſſident. Nempe earum pyramides quarum anguli baſium ſphæræ ſuperficiem tangunt, ab his angulis ad idem ſignum æquales perducunt rectas, conos pyramidum ibidem conſtituentes, ac proinde centrum ſpherarum in eiſdem conis collocãtes, cùm ab his ad curuam ſuperficiem, in baſium pyramidis angulis exiſtentem, ductæ rectæ fuerint æquales.

## MONITVM.

Horum ſolidorum vnicum octahedrum reliqua mutuò ſibi inuicem inſcripta ſuſcipit ſolida: nam octahedrum capit icoſahedrum ſibi inſcriptum: illud idem icoſahedrum concipit dodecahedrum eidem octahedro inſcriptum: Dodecahedrum verò idem, ſuſcipit cubum ipſo octahedro conceptum: cubus ille demùm Pyramidem propoſito octahedro inſcriptam circunſcribit. Hæc reliquis denegantur ſolidis.
