## Corollarium.

Dimetiens Icoſahedri binas poteſt, dimetientem inſcripti cubi quæ baſium oppoſitarum centra iungit, & dimetientem circuli baſim icoſahedri continentis. Patuit enim dimetientem B G poſſe rectam F H centra iungentem & duplam rectæ B H, hoc eſt dimetientem circuli baſim circa centrum H continentis.

## Propoſitio vigeſimaprima.

Dodecahedri latus minus ſegmentum eſt, eius quæ duplum poteſt lateris Octahedri, eidem Dodecahedro inſcripti.

Exponatur dodecahedrum A B G D C T, cuius vnum latus ſit A B. Ipſi dodecahedro inſcriptum octahedrum eſto E F L K I, cuius latus ſit E F: Dico A B latus dodecahedri eſſe minus ſegmentum cuiuſdam rectæ (extrema & media ratione ſectæ) quæ duplum poteſt lateris E F, octahedri dodecahedro inſcripti. Iungãtur octahedri dimetientes E L F K, ipſæ bifarias ſectiones oppoſitorũ dodecahedri laterũ A B & G D coniungunt (per nonam decimiquinti, & tertium corollarium decimæſeptimæ decimitertij) ſingulæque earum extrema & media ratione ſectæ, minus efficiunt ſegmentum latus dodecahedri, per quartum corollarium eiuſdẽ. ## Propoſitio vigeſimaſecunda.

Dimetiens Icoſahedri poteſt & ſuiipſius lateris ſeſquitertium, & lateris Icoſahedro inſcriptæ Pyramidis ſeſquialterum.

Cùm enim oſtẽſum ſit, à dimetiente icoſahedri ablato triplo lateris cubi ſibi inſcripti, relinqui ſeſquitertium lateris ipſius icoſahedri potentia, per vigeſimam huius. Sed tripla lateris cubi potẽtia eſt dimetiens ipſius cubi, per decimamquintam decimitertij, cubus autem & ſibi inſcripta pyramis eadem ſphæra concipiuntur, per primam huius, eodémque icoſahedro, per corollarium eiuſdem. Idem itaque diameter cubi ſiue ſphæræ cubum & pyramidem continentis, ſeſquialterum poterit lateris pyramidis, per decimamtertiam decimitertij. Sequetur itaque à dimetiẽte Icoſahedri ablata tripla potẽtia lateris cubi, ſiue ſeſquialtera lateris pyramidis, quæ ſunt eiuſdẽ dimetientis potentiæ, relinqui ſeſquitertium lateris ipſius Icoſahedri. Dimetiens igitur icoſahedri poteſt & ſuiipſius lateris ſeſquitertium, & lateris icoſahedro inſcriptæ pyramidis ſequialterum.

## Propoſitio vigeſimatertia.

Dodecahedri latus ad Icoſahedri ſibi inſcripti latus, ſe habet vt minus ſegmentum perpendicularis pentagoni, ad eam quæ ex centro in latus eiuſdem pentagoni.
