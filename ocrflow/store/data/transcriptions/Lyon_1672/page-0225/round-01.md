## PROPOSITION XVI.

Probleme.

[Subhead]
La façon d'inſcrire dans vn cercle, vn pentedecagone equilateral, & equiangle.
[/Subhead]

[Figure: circle inscribed with an equilateral triangle and a pentagon, points labelled A, D, H, I, C, B, F, G]
