## PROP. XXVIII. THEOR.

[Subhead]
In æqualibus circulis æquales rectæ lineæ circumferentias æquales auferunt, majorem quidem majori, minorem vero minori.
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:S|lines=3|style=plain}Int æquales circuli ABΓ, ΔEZ, & in ipſis æquales rectæ lineæ BΓ, EZ, quæ circumferentias quidem auferant majores BAΓ, EΔZ, minores vero BHΓ, EΘZ: dico circumferentiam BAΓ majorem majori circumferentiæ EΔZ, & minorem circumferentiam BHΓ minori EΘZ æquales eſſe.

Sumantur enim [per 1. 3.] centra circulorum K, Λ, junganturque BK, KΓ, EΛ, ΛZ.

[Diagram: two equal circles ABΓ and ΔEZ, with centres K and Λ and chords BΓ and EZ]

Quoniam autem circuli ſunt æquales, erunt & quæ ex centris æquales: duæ igitur BK, KΓ ſunt æquales duabus EΛ, ΛZ, & baſis BΓ æqualis eſt baſi EZ: ergo angulus BKΓ [per 8. 1.] angulo EΛZ eſt æqualis. æquales autem anguli [per 26. 3.] æqualibus inſiſtunt circumferentiis, quando ad centra fuerint: quare circumferentia BHΓ æqualis eſt circumferentiæ EΘZ. ſed & totus ABΓ circulus toti ΔEZ eſt æqualis: reliqua igitur circumferentia BAΓ reliquæ EΔZ æqualis erit.

Ergo in æqualibus circulis æquales rectæ lineæ circumferentias æquales auferunt, majorem quidem majori, minorem vero minori. quod erat demonſtrandum.
