First definition.

1 Those quantities shall be called communicating, or commensu-
rable, for which there will be a quantity numbering, commonly
those. And those for which there will not be a quantity numbering com-
monly those shall be called incommensurable.

The Translator.

E For example if there were the two lines a & b, & if there were found some
