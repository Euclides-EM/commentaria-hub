### XXII.

Axis autem Cylindri eſt quieſcens illa recta linea, circum quam parallelogrammum convertitur.

[Curated heading level=2 type=definitions: DEF. XXIII.]

### XXIII.

Baſes vero Cylindri ſunt circuli a duobus adverſis lateribus quae circumaguntur deſcripti.

[Curated heading level=2 type=definitions: DEF. XXIV.]

### XXIV.

Similes Coni et Cylindri ſunt, quorum axes et baſium diametri proportionales ſunt.

[Curated heading level=2 type=definitions: DEF. XXV.]

### XXV.

Cubus eſt figura ſolida ſex quadratis aequalibus contenta.

[Curated heading level=2 type=definitions: DEF. XXVI.]

### XXVI.

Tetraedrum eſt figura ſolida quatuor triangulis aequalibus et aequilateris contenta.

[Curated heading level=2 type=definitions: DEF. XXVII.]

### XXVII.

Octaedrum eſt figura ſolida octo triangulis aequalibus et aequilateris contenta.

[Curated heading level=2 type=definitions: DEF. XXVIII.]

### XXVIII.

Dodecaedrum eſt figura ſolida duodecim pentagonis aequalibus, et aequilateris et aequiangulis contenta.

[Curated heading level=2 type=definitions: DEF. XXIX.]

### XXIX.

Icoſaedrum eſt figura ſolida viginti triangulis aequalibus, et aequilateris contenta.

[Curated heading level=2 type=definitions: DEF. A.]

### DEF. A.

Parallelepipedum eſt figura ſolida ſex figuris quadrilateris, quarum quae ex adverſo parallelae ſunt, contenta.
