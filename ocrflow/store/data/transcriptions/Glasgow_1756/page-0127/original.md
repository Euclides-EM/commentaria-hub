### IV.

Figura rectilinea circa circulum dicitur circumſcribi, quando unumquodque latus circumſcriptae, circuli circumferentiam contingit.

[Diagram: square containing an inscribed circle]

[Curated heading level=2 type=definitions: DEF. V.]

### V.

Circulus ſimiliter in figura rectilinea inſcribi dicitur, quando circuli circumferentiam unumquodque latus ejus in qua inſcribitur contingit.

[Curated heading level=2 type=definitions: DEF. VI.]

### VI.

Circulus circa figuram rectilineam circumſcribi dicitur, quando circuli circumferentia unumquemque angulum ejus circa quam circumſcribitur tangit.

[Diagram: circle containing an inscribed square]

[Curated heading level=2 type=definitions: DEF. VII.]

### VII.

Recta linea in circulo aptari dicitur, quando ejus extrema in circuli circumferentia fuerint.
