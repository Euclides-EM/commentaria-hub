# EUCLIDIS ELEMENTORUM LIBER QUARTUS.

## DEFINITIONES.

[Curated heading level=2 type=definitions: DEF. I.]

### I.

{dropcap:F|lines=2|style=plain}IGURA rectilinea in figura rectilinea inſcribi dicitur, quando unuſquiſque figurae inſcriptae angulus, unumquodque latus ejus in qua inſcribitur tangit.

[Diagram: square containing an inscribed diamond]

[Curated heading level=2 type=definitions: DEF. II.]

### II.

Figura ſimiliter circa figuram circumſcribi dicitur, quando unumquodque latus circumſcriptae, unumquemque angulum ejus circa quam circumſcribitur tangit.

[Curated heading level=2 type=definitions: DEF. III.]

### III.

Figura rectilinea in circulo inſcribi dicitur, quando unuſquiſque inſcriptae figurae angulus circuli circumferentiam tangit.

[Diagram: circle containing an inscribed square]
