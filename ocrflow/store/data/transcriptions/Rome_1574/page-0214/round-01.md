## SCHOLION.

EX his infert Campanus ſequens hoc theorema.

LINEA recta ſecans duo trianguli latera bifariam, erit reliquo lateri parallela.

SECET linea DF, latera AB, AC, trianguli ABC bifariam in D, & E. Dico DE, parallelam eſſe lateri BC.
