## Problema 5, Propositio 5.

CIrca datum triangulum, circulum describere.

ORONTIVS. ¶Sit triangulum a/b/c: circa quod receptum sit describere circulum. Secentur itaq; bifariam, per decimam primi, a/b/& b/c/ ipsius dati trianguli latera: in punctis quidem d/& e. Ab ipsis deinde punctis d/& e, ad rectos excitētur angulos d/f/& e/f, per vndecimā ipsius primi. Aio primùm, rectas d/f/ & e/f/in directum productas, tandem conuenire. Cōnexa enim recta d/e, per primum postulatum: ea diuidet vtrunque rectū angulum b/d/f/& b/e/f. & proinde in rectas d/f/& e/f, recta incidēs d/e: efficiet/ad easdem partes interiores angulos, duobus rectis minores.
