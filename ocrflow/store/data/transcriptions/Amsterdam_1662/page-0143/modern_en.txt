Desire.

That one may multiply some magnitude so
    many times that the same may exceed
another of like nature.
    Example, that one may multiply 2 so many times,
that the same may exceed 20, for 2 times 2 is 9 with 3
comes 27 being: greater than 20.

Common notions.

1. If a magnitude measures as many magnitudes
as one desires, it shall also measure
the composite of the same.
    Example, 6 can measure (or divide)
12,18,24,30 then 6 shall also measure the
composite of the same as 155120.
2. A magnitude measuring another,
shall likewise measure all others
which the same measures.
