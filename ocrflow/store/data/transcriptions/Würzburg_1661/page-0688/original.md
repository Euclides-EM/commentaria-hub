### §. I.

[Subhead]
*De Additione, & Subtractione numerorum irrationalium compoſitorum.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:I|lines=2|style=plain}N additione & ſubtractione numerorum ſerventur

[Margin]
*Additio & Subtractio irrationalium compoſitorum*
[/Margin]

ea, quæ diximus §. 3. & 4. capitis præcedentis ſervanda eſſe in additione & ſubtractione irrationalium ſimplicium. Quoad ſigna verò ſerventur hæ duæ Regulæ, deſumptæ ex dictis par. 1. cap. 6. & 7.

Prima. *Eadem ſigna † aut -, ponunt idem ſignum † aut -; niſi in Subtractione, quando ſuperior major eſt quàm inferior: tunc enim ſubtrahitur ſuperior ab inferiore, & ex † fit -, ex - fit †.*

Secunda. *Diverſa ſigna mutant ſpeciem operationis, & loco Additionis fit Subtractio, loco Subtractionis Additio; & in Additione, factâ ſubtractione, ponitur ſignum majoris numeri; in Subtractione verò, factâ additione, ponitur ſignum ſuperioris, ſive major is ſit, ſive minor, ſive æqualis.*

### Exemplum Additionis.

[Calculation]
 6 † ℞ Q 18
 4 † ℞ Q  8
―――――――――――
10 † ℞ Q 50
[/Calculation]

In hoc Exemplo quoniam ℞ Q 18, & ℞ Q 8, ſunt ſimiles, inæquales, commenſurabiles; dividuntur per communem menſuram 2, fiunt quoti 4, & 9; quorum radices ſunt 2, & 3; harum ſumma eſt 5; quadratum ſummæ ſunt 25; hæc ducta in communem menſuram 2, faciunt ℞ Q 50.

### Exemplum Subtractionis.

[Calculation]
℞ Q 50 - 5
℞ Q  8 - 2
――――――――――
℞ Q 18 - 3
[/Calculation]

In hoc exemplo quoniam ℞ Q 50, & ℞ Q 8 ſunt ſimiles, inæquales, commenſurabiles per menſuram communem 2; accipiuntur quotorum 25, & 4, radices 2, & 5, minor ſubtrahitur à majori, reſtant 3; hæc quadrantur, fiunt 9; ducuntur in communem menſuram 2, fiunt 18.

### §. II.

[Subhead]
*De Multiplicatione numerorum irrationalium compoſitorum.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=plain}Erventur illa, quæ diximus §. 2. de Multiplicatione

[Margin]
*Multiplicatio irrationalium compoſitorum.*
[/Margin]

ſimplicium; quoad ſigna verò obſervetur hæc Regula, ex cap. 8. partis 3. deſumpta: *Eadem ſigna ponunt †, Diverſa -.* Sive ergo plus in plus, ſive minus in minus multiplicetur, ſemper producitur plus: & ſive plus in minus, ſive minus in plus ducatur, ſemper procreatur minus.

### Exemplum.

[Calculation]
 18 † ℞ Q 54
  6 - ℞ Q 24
―――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――
108 † ℞ Q1944 - ℞ Q7776 - ℞ Q1296.
[/Calculation]

In hoc exemplo, ducta ſunt primò 6 in 18, & producta 108. Secundò ducta ſunt 6 in ℞ Q 54, reductis priùs 6 ad quadratum 36, & his ductis in ℞ Q 54, ac productis † ℞ Q 1944. Tertiò, ducta ſunt ℞ Q 24 in 18, reductis 18 priùs ad quadratum 324, & his ductis in ℞ Q 24, ac productis - ℞ Q 7776. Quartò, ducta ſunt ℞ Q 24 in ℞ Q 54, & producta - ℞ Q 1296, hoc eſt, - 36.

Simili ratione ex - ℞ Q 45 in - ℞ Q 20, fit † ℞ Q 900, hoc eſt, † 30. Et ex - ℞ Q 45 in † 6, id eſt, in - ℞ Q 36, fit ℞ Q 1620.

### §. III.

[Subhead]
*De Diviſione numerorum irrationalium compoſitorum.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:P|lines=2|style=plain}Rimò, *Quando Diviſor eſt ſimplex, & eundem*

[Margin]
*Diviſio irrationalium compoſitorum.*
[/Margin]

*habet cum Dividendo characterem; dividantur per illum ſingulæ particulæ Dividendi.* Ut ſi ſint ℞ Q 48 † ℞ Q 20, dividenda per ℞ Q 4; dividantur ℞ Q 48 per ℞ Q 4, proveniunt ℞ Q 12.

Secundò, *Quando Diviſor eſt ſimplex, & non habet eundem characterem cum Dividendo; reducatur ad eundem, & diviſio inſtituatur ut antea.* Ut ſi ſint dividenda ℞ Q 200 - ℞ C 80, per 2; factâ reductione, dividantur primò ℞ Q 200 per ℞ Q 4, proveniunt ℞ Q 50: dividantur deinde ℞ C 80 per ℞ C 8, proveniunt ℞ C 10.

Tertiò, *Quando Diviſor eſt compoſitus, ſuntque particulæ radices quadratæ, aut biquadratæ; reducatur diviſor ad ſimplicem, hoc modo: Signum † poſterioris particulæ Diviſoris mutetur in -, & - in †, atque in Diviſorem hoc modo mutatum ducatur tam Dividendus, quàm Diviſor, ut producantur novi Dividendus & Diviſor.* EXEMPLUM. Sint dividenda 60 per ℞ Q 18 † ℞ Q 8. Duc tam 60, quàm ℞ Q 18 † ℞ Q 8, in ℞ Q 18 - ℞ Q 8, & producetur hic novus Dividendus, ℞ Q 64800 - ℞ Q 28800, ſive ℞ Q 7200, & hic novus Diviſor, 10. Diviſis ergo ℞ Q 7200, per 10, ſive per ℞ Q 100, proveniunt ℞ Q 72.

### Annotatio.

{dropcap:Q|lines=2|style=plain}*Vid faciendum, quando Diviſio eſt inſtituenda per Diviſorem trium aut plurium particularum, quæ ſint radices quadratæ, aut biquadratæ; item quando inſtituenda eſt per numerum radicum cubicarum compoſitum &c: vide apud Clavium in Algebra cap. 23. non enim libet diutiùs immorari, cùm hi caſus rarò occurrant.*

## CAPUT IV.

[/Subhead]

[Margin]
*Elementa radicum univerſalium.*
[/Margin]

[/Margin]
