### IX.

Sector autem circuli eſt, cùm ad ipſius circuli

[Margin]
*Circuli ſector.*
[/Margin]

centrum conſtitutus fuerit angulus, comprehenſa nimirum figura & à rectis lineis angulum continentibus, & à peripheria ab illis aſſumpta. *Talis eſt in præcedenti figura circuli pars A D B, contenta rectis A D, B D, & circumferentiâ A B.*

### X.

Similia circuli ſegmenta

[Margin]
*Segmenta ſimilia circuli.*
[/Margin]

ſunt, quæ angulos capiunt æquales; aut in quibus anguli inter ſe ſunt æquales. *Sic duo ſegmenta A B C, D E F, inæqualium circulorum, dicuntur ſimilia, ſi anguli ad B & E æquales ſunt. Eodem modo ſi anguli A G C, D E F, æquales ſunt, ſegmenta eadem, in quibus dicti anguli exiſtunt, dicuntur ſimilia.*

[Diagram: figure 154; large circle with chord C A and lines from G and B, small circle with triangle E F D]

### XI.

*Angulus contingentiæ eſt, qui continetur lineâ rectâ contingente circulum, & circumferentiâ circuli;*

[Margin]
*Angulus contingentiæ.*
[/Margin]

*vel certè duabus circumferentijs ſe mutuò tangentibus, ſive exteriùs, ſive interiùs.*

## PROPOSITIONES.

## Propoſitio I. Problema.

[Subhead]
*Dati circuli centrum reperire.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:D|lines=2|style=plain}Ucatur in eo utcunque linea A C, quæ bifariam ſecetur in E, & per E ad A C agatur per-

[Margin]
*Centrum circuli reperire.*
[/Margin]

pendicularis B D, & dividatur bifariam in F. Dico, F eſſe centrum. Si enim non eſt, ſit aliud G extra ipſam B D, & ducantur G A, G C, G E; erunt duo latera G E, E A, æqualia duobus lateribus G E, E C; & baſis A G baſi C G, *per 15. Defi. pri.* ergo, *per 8. pri.* anguli A E G, C E G, æquales ſunt, ac proinde recti, *per 10. Defi. pri.* Eſt autem & angulus A E F rectus, ex conſtructione; ergo cùm, *per 10. Axi. pri.* omnes anguli recti inter ſe æquales ſint, erunt A E F, A E G, æquales, pars & totum; quod eſt abſurdum, & contra *9. Axi. pri.*

[Diagram: figure 155; circle with diameter B D, chord C E A, centre F, point G joined to A, C and E]

### Corollarium.

{dropcap:H|lines=2|style=plain}Inc colligitur, ſi in circulo recta aliqua linea aliquam rectam lineam bifariam & ad angulos rectos ſecat, in ſecante eſſe centrum circuli. Et quidem ſi utraque bifariam ſecatur, centrum eſt in communi earum concurſu.

## Propoſitio II. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Si in circuli peripheria duo quælibet puncta accepta fuerint; recta linea, quæ ad ipſa puncta adjungitur, intra circulum cadit.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:R|lines=2|style=plain}Ecta A B nectat duo puncta peripheriæ A & B; Dico, illam cadere intra circulum, ita ut omnia ejus puncta intermedia exiſtat intra circulum. Invento enim centro C, *per 1. hujus*, ducantur ſemidiametri C A, C B (qui erunt inter ſe æquales, *per 15. Def. pri.*) & ad quodvis aliud punctum D rectæ A B, ducatur recta C D. Erunt igitur, *per 5. pri.* anguli C A B, C B A æquales: eſt autem, *per 16. pri.* C D A major quàm C B A; ergo etiam major eſt quàm C A D; ideoque, *per 19. pri.* C D minor eſt ſemidiametro C A, ac proinde C D intra circulum cadit.

[Diagram: figure 156; circle with chord B D A and lines from A and B to centre C, with C D]

### Corollarium.

{dropcap:C|lines=2|style=plain}Olligitur hinc, lineam rectam quæ circulum tangit, in uno tantùm puncto ipſum tangere,

[Margin]
*Linea recta tangens circulum tangit eum in puncto.*
[/Margin]

alioquin ſi in duobus punctis eum tangeret, pars ejus caderet intra circulum, ideoq; circulum ſecaret.

## Propoſitio III. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Si in circulo recta quædam linea per centrum extenſa, quandam non per centrum extenſam bifariam ſecet; etiam ad angulos rectos eam ſecat: & ſi ad angulos rectos eam ſecat, bifariam quoque ſecat.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:D|lines=2|style=plain}Iameter C A E, ſecet aliam B D non per centrum ductam, bifariam. Dico, ſecare eam ad angulos rectos: & vice verſâ, ſecet eam ad angulos rectos; dico, ſecare eam bifariam. Ductis enim rectis A B, A D, erunt in prima hypotheſi, duo latera A F, F B, æqualia duobus lateribus A F, F D; & baſis A B baſi A D æqualis; ergo, *per 8. pri.* angulus A F B æqualis eſt angulo A F D, ac proinde uterque rectus, *per 10. Defi. pri.* In ſecunda verò hypotheſi, præter angulos rectos ad F, erunt *per 5. pri.* anguli A B D, A D B æquales, & latus A F ipſis oppoſitum commune; ergo *per 26. pri.* latus F B erit æquale lateri F D.

[Diagram: figure 157; circle with diameter C A F E and chord D F B, lines A D and A B]

### Corollarium.

{dropcap:C|lines=2|style=plain}Olligitur hinc, in omni triangulo iſoſceli A B D, & conſequenter in omni triangulo æquilatero, rectam A F ſecantem bifariam baſim B D, ſecare ipſam ad angulos rectos; & ſecantem ipſam ad angulos rectos, ſecare bifariam.

## Propoſitio IV. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Si in circulo duæ rectæ lineæ ſeſe mutuò ſecent non per centrum extenſæ; ſeſe mutuò bifariam non ſecabunt.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:D|lines=2|style=plain}Uæ rectæ A B, C D, ſecent ſe mutuò in E extra centrum F. Dico, eas non ſecare ſe mutuò bifariam. Si enim A B, C D, ſectæ eſſent bifariam in E; recta F E, ducta ex centro F, eſſet *per 3. hujus*, perpendicularis ad utramque, & anguli F E A, F E C, eſſent æquales, pars & totum; quod eſt abſurdum, & contra *9. Axi. pri.*

[Diagram: figure 158; circle with chords D A and B C crossing at E, centre F joined to E]
