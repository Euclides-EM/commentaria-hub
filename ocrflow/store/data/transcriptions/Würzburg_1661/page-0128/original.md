## Propoſitio XXIII. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Super eadem recta linea, duo ſegmenta circulorum ſimilia, & inæqualia, non conſtituentur ad eaſdem partes.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:V|lines=2|style=plain}El, *ſegmenta ſimilia, & ſuper eadem baſi conſtituta, ſunt æqualia.* Nam ſi unum alteri ſuperponatur, ſibi penitus congruunt, alioquin aliqua recta A C D, ſecabit unius peripheriam in C, alterius in D, fietque angulus externus A C B major interno C D B, *per 16. pri.* quod eſt contra hypotheſin: anguli enim ſimilium ſegmentorum debent eſſe æquales, *per 10. ## Propoſitio XXIV. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Super æqualibus rectis lineis, ſimilia circulorum ſegmenta, ſunt inter ſe æqualia.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:D|lines=2|style=plain}Emonſtratur eodem modo ut præcedens: ſicut enim rectæ mutuò ſuperpoſitæ ſibi congruunt, ita & peripheriæ; alioquin ſequitur idem abſurdum quod antea.

[Subhead]
*Circuli ſegmento dato, deſcribere circulum cujus eſt ſegmentum.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:V|lines=2|style=plain}El, *Dati ſegmenti centrum reperire.* In dato ſegmento

## Propoſitio XXVI. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*In æqualibus circulis, æquales anguli æqualibus peripheriis inſiſtunt, ſive ad centra, ſive ad peripherias conſtituti inſiſtant.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=plain}Int *primò* æquales anguli ad centra G & H. Cùm igitur circa eaſdem ſint quatuor ſemidiametri æquales, erit, *per 4. pri.* baſis A C æqualis baſi D F. Et quia iidem anguli G & H, *per 20. hujus.* dupli ſunt angulorum A B C, D E F; ideoque ſuper æqualibus baſibus ſegmenta A B C, D E F, ſimilia; erunt, *per 24. hujus,* eadem ſegmenta æqualia, & peripheriæ B A C, E D F, nec non reliquæ B C, E F æquales. *Secundò,* ſi anguli B & E ponantur æquales, neceſſe eſt etiam G & H eſſe æquales, utpote illorum duplos, ergo &c:

*Etiam in eodem circulo, æquales anguli inſiſtunt æqualibus peripherijs, propter eandem cauſam.*

## Propoſitio XXVII. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*In æqualibus circulis, anguli qui æqualibus peripherijs inſiſtunt, ſunt inter ſe æquales, ſive ad centra, ſive ad peripherias conſtituti inſiſtant.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=plain}Int arcus A C, D F æquales. Dico, tam angulos ad centra G & H, quàm angulos B & E ad peripheriam eſſe æquales. Si enim angulus A G C eſſet major angulo D H F, ipſique D H F fieret æqualis A G I; arcus A I eſſet æqualis arcui D F, *per 26. hujus,* hoc eſt, ipſi A C; quod eſt abſurdum, & contra 9. *Axi. pri.* Similis eſt ratio de angulis B & E.

## Propoſitio XXVIII. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*In æqualibus circulis, æquales rectæ lineæ, æquales peripherias auferunt, majorem quidem majori, minorem autem minori.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=plain}Int rectæ A C, D F, æquales. Dico, majorem peripheriam A B C, æqualem eſſe majori D E F, & minorem A C minori D F. Nam ductis A G, C G, item D H, F H, erunt, *per 8. pri.* anguli G & H æquales, quia circa ipſas ſunt quatuor ſemidiametri æquales, & inſuper baſis A C ponitur æqualis baſi D F: Ergo, *per 26. hujus,* arcus A C eſt æqualis arcui D F, & reliquus A B C reliquo D E F.
