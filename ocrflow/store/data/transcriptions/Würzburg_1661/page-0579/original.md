### I.

{dropcap:C|lines=7|style=decorated|decoration="foliate ornament on a patterned ground"}*Orpus radioſum vocamus*

[Margin]
*Catoptricæ definitiones,*
[/Margin]

*omne illud, quod vel lumen, vel ſpecies colorum diffundit per medium diaphanum. Tam autem lumen, quàm ſpecies colorum appellamus radios. Et corpus quidem profundens lumen, vocamus luminoſum; corpus verò profundens ſpecies, coloratum.* Explicatio petatur ex dictis libro præcedenti.

### II.

*Radij corporis luminoſi & colorati, non ſunt lineæ mathematicæ omnis latitudinis expertes; ſed ſunt lumen ipſum, ipſæque ſpecies, diffuſæ per medium diaphanum ſphæricè, & vijs rectis.* Concipimus tamen in hujuſmodi lumine, & ſpeciebus, lineas mathematicas, & ſuperficies, & ſolidas figuras, ut pyramides, conos, cylindros &c: & ijs ut talibus utimur in explicandis ac demonſtrandis ſpeculorum phænomenis.
