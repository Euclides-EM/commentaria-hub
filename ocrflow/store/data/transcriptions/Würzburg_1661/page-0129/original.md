## Propoſitio XXIX. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*In æqualibus circulis, æquales peripherias, æquales rectæ lineæ ſubtendunt.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:Q|lines=2|style=plain}Uia in ijſdem circulis, *per 27. hujus*, etiam angulus G erit æqualis angulo H, ſi peripheria A C ſit æqualis peripheriæ D F, &, *per 4. pri.* baſis A C æqualis baſi D F, propter quatuor ſemidiametros æquales.

## Propoſitio XXX. Problema.

[Subhead]
*Datam peripheriam ſecare bifariam.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:P|lines=2|style=plain}Eripheria A B C ſit bifariam ſecanda.

[Margin]
*Peripheriam datam ſecare bifariam.*
[/Margin]

Subtendatur A C, eaque ſecetur bifariam in D, & erigatur perpendicularis D B. Dico, peripheriam eſſe in B ſectam æqualiter. Nam ductis B A, B C, duo latera A D, D B, ſunt æqualia duobus C D, D B, & anguli ad D æquales, quia recti; ergo *per 4. pri.* baſes A B, C B, æquales; ergo *per 28 hujus,* etiam peripheriæ A B, C B æquales ſunt.

## Propoſitio XXXI. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*In circulo angulus qui in ſemicirculo, rectus eſt: qui autem in majore ſegmento quàm ſemicirculus, minor recto; qui verò in minore ſegmento, major recto. Et inſuper angulus majoris ſegmenti recto quidem major eſt, minoris autem ſegmenti angulus minor eſt recto.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:C|lines=2|style=plain}Irculi diameter ſit A D C, conſtituaturque in ſemicirculo angulus A B C, exiſtetque angulus B A C in majore ſegmento C A B. Conſtituatur quoque in C E B minori ſegmento angulus B E C. Dico *primò*, angulum A B C in ſemicirculo, eſſe rectum. Nam rectæ D A, D B, ſunt æquales, *per 15. Defi. pri.* ergo anguli D A B, D B A, æquales ſunt, *per 5. pri.* Item rectæ D B, D C, æquales ſunt, & anguli D B C, D C B, æquales, propter eandem cauſam. Jam ſic. Angulus A D B, *per 20. hujus,* duplus eſt anguli D B C; & angulus, C D B, *per 32. pri.* duplus eſt anguli D B A; ergo duo anguli ad D, ſunt duplum duorum angulorum D B A, C B D, *per 23. Axi. pri.* Sed duo anguli ad D, æquales ſunt duobus rectis, *per 13. pri.* ergo alij duo D B A, D B C, æquales ſunt uni recto. Dico *ſecundò*, in majori ſegmento C A B, angulum B A C eſſe acutum. Nam trianguli A B C duo anguli A B C, B A C, ſunt duobus rectis minores, *per 17. pri.* quia ergo prior eſt rectus, erit alter acutus. Dico *tertiò*, angulum B E C in minori ſegmento B E C, eſſe obtuſum. Nam in quadrilatero A B E C, oppoſiti anguli E & A, ſunt æquales duobus rectis, *per 22. hujus*; cùm ergo A ſit acutus, ut fuit oſtenſum, erit E obtuſus. Dico *quartò*, angulum mixtum A B C, ex arcu A B & recta B C, qui eſt angulus majoris ſegmenti, eſſe recto majorem, quia major eſt recto rectilineo A B C, cùm præter hunc includat angulum ſegmenti quod abſcindit. Dico *quintò*, angulum C B E ſegmenti minoris, comprehenſum recta C B, & arcu C E B, eſſe minorem recto, quia eſt minor angulo C B F, qui rectus eſt, *per 13. pri.*

*Vice etiam versâ, ſegmentum circuli, in quo angulus conſtitutus eſt rectus, ſemicirculus eſt. Et angulus rectus eſt ad ſemicirculum, hoc eſt, ſi angulus A B C eſt rectus, & A C ſectâ bifariam in D, deſcribatur circa eam ſemicirculus; ipſe tranſibit per B.*

## Propoſitio XXXII. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Si circulum tetigerit aliqua recta linea, à contactu autem producatur quædam recta linea circulum ſecans; anguli quos ad contingentem facit, æquales ſunt ijs, qui in alternis circuli ſegmentis conſiſtunt, angulis.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:R|lines=2|style=plain}Ecta A B tangat circulum in C, & C D ſecet eundem in C & D. Dico, angulo A C D æquales eſſe angulos in alterno ſegmento D E C; & angulo B C D angulos in alterno ſegmento C F D. Nam ſi C D non tranſit per centrum H, tranſeat C H E; eruntque *per 18. hujus,* E C A, E C B, recti, & ipſis erunt æquales C G E, C D E, in alternis ſegmentis, hoc eſt, *per præcedentem,* in ſemicirculis quos abſcindit ſemidiameter C E. Deinde, quoniam C D E rectus eſt; erunt D E C, D C E, uni recto, nempe toti E C A æquales, *per 32. pri.* demptoque communi D C E, reliquus D E C in alterno ſegmento, æqualis erit reliquo A C D, ad punctum contactus C. Denique, in quadrilatero E D F C, anguli oppoſiti D F C, C E D, ſunt, *per 22. hujus,* æquales duobus rectis, hoc eſt, duobus D C A, D C B: ſed D C A æqualis eſt D E C; ergo reliquus D F C in alterno ſegmento, eſt æqualis reliquo D C B, ad contactum C.
