## CAPUT II.

[Subhead]
*De Logiſtica magnitudinum ſimplicium pro Algebra Specioſa.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:L|lines=2|style=plain}Ogiſtica apud Arithmeticos eſt modus addendi,

[Margin]
Logiſtica Algebræ Specioſæ magnitudinum ſimplicium.
[/Margin]

ſubtrahendi, multiplicandi, & dividendi. Alii vocant Algorithmum. Nos hoc libro ſuprà par.1.& lib.2.appellavimus Elementa Arithmeticæ, & Algebræ. Convenit in multis cum Logiſtica Algebræ numeroſæ, uti ex ſequentibus patebit, quæ ex Herigono deſumpta ſunt, quoniam perinde eſt ad explicationem, ſive noſtra, ſive aliena ponamus verba & exempla.

### §. I. *De Additione.*

{dropcap:A|lines=2|style=plain}Dditio in iiſdem litteris fit ut in numeris ab-

[Margin]
Additio Algebræ Specioſæ.
[/Margin]

ſolutis; at in diverſis litteris fit interjecto ſigno †.

*Exemplum in ijſdem litteris.*

[Calculation]
 a     b     5a     5a2
 a     b     3a     3a2
──────────────────────────
2a    2b     8a     8a2
[/Calculation]

*Exemplum in diverſis litteris.*

[Calculation]
 a              5a
 b       .      3b
──────────────────────────
 a  †  b      5a † 3b.
[/Calculation]

### §. II. *De Subtractione.*

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=plain}Ubtractio in iiſdem litteris fit ut in numeris

[Margin]
Subtractio Algebræ Specioſæ.
[/Margin]

abſolutis; at in diverſis litteris fit interjecto ſigno —.

*Exemplum in ijſdem litteris.*

[Calculation]
2a     8b     8a2
 a     3b     5a2
──────────────────────
 a     5b     3a2
[/Calculation]

*Exemplum in diverſis litteris.*

[Calculation]
 a        b        5a
 b        d        3b
──────────────────────────
a — b   b — d   5a — 3b
[/Calculation]

### §. III. *De Multiplicatione.*

{dropcap:M|lines=2|style=plain}Ultiplicatio in iiſdem litteris fit additione

[Margin]
Multiplicatio Algebræ Specioſæ.
[/Margin]

Exponentium; in diverſis verò litteris ponuntur litteræ continuè cum ſuis Exponentibus. Si litteræ habent numeros præpoſitos, multiplicari debent inter ſe ut in numeris abſolutis.

*Exemplum in ijſdem & diverſis litteris.*

[Calculation]
 a     a3     b2     a
 a     a2     b      b
──────────────────────────
 a2    a5     b3     ab
[/Calculation]

*Exemplum in numeris præpoſitis.*

[Calculation]
 7a2      a      6a3
 4a3      b      3b2
──────────────────────────
28a5     ab    18a3b2.
[/Calculation]

### §. IV. *De Diviſione.*

{dropcap:D|lines=2|style=plain}Iviſio in iiſdem litteris fit ſubductione Exponentis Diviſoris ab Exponente Dividendi;

[Margin]
Diviſio Algebræ Specioſæ.
[/Margin]

in diverſis verò litteris fit fractio ſubjiciendo Diviſorem Dividendo. Quòd ſi litteræ habeant numeros præpoſitos, fiet diviſio ut in numeris abſolutis.

*Exempla.*

[Calculation]
a5          | b3
a2   (a3    | b2   (b
[/Calculation]

*Alia.*

[Calculation]
28a5          | 7ab2
 4a3   (4a2   |  ab    (7b
[/Calculation]

*Aliud.*

[Calculation]
a3  |  a3
──     ──
b2  |  b2
[/Calculation]

## CAPUT III.

[Subhead]
*De Logiſtica magnitudinum compoſitarum.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:M|lines=2|style=plain}Agnitudines compoſitæ ſunt, quæ habent

[Margin]
Logiſtica Algebræ Specioſæ magnitudinum compoſitarum.
[/Margin]

ſigna † vel -. Hæc difficilior eſt quàm præcedens. Verba ferè & exempla Herigoni damus. Notandum magnitudines quibus non præponitur ſignum -, intelligi habere ſignum †.

### §. I. *De Additione.*

{dropcap:A|lines=2|style=plain}Dditio in iiſdem ſignis affectionis habet

[Margin]
Additio.
[/Margin]

idem ſignum; in diverſis ſignis, additio eſt ſubtractio, & reſiduum habet ſignum majoris.

*Exempla in ijſdem ſignis.*

[Calculation]
  a2†bd       |  a - 3b
  a2†d2       |  a -  b
──────────────────────────
2a2†bd†d2     | 2a - 4b.
[/Calculation]

*Alia.*

[Calculation]
 5a†4     |   5a2-4
 4a†3     |   4a2-3
──────────────────────
 9a†7         9a2-7
[/Calculation]

*Exempla in diverſis ſignis.*

[Calculation]
 a2 † 2ab   |  a3-a2b
 a2 -  ab   |  2a3†ab2
2a2 †  ab   |  3a3-a2b†ab2.
[/Calculation]

*Aliud.*

[Calculation]
6  a  2  †  8  a
2  a  2  -  10 a
──────────────────
8  a  2  -  2  a.
[/Calculation]
