## Propoſitio X. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Circulus circulum in pluribus quàm duobus punctis non ſecat.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=plain}Ecent enim, ſi fieri poteſt, ſe mutuò in tribus punctis, A, B, C, & centrum unius ſit D. Cùm ergo tria puncta, B, A, C, ſint communia utriq; circulo; erunt etiam ad alterius circuli peripheriam tres rectæ, D B, D A, D C, æquales; ideoque D erit centrum utriuſque; quod eſt contra *5. hujus.*

## Propoſitio XI. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Si duo circuli ſeſe intus contingant, atque accepta fuerint eorum centra; ad eorum centra adjuncta linea, & producta, in contactum circulorum cadet.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:P|lines=2|style=plain}Unctum contactus ſit A, centrum interioris circuli ſit G, exterioris F; & recta F G, ſi fieri poteſt, non tranſeat per A, ſed interiorem ſecet in B, exteriorem in E. Ductis A F, A G, erunt G A, G B æquales, *per 15. Defi. pri.* adjectâque F G, erunt A G, G F æquales F B: ſed A G, G F ſunt majores A F, *per 20. pri.* ergo etiam F B major erit quàm A F, hoc eſt, major quàm F E; quod eſt abſurdum. Idem ſequeretur ex altera parte, ſi F poneretur eſſe centrum interioris, & G exterioris, etiamſi puncta C, D, vel B, E, ponerentur coincidere in unum, quia ſemper unus circulus eſt altero major.

[Diagram: figure 165; two circles touching internally at A, with points E, B, G, F, C and D]

## Propoſitio XII. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Si duo circuli ſeſe exteriùs contingant, linea recta quæ ad centrum eorum conjungitur, per contactum tranſibit.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=plain}I enim F G, conjungens centra F, G, non tranſit per contactum B, ſed ſecet circulos in C & E; erunt duo latera, F B, G B, vel æqualia, vel minora tertio F C E G; quod eſt contra *20. pri.*

## Propoſitio XIII. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Circulus circulum non tangit in pluribus punctis, quàm uno, ſive intùs, ſive extrà tangat.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=plain}I enim duo circuli ſe mutuò tangerent interiùs in A & B; recta C D, conjungens centra C & D,

[Diagram: figure 167; two circles supposed to touch internally at A and B, with diameter B D C A]

*per 11. hujus*, tranſiret per utrumque, eſſetque A C D B communis diameter, eademque ſecta bifariam in duobus punctis C & D; quod eſt abſurdum. Si autem ſe tangerent exteriùs in duobus punctis B & F; una eademque D E tranſiret per utrumque, *per 12. hujus*; vel certè D B, E B erunt æquales rectis D F, E F, hoc eſt, ipſi D E; cùm tamen ſint majores, *per 20. pri.*

## Propoſitio XIV. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*In circulo æquales rectæ lineæ æqualiter diſtant à centro: Et quæ æqualiter diſtant à centro, æquales ſunt inter ſe.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=plain}Int *primò* rectæ A B, D C, æquales. Dico, ipſas æqualiter à centro E diſtare. Ducantur enim perpendiculares E F, E G, & conjungantur E A, E D: ſecabunt illæ duas A B, D C bifariam, *per 3. hujus*, & ideo A F, D G, ſemiſſes æqualium, æquales erunt: & quia *per 47. pri.* æqualibus quadratis ſemidiametrorum E A, E D, æqualia ſunt quadrata A F, F E, & quadrata D G, G E; neceſſe eſt, hæc duo illis duobus eſſe æqualia, & demptis æqualibus A F, D G, remanere æqualia quadrata E F, E G, & conſequenter rectas E F, E G æquales eſſe. Diſtant igitur, *per 4. Defi. hujus*, rectæ A B, D C, æqualiter à centro E. Sint *Secundò* A B, D C, æqualiter diſtantes à centro E. Dico, eas eſſe inter ſe æquales. Nam ſi ex duobus quadratis E F, F A, quæ ſunt æqualia duobus E G, G D, quia ſunt æqualia quadratis E A, E D, tollantur æqualia E F, E G; remanent æqualia quadrata F A, G D, ipſæque A F, D G æquales. Eſt autem, *per 3. hujus*, A F medietas totius A B, & D G medietas totius D C; Ergo A B, D C, ſunt æquales.

## Propoſitio XV. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*In circulo maxima quidem linea eſt diameter; aliarum autem propinquior centro, remotiore ſemper major.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:D|lines=2|style=plain}Iameter ſit A F, ei propinquior H I, remotior C D. Dico, A F eſſe maximam, & H I majorem quàm C D. Ducantur enim perpendiculares G K, G L; erit illa, *per 4. Defi. hujus*, minor iſtâ, & ideo ex G L poterit abſcindi G M, æqualis ipſi G K; & B E æquidiſtans ipſi C D, erit *per 14. hujus*, æqualis I H. Nectantur jam G B, G C, G D, G E: eruntque *per 20. pri.* duo latera G B, G E majora reliquo B E: ſed G B, G E ſunt æquales diametro A F; ergo B E, & conſequenter I H, minor eſt diametro. Quòd autem I H ſit major quàm C D, patet *per 24. pri*, quia latera G B, G E ſunt æqualia lateribus G C, G D, & angulus B G E major angulo C G D; ergo &c:
