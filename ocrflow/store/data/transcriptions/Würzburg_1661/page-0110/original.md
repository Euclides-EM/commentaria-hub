## Propoſitio XIV. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Si ad aliquam rectam lineam, atque ad idem ejus punctum, duæ rectæ lineæ non ad eaſdem partes ductæ, eos, qui ſunt deinceps, angulos duobus rectis æquales fecerint; in directum erunt inter ſe ipſæ rectæ lineæ.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:A|lines=2|style=plain}D punctum C, lineæ rectæ AB, in diverſas

[Margin]
*Lineæ duæ rectæ ad aliam ad diverſas partes ductæ, ſi faciant duos angulos æquales duobus rectis, in directum ſunt poſitæ.*
[/Margin]

partes eductæ ſint duæ rectæ, CD, CE, facientes cum AB, duos angulos ACD, ACE, vel rectos, vel duobus rectis æquales. Dico, ipſas CD, CE, eſſe inter ſe conſtitutas in directum, ita ut conſtituant unam lineam rectam DCE. Si enim DCE non eſt recta, ſeu ſi DC, EC, non ſunt in directum conſtitutæ; ergo recta DC producta ultra C in directum & continuum, cadet aut ſupra CE, ut ſit recta DCF; aut infra CE, ut ſit recta DCG. Si cadit ſupra, & DCF conſtituit unam rectam lineam; cùm AC conſiſtat ſuper rectam DCF, fient duo anguli ACD, ACF, duobus rectis æquales, *per 13. pri.* ſunt autem ex hypotheſi etiam duo anguli ACD, ACE, æquales duobus rectis; Erunt ergo priores duo ACD, ACF, poſterioribus duobus ACD, ACE, æquales, pars toti; quod eſt impoſſibile, & contra *9. Ax.* Non igitur recta DC producta cadit ſupra CE. Neque etiam infra cadit, propter ſimilem rationem, quia ſequeretur angulos ACD, ACE, eſſe æquales angulis ACD, ACG.

## Propoſitio XV. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Si duæ rectæ lineæ ſe mutuò ſecuerint, angulos ad verticem oppoſitos efficient inter ſe æquales.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=plain}Ecent ſe duæ rectæ AB, CD, in puncto E utcunque.

[Margin]
*Anguli oppoſiti ad verticem, ſunt æquales inter ſe.*
[/Margin]

Dico, angulos ad verticem E oppoſitos, eſſe inter ſe æquales, nimirum angulum AED angulo CEB, & angulum AEC angulo DEB. Nam duo anguli, AED, DEB, ſunt æquales duobus rectis, *per 13. pri.* item duo anguli, DEB, BEC, ſunt duobus rectis æquales, *per eandem 13. pri.* Cùm ergo omnes recti ſint inter ſe æquales, *per 10. Ax.* erunt duo anguli AED, DEB, duobus angulis DEB, BEC, æquales; ac pro inde dempto communi angulo DEB, remanebit angulus AED æqualis angulo BEC, *per 3.
