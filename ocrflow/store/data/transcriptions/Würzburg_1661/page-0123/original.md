## Propoſitio XIV. Problema.

[Subhead]
*Dato rectilineo æquale quadratum conſtituere.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=plain}It datum rectilineum A, cui quadratum æquale conſtituendum eſt. Conſtituatur, *per 42. vel 45. pri.* dicto rectilineo æquale parallelogrammum rectangulum B C D E, cujus unum latus, ut C D, producatur ad F, ſitque C F æqualis rectæ C B, & tota D F dividatur bifariam in G, ex quo velut centro deſcribatur circa totam D F ſemicirculum, eumque ſecet B C protracta in H, & ducatur G H. Dico, quadratum C H ęquale eſſe rectangulo D B, & conſequenter rectilineo A. Nam quia recta D F dividitur bifariam in G, & non bifariam in C; erit *per 5. ſec.* rectangulum comprehenſum ſub D C, C F, hoc eſt, rectangulum D B, unà cum quadrato G C, æquale quadrato G F, hoc eſt, quadrato G H: Sed huic, *per 47. pri.* æqualia ſunt quadrata G C, C H; Ergo hæc duo quadrata ſunt æqualia dicto rectangulo D B, & quadrato G C; Ergo dempto communi quadrato G C, remanet quadratum C H æquale rectangulo D B, hoc eſt, rectilineo A.

Ero in demonſtrationibus ſequentibus brevior, quàm in præteritis; & ſæpe brevitatis causâ omittam citationes Axiomatum & Definitionum ex Primo lib. Elem.*

## DEFINITIONES.

### I.

{dropcap:Æ|lines=2|style=plain}Quales circuli ſunt, quorum diametri ſunt æquales;

[Margin]
*Circuli æquales quorum diametri æquales.*
[/Margin]

vel quorum, quæ ex centris, rectæ lineæ, ſunt æquales. *Inæquales ergo circuli ſunt, quorum diametri vel ſemidiametri inæquales; majorque aut minor ille, cujus diameter vel ſemidiameter major aut minor eſt.*

### II.

Recta linea circulum tangere

[Margin]
*Linea tangens circulum quænam?*
[/Margin]

dicitur, quæ cùm circulum tangat, ſi producatur, circulum non ſecat. *In appoſita figura, recta A B tangit circulum in C; at recta E G ſecat eundem in F.*

[Diagram: figure 149; circle touched by line B C A at C, and line from E cutting circle at F and G]

### III.

Circuli ſe mutuò tangere

[Margin]
*Circuli tangentes ſe.*
[/Margin]

dicuntur, qui ſeſe mutuò tangentes, ſeſe mutuò non ſecant. *In appoſitis figuris, circuli A & B tangunt ſe mutuò in C; at circuli D & E, ſecant ſe mutuò in F & G.*

[Diagram: figure 150; pairs of circles, A and B touching at C, D and E intersecting at F and G]

### IV.

In circulo æqualiter diſtare à centro rectæ lineæ

[Margin]
*Lineæ æqualiter diſtantes à centro circuli quænam?*
[/Margin]

dicuntur, cùm perpendiculares, quæ à centro in ipſas ducuntur, ſunt æquales. Longiùs autem abſeſſe illa dicitur, in quam major perpendicularis cadit. *In præſenti figura rectæ B C, D E, diſtant æqualiter à centro A, ſi perpendiculares A H, A I, ſunt æquales: at F G diſtat magis, ſi A K major eſt quàm A H, aut A I.*

[Diagram: figure 151; circle with centre A, chords E C, D B, G F, perpendiculars A I, A H, A K]

### V.

Segmentum circuli eſt figura,

[Margin]
*Circuli ſegmentum*
[/Margin]

quæ ſub recta linea, & circuli peripheria comprehenditur. *In figura appoſita, tam A D C, quàm A B C, eſt ſegmentum circuli, quorum hoc majus, illud minus dicitur. Vide quæ diximus lib. 1. cap. 3. a. 3. num. 10.*

[Diagram: figure 20; circle with chord A O C, point B above with lines to F and E, D below]

### VI.

Segmenti autem angulus eſt, qui ſub recta linea

[Margin]
*Angulus ſegmenti circuli.*
[/Margin]

& circuli peripheria comprehenditur. *Tales ſunt in præcedenti figura anguli mixtilinei D A C, D C A; item B A C, B C A; quorum hi dicuntur anguli ſegmenti majoris, illi verò anguli ſegmenti minoris.* Quòd ſi ſegmentum fuerit ſemicirculus, dicetur angulus ſemicirculi.

[Margin]
*Angulus ſemicirculi.*
[/Margin]

### VII.

In ſegmento autem angulus eſt, cùm in ſegmenti

[Margin]
*Angulus in ſegmento.*
[/Margin]

peripheria ſumptum fuerit quodpiam punctum, & ab illo in terminos rectæ ejus lineæ quæ ſegmenti baſis eſt, adjunctæ fuerint rectæ lineæ; is inquam angulus ab adjunctis illis lineis comprehenſus. *In appoſita figura tam angulus rectilineus A C B, quàm A D B, eſt angulus in ſegmento; & prior dicitur exiſtere in ſegmento A C B, poſterior in ſegmento A D B. Quòd ſi baſis hujuſmodi angulorum eſt diameter circuli, dicuntur anguli in ſemicirculo.*

[Margin]
*Angulus in ſemicirculo.*
[/Margin]

[Diagram: figure 152; circle with chord B A, triangle A C B above and angle A D B below]

### VIII.

Cùm verò comprehendentes angulum rectæ lineæ

[Margin]
*Angulus peripheriæ inſiſtens.*
[/Margin]

aliquam aſſumunt peripheriam; illi angulus inſiſtere dicitur. *In appoſita figura, tam angulus rectilineus A C B ad peripheriam, quàm A D B ad centrum, dicitur inſiſtere peripheriæ A B, quam intercipiunt lineæ rectæ continentes angulos C & D.*

[Diagram: figure 153; circle with angle A C B at circumference and angle A D B at centre D]
