Diſtinctas vocabimus Numeros irrationales compoſitos (videlicet abſolutos) radices verò Univerſales vocabimus etiam Radices numerorum irrationalium compoſitorum.

## CAPUT II.

[Subhead]
*De Elementis numerorum irrationalium ſimplicium.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:E|lines=2|style=plain}Lementa horum numerorum ſeu radicum ſunt Additio, Subtractio, Multiplicatio, Diviſio.

[Margin]
*Elementa irrationalium numerorum ſimplicium.*
[/Margin]

His præmittenda eorundem Reductio ad eandem denominationem, quando opus fuerit.

### §. I.

[Subhead]
*De Reductione numerorum irrationalium ſimplicium ad eandem denominationem.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=plain}I numeri irrationales ſimplices ſunt diverſæ denominationis,

[Margin]
*Reductio irrationalium ſimplicium.*
[/Margin]

hoc eſt, habent diverſos characteres; ut poſſint addi, ſubtrahi, multiplicari, ac dividi, debent reduci priùs ad eandem denominationem, ſeu ad eoſdem characteres: quod fit hoc modo.

*Collocentur numeri ſuprà, characteres infrà, ut hîc apparet. Deinde per crucem fiat multiplicatio talis, qualem character indicat, ut fiant novi Numeratores. Poſtremo utrique Numeratori novo producto præfigatur uterque character.*

[Diagram: crossed lines between 16 and 8 above, ℞c. and ℞q below, with 256 and 512 beneath]

EXEMPLUM. Sint reducendi ad eoſdem characteres hi duo numeri, ℞ c 16, & ℞ q 8. Collocatione factâ ut vides, multiplicentur 16 quadratè, propter characterem ℞ q per crucem his reſpondentem; 8 verò multiplicentur cubicè, propter characterem ℞ c eis reſpondentem per crucem; producentur 256, & 512. His præfigatur uterque character hoc modo, ℞ cq 512, & ℞ cq 256. Quo facto, erit ℞ cq 512, æqualis ℞ c 16; & radix ℞ cq 256, æqualis ℞ q 8.

### §. II.

[Subhead]
*De Multiplicatione, ac Diviſione numerorum irrationalium ſimplicium.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:M|lines=2|style=plain}Ultiplicatio ac Diviſio præmittendæ ſunt Additioni ac Subtractioni, quoniam hæ ab illis pendent.

[Margin]
*Multiplicatio ac Diviſio irrationalium ſimplicium.*
[/Margin]

Primò itaque, *Quando duo numeri irrationales multiplicandi, aut dividendi, habent eoſdem characteres, multiplicetur aut dividatur unus numerus per alterum, & producto præponatur idem character quem antea habebant.* Sic ex multiplicatione ℞ q 7, per ℞ q 10, fit ℞ q 70. Et ex ℞ q 3, per ℞ q 12, fit ℞ q 36, id eſt, 6. Et ex ℞ q 2 1/4 per ℞ Q 8, fit ℞ Q 18. Sic etiam ex diviſione ℞ Q 70 per ℞ Q 7, fit ℞ Q 10. Et ex diviſione ℞ Q 36, per ℞ Q 12, fit ℞ Q 3. Et ex ℞ Q 18, per ℞ Q 8, fit ℞ Q 2 1/4.

Secundò, *Quando duo numeri irrationales, multiplicandi, aut dividendi, habent diverſos characteres, reducantur priùs ad eoſdem, deinde multiplicentur aut dividantur ut antea.* Sic ſi multiplicandi ſint ℞ C 16, per ℞ Q 8; reducantur ad eoſdem characteres, & fient ℞ Cq 512, & ℞ Cq 256; & factâ multiplicatione illius per hunc, producentur ℞ Cq 131072. Sic etiam ſi dividendi ſint ℞ C 8000, per ℞ Q 16; reducantur ad eoſdem characteres, & fient ℞ C q 64000000, & ℞ C q 4096; & factâ diviſione illius numeri per hunc, proveniunt ℞ C q 15625, cujus radix quadrata eſt 125, hujus verò cubica 5. Diviſis ergo ℞ C 8000 per ℞ Q 16, proveniunt 5.

Tertiò, *Quando numerus irrationalis (ſeu radix ſurda) multiplicandus eſt in ſe quadratè, vel cubicè, vel biquadratè, vel ſecundùm exigentiam alterius cujuſcunque characteris quem gerit; tollitur character, & ipſemet numerus ſumitur pro numero producto.* Sic ℞ Q 3 in ſe quadratè multiplicata, facit 3: & ℞ C 4 in ſe cubicè multiplicata, facit 4 &c: Ratio eſt, quia ℞ Q 3 in ℞ Q 3, facit ℞ 9, hoc eſt, 3: & ℞ C 4 in ℞ C 4, facit ℞ C 16; & ℞ C 4 in ℞ C 16, facit ℞ C 64, hoc eſt, 4.

Quartò *Quando numerus irrationalis (ſeu radix ſurda) multiplicandus eſt in ſe ſecundùm exigentiam alterius characteris quem ipſe non gerit; ducitur numerus in ſe ſecundùm exigentiam alterius illius characteris; & producto præponitur character numeri multiplicati.* Sic ex ℞ Q 6 in ſe cubicè multiplicata, fit ℞ Q 216. Quod inde patet, quia ex ℞ Q 6 in ſe, fit ℞ Q 36; & ex eadem ℞ Q 6 in ℞ Q 36, fit ℞ Q 216. Unde radix cubica hujus ultimi producti eſt ℞ Q 6.

Quintò, *Quando numerus irrationalis multiplicandus aut dividendus eſt per rationalem, reducatur primò rationalis ad ſpeciem irrationalis, hoc eſt, multiplicetur quadratè, cubicè, biquadratè &c: prout irrationalis habuerit characterem ℞ Q, aut ℞ C, aut ℞ Qq &c: & deinde inſtituatur operatio ut in prima Regula.* Sic ſi ducenda ſint ℞ Q 32 in 8, multiplicanda ſunt priùs 8 quadratè, propterea quòd irrationalis ℞ Q 32, habet characterem Q; ac deinde ℞ Q 32 ducenda in ℞ Q 64, ut producantur ℞ Q 2048. Sic etiam ſi ℞ C 12 ſit multiplicanda per 6; multiplicanda ſunt 6 cubicè, ut fiant ℞ C 216; ac deinde ℞ C 12 ducenda ſunt in 216, ut producantur ℞ C 2592. Sic quoque ſi dividenda ſint ℞ Q 32 per 8; multiplicanda ſunt cubicè 8, ut fiant ℞ Q 64; ac deinde ℞ Q 64 ducenda ſunt in ℞ Q 32, ut fiant ℞ Q 2048.

### Annotatio I.

{dropcap:I|lines=2|style=plain}*Taque ſi radix ſurda quadrata eſt duplicanda, id eſt, multiplicanda per 2; multiplicandus eſt ejus numerus per 4: producti enim ℞ Q, eſt dupla radicis datæ. Ut duplum ℞ Q 3, eſt ℞ Q 12: quia ut ℞ Q 3 multiplicetur per 2, reducendi ſunt numeri ℞ Q 3, & 2, ad eandem denominationem modo prædicto, ut fiant ℞ Q 3, & ℞ Q 4. Quòd ſi radix ſurda quadrata eſt triplicanda, multiplicandus eſt ejus numerus per 9; ſi quadruplicandus, eſt multiplicandus per 16, & ſic ulterius.*
