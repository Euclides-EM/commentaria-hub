#### Praxis III. Angulum rectilineum alteri dato æqualem conſtituere.

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=plain}It datus angulus rectilineus B A C, eique conſtituendus æqualis alius. Centro A, intervallo quolibet A B, fiat arcus B C. Deinde ductâ rectâ D E, ex E puncto velut centro, intervallo eodem A B, fiat alius arcus E G. Demum intercapedo B C circino accepta transferatur in arcum E G, ex E in F, & ducatur recta D F; erit angulus E D F æqualis angulo B A C.
