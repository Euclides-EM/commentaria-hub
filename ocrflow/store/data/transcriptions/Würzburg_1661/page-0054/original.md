##### Annotationes.

I.

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=plain}Ufficit tamen, delineare tantùm arcus parvos infrà & ſuprà, ubi C & D, interſecantes ſeſe.

II. Si linea data eſt in extremo plani, ita ut infra aut ſupra ipſam non ſit locus in quo deſcribi poſſint arcus ſeſe interſecantes; tuno{printer-error-correction:tunc} deſcriptis ſupra aut infra ipſam duobus arcubus interſecantibus ſeſe in C, decribantur{printer-error-correction:deſcribantur} alii duo interſecantes ſe in D, ſive hoc fiat infra C, ſive ſupra.

[Diagram: figure 56, line A E B with perpendicular from E through D and C, arcs crossing at C and D above the line]

III. Si recta data tam longa ſit, ut centris A & B non poſſint formari infra & ſupra arcus ad intervallum majus medietate ipſius lineæ datæ; abſcinde utrimque æquales portiones A D, B C, & ex D & C centris operare ut dictum.

##### Corollarium.

{dropcap:P|lines=2|style=plain}Atet hinc, quomodo propoſita linea ſit dividenda in quatuor, in octo, & quotcunq; æquales partes numero pares, ſi nimirũ qualibet medietas ſubdividatur.
