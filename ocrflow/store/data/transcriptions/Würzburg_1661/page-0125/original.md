## Propoſitio V. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Si duo circuli ſeſe mutuò ſecent; non erit illorum idem centrum.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=plain}Ecent ſe mutuò duo circuli in B & D, & ſit eorum idem centrum, ſi fieri poteſt, C. Ergo recta C B erit ſemidiameter communis, eique erunt æquales aliæ C A, C E, ideoq; æquales inter ſe, *per 1. Axi. pri.* quod eſt abſurdum, & contra 9. *Axi. pri.*

## Propoſitio VI. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Si duo circuli ſeſe mutuò tangunt interiùs; eorum non erit idem centrum.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:T|lines=2|style=plain}Angant ſe duo circuli interiùs in B, & ſit eorum centrum commune D, ſi poteſt fieri. Erit D B communis ſemidiameter, & D C, D A æquales erunt eidem *D B*; & æquales inter ſe, *per 1. Axi. pri.* & contra *9. Axi.* ejuſdem.

[Diagram: figure 160; two circles touching internally at B, with lines from D to B, C and A]

## Propoſitio VII. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Si in diametro circuli quodpiam ſumatur punctum, quod circuli centrum non ſit, ab eoq; puncto in circulum quædam rectæ lineæ cadant; maxima quidem erit ea in qua centrum, minima verò reliqua; aliarum verò propinquior illi quæ per centrum ducitur, remotiore ſemper major erit: duæ autem ſolùm rectæ lineæ æquales ab eodem puncto in circulum cadunt, ad utraſq; partes minimæ.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:I|lines=2|style=plain}N diametro præſentis circuli, cujus centrum F, aſſumatur quodcunque punctum excentricum I, & ex eo educantur I C, I D, I E, utcunque. Erit I F A omnium maxima, *I B* minima, I C major quàm I D, & I D major quàm I E; & eidem I E v.g. una tantùm I G v.g. poterit eſſe æqualis ex altera parte minimæ. Nam *primò, per 20 pri.* F I, F C ſimul, majores ſunt quàm I C: ſed F I, F A ſunt æquales ipſis F I, F C; ergo I A major eſt quàm I C, &c. *Secundò*, I F, F C, ſunt æquales I F, F D; & angulus I F C major eſt angulo I F D; ergo, *per 24. pri.* I C major eſt quàm I D &c. *Tertiò*, I F, I E, ſunt majores, quàm F E, hoc eſt, quàm F D; demptâ ergo communi I F, remanet I E major quàm *I B* &c. *Quartò*, ſi angulo *B F E* fiat æqualis angulus *B F G*; erunt circa ipſos latera I F, F E, æqualia lateribus I F, F G; ergo *per 4. pri.* & baſis I E, baſi I G æqualis erit, & nulla alia, quia reliquæ omnes ſunt majores vel minores ex præmiſſis.

[Diagram: figure 161; circle with diameter A F I B and lines from I to C, D, E and G]

## Propoſitio VIII. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Si extra circulum ſumatur punctum quodpiam, ab eoque puncto ad circulum deducantur rectæ quædam lineæ, quarum una quidem per centrum protendatur, reliquæ verò ut libet; in cavam peripheriam cadentium rectarum linearum maxima quidem eſt illa, quæ per centrum ducitur; aliarum autem propinquior ei quæ per centrum tranſit, remotiore ſemper major eſt; in convexam verò peripheriam cadentium rectarum linearum minima quidem eſt illa, quæ inter punctum & diametrum interponitur; aliarum autem ea quæ propinquior eſt minimæ, remotiore ſemper minor eſt. Duæ autem tantùm rectæ lineæ æquales ab eo puncto in ipſum circulum cadunt ad utraſq; partes minimæ.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:E|lines=2|style=plain}X puncto A extra circulum poſito, ducantur quotcunq; rectæ, quarum A B F E per centrum tranſeat, A D I, A G H cadant utcunque tam ad convexam, quàm ad concavam peripheriam. Dico, A E eſſe maximam eductarum ad concavam; *A B* minimam eductarum ad convexam; A I majorem eſſe A H, & A G majorem A D; ipſique A D v.g. unam tantùm aliam poſſe eſſe æqualem. Ducantur enim E I, F H, F G, F D, F C. *Primò*, A F, F I, ſunt, *per 20. pri.* majores A I; ergo & A F E, quæ eſt æqualis ipſis A F, F I, &c. *Secundò*, A D, D F, ſunt, *per eandem 20. pri.* majores A F; ergo demptis æqualibus *F D, F B*, remanebit *A D* major quàm *A B* &c. *Tertiò*, A F, F I, ſunt æquales A F, F H, angulus autem A F I major angulo A F H; ergo *per 24. pri.* A I major eſt quàm A H &c. *Quartò*, Duæ *A B, D F*, ſunt *per 21. pri.* minores duabus A G, G F; & G F, D F, ſunt æquales; ergo A D minor quàm A G &c. *Quintò*, Angulus A F C ſit æqualis angulo A F D; ergo *per 4. pri.* A D erit æqualis A C, quia circa æquales angulos latera A F, F D, ſunt æqualia lateribus A F, F C.

## Propoſitio IX. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Si in circulo acceptum fuerit punctum aliquod, & ab eo ad circulum cadunt plures quàm duæ rectæ lineæ æquales; acceptum punctum eſt circuli centrum.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:T|lines=2|style=plain}Res rectæ, *A B, A C, A D*, ſint æquales; Dico, *A* eſſe centrum. Ducantur enim *B C, C D*, & ſecentur bifariam in E & F, & ejiciantur *A E, A F*. Erunt duo latera *A E, E B*, æqualia duobus lateribus *A E, E C*, & baſis *A B* æqualis baſi *A C*; ergo, *per 8. pri.* anguli *A E B, A E C*, ſunt æquales, & conſequenter recti; & *per Coroll. primæ hujus*, in *E A* producta erit centrum circuli: ſed propter eandem cauſam debet eſſe in *F A* producta; ergo centrum eſt *A*, alioquin non eſſet in *E A*, & *F A* producta.

[Diagram: figure 163; circle with point A inside joined to B, C and D, chords B C and C D bisected at E and F]
