### II.

In omni parallelogrammo ſpatio, unumquodlibet eorum, quæ circa diametrum illius ſunt, parallelogrammorum, cum duobus complementis, Gnomon vocetur. *Sic in parallelogrammo A B C D, diviſo in alia quatuor, modo explicato libro præcedente Propoſit. 43, duo complementa D H G F, E B I G, unà cum G I C F, conſtituunt gnomonem; ſic dictum, ob ſimilitudinem quam habet cum Norma rectangula, de qua lib. 1. cap. 4. a. 1. praxi 4. in Sexto Modo.*

[Diagram: parallelogram with corners A, E, B above and D, F, C below, divided by diameter into four parts, points H, G, I]

### Annotatio.

{dropcap:M|lines=2|style=plain}*Athematici cum Euclide in hoc libro, & aliis*

[Margin]
Rectangulum quid ſit apud Mathematicos.
[/Margin]

*ſequentibus, appellant parallelogrammum rectangulum ſimpliciter & ſine addito rectangulum. Et ne toties eædem litteræ repeti debeant, ſolent omne parallelogrammum, etiam non rectangulum, exprimere duabus duntaxat litteris quæ per diametrum in eo opponuntur. Sic præcedens parallelogrammum integrum appellant A C, vel B D: quatuor verò partialia illa appellant A G, E I, I F, I D; ſive H E, G B, G C, H F.*

## PROPOSITIONES.

## Propoſitio I. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Si fuerint duæ rectæ lineæ, ſeceturque ipſarum altera in quotcunque ſegmenta; rectangulum comprehenſum ſub illis duabus rectis lineis, æquale eſt eis, quæ ſub inſecta & quolibet ſegmentorum comprehenduntur, rectangulis.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=plain}Int duæ rectæ G B, & B C, quarum B C ſecetur quomodocunq; in quotlibet ſegmenta B D, D E, E C; at G B maneat inſecta. Dico, rectangulum ſub G B, & B C comprehenſum, æquale eſſe omnibus rectangulis ſimul ſumptis, quæ ſub linea G B indiviſa, & quolibet ſegmento comprehenduntur, nempe rectangulo ſub G B & B D, item ſub G B & D E, item ſub G B & E C comprehenſis. Si enim fiat ex G B inſecta, & B D ſecta in punctis D & E, rectangulum B F, & ex punctis D & E erigantur D H, E I, parallelæ lateri G B; diſtribuitur rectangulum B F in rectangula B H, D I, E F, quorum primum continetur ſub G B & B D, ſecundum ſub H D, id eſt, G B (nam H D eſt æqualis ipſi G B) & D E, tertium ſub I E, id eſt, G B, & E C. His autem tribus ſimul ſumptis æquale eſt rectangulum B F, quia *per 19. Ax.* totum eſt æquale ſuis partibus.

[Diagram: figure 135; rectangle with corners G, H, I, F above and B, D, E, C below, divided into three rectangles; line G B beneath]

*Pleræque Propoſitiones hujus libri poſſunt applicari numeris. Sit itaq; in hac Propoſitione G B 6 partium, & B C 10. Si 6 ducantur in 10, fiunt 60. Secetur B C in B D 5, D E 4, E C 1; ſi 6 ducantur in 5, in 4, in 1, fiunt 30, 24, 6, quæ ſimul faciunt 60 ut antea.*

## Propoſitio II. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Si recta linea ſecta ſit utcunq; rectangula quæ ſub tota & quolibet ſegmentorum comprehenduntur, æqualia ſunt ei, quod à tota fit, quadrato.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:R|lines=2|style=plain}Ecta linea A B dividatur utcunq; duas in partes A C, C B. Dico, duo rectangula comprehenſa ſub tota A B, & ſegmentis A C, C B, ſimul ſumpta, æqualia eſſe quadrato totius lineæ A B. Fiat enim, *per 46. pri.* ſuper A B quadratum A D. & ex C ducatur C F parallela rectæ A E, vel B D; dividetur quadratum A D in rectangulum A F contentum ſub E A, A C, hoc eſt, ſub A B, A C; & in rectangulum C D contentum ſub F C, C B, hoc eſt, ſub A B, C B; ideoq; quia, *per 19. Ax.* totum eſt æquale ſuis partibus, erit quadratum A D æquale dictis duobus rectangulis.

[Diagram: figure 136; square with corners E, F, D above and A, C, B below, divided by line F C]

*Si numerus 10 dividatur in 7 & 3, numerus quadratus 100, erit æqualis numeris 70 & 30.*

## Propoſitio III. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Si recta linea ſecta ſit utcunque; rectangulum ſub tota & uno ſegmentorum comprehenſum, æquale eſt illi rectangulo quod ſub ſegmentis comprehenditur, & illi quadrato quod à prædicto ſegmento deſcribitur.*
[/Subhead]

{dropcap:L|lines=2|style=plain}Inea recta A B diviſa ſit utcunq; in C. Dico, rectangulum comprehenſum ſub tota A B, & utrovis ſegmento, ut A C (ſive hoc ſegmentum maius ſit, ſive minus) æquale eſſe rectangulo ſub ſegmentis A C, C B comprehenſo, & quadrato prioris ſegmenti aſſumpti A C. Fiat enim rectangulum A F, cujus latus E A æquale ſit ſegmento A C; erit hoc rectangulum comprehenſum ſub tota A B, & uno ſegmentorum. Ducatur deinde ex C, recta C D parallela rectæ A E, vel B F; dividetur prædictum rectangulum A F, in quadratulum A D, deſcriptum ſuper ſegmento A C; & in rectangulum C F comprehenſum ſub D C, C B, hoc eſt, ſub A C, C B: eſt autem rectangulum A F æquale quadrato A D, & rectangulo C F, *per 19. Ax.* ergo &c:

[Diagram: figure 137; rectangle with corners E, D, F above and A, C, B below, divided by line D C]

*Numerus 10. dividatur in 7 & 3: productum ex 10 in 7, nempe 70. eſt æquale producto ex 7 in 3, id eſt 21, & ex 7 in 7, id eſt, 49.*

## Propoſitio IV. Theorema.

[Subhead]
*Si recta linea ſecta ſit utcunque; quadratum quod à tota deſcribitur, æquale eſt & illis, quæ à ſegmentis deſcribuntur, quadratis, & ei, quod bis ſub ſegmentis comprehenditur rectangulo.*
[/Subhead]
