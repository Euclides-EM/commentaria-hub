## PROP. XII.

[Diagram: pentagon F–G–H–K–L surrounding a circle, with points A–B–C–D–E, centre I, and intersecting radial lines]

Cum pentagonum quodlibet in tria, reſoluatur triangula BCD, BDE, BEF, ut hic apparet, unumquodque autem triangulum tres angulos habet duobus rectis æquales. Tria itaque triangula 6 rectos angulos habent: Quare quodlibet pentagonum, ſive ordinatum ſive inordinatum fuerit, 6 rectos angulos continebit. ALITER. Si circuli centro [quod I vocemus] in ſingulos angulos F, G, H, K, L, pentagoni rectæ ducantur, erit pentagonum in 5 triangula reſolutum: ergo in 10 angulos rectos. Sed 4 ad I centrum ablatis, relinquentur 6 recti ad circumferentiam: quare quilibet pentagoni angulus erit 1⅕ recti, ſive, 108 Grad.
