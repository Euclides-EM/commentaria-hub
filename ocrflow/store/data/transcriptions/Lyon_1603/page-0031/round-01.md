## PROP. III.

[Diagram: rectangle divided by a vertical line, labelled G, E, F above and C, D, B below]

Diviſus ſit quicunque numerus, ut 10 in partes quaſcunque duas, ut 8 & 2: dico numerum 80 ex 10 in 8 productum, æqualem eſſe 16, qui ex 8 in 2 producitur, una cum 64 quadrato prioris partis 8, ut perſpicuum eſt. Pari ratione erit numerus 20, natus ex 10 in 2 æqualis numero 16 ex 2 in 8 procreato, ſimul cum, 4 quadrato prædicti numeri 2: quod res ipſa indicat.

## PROP. IV.

[Diagram: square divided by vertical and horizontal lines and a diagonal from B to F, labelled E, G, F above, I, H, K across the middle, and B, D, C below]

Sumatur numerus 10, diviſus utcunque in 7 & 3. Manifeſtum eſt 100 quadratum totius, æquari 49 & 9 quadratis partium 7 & 3, una cum 21 numero, qui ex 7 in 3 ductu oritur, bis ſumpto. Nam 49, 9, 21, 21 compoſiti, efficiunt 100: tantum eſt quadratum 10 numeri.

*Ex hoc theoremate ſurdorum Additio defluxit, cuius hic eſt Canon.*
