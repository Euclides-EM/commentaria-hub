1. Pars. Radius A B ad contactum ductus a eſt perpendicularis ad tangentem I F. Quare ſi ductis rectis A F, A I, A N &c. polygonum reſoluatur in triangula, erit radius A B communis omnium altitudo; adeoque triangula ipſa liquet eſſe æqualia. Ergo triangulum baſim habens parem ſummæ laterum F I, I N, N T &c. altitudinem vero A B, æquabitur illis b omnibus, hoc eſt toti polygono circumſcripto.

## PROPOSITIO V.

{dropcap:C|lines=2|style=plain}Irculus eſt æqualis triangulo, cuius baſis eſt peripheria circuli, altitudo autem ſemidiameter.

[Margin]
Fig.2.
[/Margin]

Polygona ordinata circulo circumſcripta, & triangula baſes habentia ambitum polygoni, altitudinem vero radium circuli, ſemper ſunt a æqualia. Atqui polygona circulo in infinitum circumſcripta, in circulum b deſinunt: ſimiliterque triangula (vt mox oſtendam) quę pro baſi habent ambitum polygoni circumſcripti, pro altitudine vero radium A B, tandem deſinunt in triangulum pro baſi habens peripheriam, pro altitudine radium A B. Ergo c circulus, & triangulum pro baſi habens peripheriam, pro altitudine radium A B, æqualia ſunt.
