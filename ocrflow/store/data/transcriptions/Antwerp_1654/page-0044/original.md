[Curated heading level=3 type=numbered_item: 4.]

4. Si inæqualibus addas æqualia, tota erunt inæqualia.

[Curated heading level=2 type=axioms: Lib. I, Ax. 5]

[Curated heading level=3 type=numbered_item: 5.]

5. Si ab inæqualibus tollantur æqualia, quæ remanent, erunt inæqualia.

[Curated heading level=2 type=axioms: Lib. I, Ax. 6]

[Curated heading level=3 type=numbered_item: 6.]

6. Quæ eiuſdem dimidia ſunt, inter ſe ſunt æqualia : & quæ eiuſdem ſunt dupla, vel tripla, vel quadrupla, inter ſe æqualia ſunt.

[Curated heading level=2 type=axioms: Lib. I, Ax. 7]

[Curated heading level=3 type=numbered_item: 7.]

7. Quæ mutuo ſibi congruunt, æqualia ſunt.

Non rectè Clauius hoc Axioma conuertit.

Falſum eſt enim, ea quæ vniuerſim inter ſe æqualia ſunt, ſibi mutuo congruere: Dißimiles enim magnitudines poſſunt eſſe æquales, neque tamen congruent. Quòd ſi ſimiles & æquales fuerint, valebit conuerſa. Statuamus igitur axioma.

[Curated heading level=2 type=axioms: Lib. I, Ax. 8]

[Curated heading level=3 type=numbered_item: 8.]

8. Si rectæ lineæ æquales fuerint, ſibi mutuo congruent : & anguli ſi æquales fuerint, ſibi mutuo congruent.

[Curated heading level=2 type=axioms: Lib. I, Ax. 9]

[Curated heading level=3 type=numbered_item: 9.]
