## Scholium.

[Margin]
Fig 65.
[/Margin]

{dropcap:E|lines=2|style=plain}X hac, & ex ſcholio prop.35. diſcimus, aream trianguli (AFB) cuiuſcunque produci ex dimidia altitudine FI ducta in baſim (AB) vel ex dimidia baſi in altitudinem. Quare noto vno latere trianguli, & altitudine, ſiue perpendiculari, quæ in latus notum ex angulo oppoſito ducitur, habetur trianguli dimenſio: vt ſi baſis AB ſit pedum 100. altitudo FI, 85; multiplica baſis dimidium 50. per 85. proueniunt 4250 pedes quadrati pro area trianguli AFB. Porro altitudo ſiue perpendicularis illa, quando area trianguli peragrari poteſt, mechanicè innoteſcit, vti latera. Si area peragrari nequeat, inuenietur geometricè altitudo per 12. & 13. lib.2. vt in ſcholio ibidem docebimus.

In triangulo rectangulo altitudo eſt eadem cum alterutro latere circa angulum rectum. Huius ergo ſemißis ducta in latus alterum recto adiacens dabit trianguli aream.
