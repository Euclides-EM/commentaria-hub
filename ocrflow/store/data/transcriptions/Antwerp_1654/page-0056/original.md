## PROPOSITIO XIII.

[Margin]
Fig. 34.
[/Margin]

{dropcap:R|lines=2|style=plain}Ecta (B A) ſuper rectam (C F) conſiſtens aut duos rectos angulos facit, aut duobus rectis æquales.

Nam ſi B A inſiſtat perpendiculariter, erunt per def. 14. anguli B A C, B A F, vtrimque recti. Si vero B A inſiſtat obliquè, excitetur a perpendicularis A L. Quia tum anguli inæquales C A B, F A B eundem locum occupant, quem duo recti C A L, L A F, ac proinde ijs congruunt, erunt b his illi æquales. Quod erat demonſtrandum.
