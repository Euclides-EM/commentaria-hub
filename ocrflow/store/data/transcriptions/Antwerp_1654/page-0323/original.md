Quod autem triangula ſub ambitu polygoni, & radio, deſinant in triangulum ſub peripheriâ & radio, ſic oſtendo. Triangula ſub ambitu circumſcripti polygoni & radio A B ſunt ad triangulum ſub peripheria & radio A B, vt i baſis ad baſim, nempè vt ambitus polygoni ad peripheriam; cum altitudinem habeant communem.
