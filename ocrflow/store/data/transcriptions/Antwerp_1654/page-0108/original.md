## PROPOSITIO IV.

[Curated heading level=2 type=figures: Fig. 4 & 5]

[Margin]
Fig. 4. & 5.
[/Margin]

{dropcap:S|lines=2|style=plain}I in circulo duæ rectæ ( B C, F L) non ambæ per centrum ductæ , ſe ſecent, non ſecabunt ſe mutuo bifariam.

[Margin]
Fig. 5.
[/Margin]

Nam ſi vna L F tranſeat per centrum, patet hanc non biſecari ab altera B C, quæ ex hyp. per centrum A non tranſit.

[Margin]
Fig. 4.
[/Margin]

Si neutra per centrum tranſit, ex A centro duc A O. Si iam ambæ B C, F L forent biſectæ in O, anguli quoque A O C, A O L eſſent a recti, ac proinde æquales, totum & pars. Quod fieri non poteſt.

[Margin]
a Per præc.
[/Margin]

[Curated heading level=2 type=propositions: Lib. III, PROPOSITIO V. & VI.]

## PROPOSITIO V. & VI.

[Curated heading level=2 type=figures: Fig. 6 & 7]

[Margin]
Fig. 6. & 7.
[/Margin]

{dropcap:C|lines=2|style=plain}Irculi ſe mutuo ſecantes , aut interius tangentes non habent idem centrum.

Alias enim ductis ex communi centro A rectis A B, A C F, eſſent A C, A F, (pars & totum) æquales, quia ambæ ſunt æquales eidem A B.
