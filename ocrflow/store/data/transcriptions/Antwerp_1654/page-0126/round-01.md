## PROPOSITIO XVII.

{dropcap:A|lines=2|style=plain}Puncto dato (B) rectam ducere, quæ datum circulum (O Q) tangat.

[Margin]
Fig. 26.
[/Margin]

Ex A dati circuli centro ducatur ad datum punctum B recta A B, ſecans peripheriam in O. Centro A deſcribe per B alium circulum, B S, & ex O duc O C perpendicularem ad A B, quæ occurrat circulo B S in C. Duc C A, occurrentem circulo O Q in I. Ex B ad I ducta recta tanget circulum O Q.
