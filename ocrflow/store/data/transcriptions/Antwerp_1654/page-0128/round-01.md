## PROPOSITIO XX.

[Margin]
Fig. 30. 31. 32.
[/Margin]

{dropcap:A|lines=2|style=plain}Ngulus ad centrum (B A C) duplus eſt anguli (B F C) ad peripheriam, cum idem arcus (B C) eſt baſis angulorum.

[Margin]
Fig. 30.
[/Margin]

Triplex eſt caſus. In primo latera B A, B F coincidunt. Tum vero quia A F, A C ex centro ductæ æquantur, erunt in triangulo Z anguli F, & C a æquales. Sed B A C b æqualis eſt duobus F & C. Ergo B A C duplus eſt vnius F.

[Margin]
a Per 5.l.1.
b Per 32.l.1.
[/Margin]
