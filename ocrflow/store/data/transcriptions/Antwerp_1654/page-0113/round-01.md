## PROPOSITIO XIII.

{dropcap:C|lines=2|style=plain}Irculi & ſeſe mutuo, & lineam rectam punctualiter tangunt.

[Margin]
Fig. 15.
[/Margin]

Tangant enim ſe intra (ſi fieri poteſt) duo circuli in parte circumferentiæ C L. Per centra A, & B ducta recta b tranſibit per contactum puta in C. Ducantur inſuper A L, B L. Quoniam igitur c B L, B C æquantur (ſunt enim ex centro B ad peripheriam O L C,) addita communi A B, erunt A B, B L æquales A C. Sed A C eſt par A L, (ſunt enim ex centro A ad peripheriam Q L C.) Ergo etiam A B, B L ſunt æquales A L, contra 20.l.1.

[Margin]
b Per 11.l.3.
[/Margin]

[Margin]
c Quia ex centro.
[/Margin]

[Margin]
Fig 16.
[/Margin]

Tangant ſe deinde exterius duo circuli, ſi fieri poteſt, in arcu O L. Recta A P centra A & P iungens d tranſibit per contactum, puta in O. Ducantur inſuper A L, P L. Erunt igitur duo trianguli latera A L, P L æqualia ipſis, A O, P O, ſeu toti A P. Contra 20.l.1.

[Margin]
d Per 12.l.3.
[/Margin]

[Margin]
Fig.16.
[/Margin]

Denique recta B F & circulus ſe tangãt, ſi fieri poteſt, in parte aliqua C E. Ducantur ad centrum rectæ C A, E A. Erunt igitur C A, E A æquales, ac proinde triangulum C A E eſt iſoſceles. Quare e anguli ACE, AEC acuti. Ergo perpendicularis ad B F, ducta ex A centro, cadet inter f C & E puta in D.

[Margin]
e Per coroll. 11.p.32.l.1.
[/Margin]

[Margin]
f Per coroll. 3.p.32.l.1.
[/Margin]
