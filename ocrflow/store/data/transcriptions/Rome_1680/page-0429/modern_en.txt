IV.

Straight lines are said to be incommensurable in power,
when no space is found that measures their squares.

When straight lines are commensurable in length, they are also com-
mensurable in power, as will be demonstrated in its place; and when straight li-
nes are not commensurable in length, it can be that they are commensura-
ble in power; and it can be that they are not; that is, it can be that some space
measures their squares, and it can be that no space is ever found that mea-
sures them: if some space measures their squares, those straight lines are said to be com-
mensurable in power, as was said in the preceding definition: but if in
no way is a space found that measures the said squares, those straight lines are
said to be incommensurable in power: and in this case they will be incom-
mensurable in power, and they will also be incommensurable in length, in
such a way that, just as, when two straight lines are commensurable in leng-
th, they are also commensurable in power, so, when they are incommensu-
rable in power, they are also incommensurable in length; all of which will
be demonstrated in its place.
