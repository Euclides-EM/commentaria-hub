## Preliminary Intellectual Reading

1. Intellectual claims are often procedural: title pages advertise what has been done to the book or what the book enables readers to do.
2. The most important value families to inspect closely are utility/application, demonstration/method, correction/revision, augmentation/enrichment, translation/vernacularization, and ancient/restorative authority.
3. Composite books are intellectually important, not just bibliographic clutter: additions, bound-with works, tables, notes, comments, and demonstrations define what mathematical knowledge is made of.
4. Absence is meaningful: some title pages lean on canonical identity with few explicit pedagogical promises.
5. Next step: integrate the deep social topology and deep intellectual topology into a more robust argument map.
