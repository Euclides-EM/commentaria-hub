## Preliminary Reading

1. The intellectual vocabulary is not separate from the social vocabulary. Audience/community markers often travel with utility, demonstration/method, and enrichment language.
2. Geometry/Theory is especially tied to identity, Euclid/ancient authority, demonstration/method, and enrichment; this may be the bridge back to the old Euclid question.
3. Instruments/Measurement and Applied Mechanics/Military tend to connect social address to use, practice, and professional applicability.
4. The next step should inspect examples in `intellectual_value_examples.csv` and refine the taxonomy, especially because multilingual OCR variation makes the heuristic noisy.
