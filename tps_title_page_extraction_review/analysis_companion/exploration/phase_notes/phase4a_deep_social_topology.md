## Preliminary Social Reading

1. The social layer is not one thing: audience, institution, editor credential, and dedication/patronage point to different relationships.
2. Patronage is common and politically important, but it should not be confused with intended audience.
3. Explicit audiences often name practical publics: students, general readers/lovers, military users, surveyors/engineers, artisans, architects/builders, merchants, and navigators.
4. Institutional markers are dominated by universities/academies/colleges and religious/Jesuit contexts, which likely matter for pedagogy and authority.
5. Editor credentials matter socially: title pages make expertise legible through professor/lecturer, royal official, religious identity, and practitioner roles.
6. Next, the intellectual analysis should be deepened separately, then we can return to crossings with more confidence.
